Indonesia and China Seal US$71.8 Billion in Strategic Partnerships at APEC Shenzhen, Led by Danantara Smart City Push

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. Our top story tonight — a blockbuster deal between Indonesia and China at the APEC Summit in Shenzhen. President Prabowo Subianto witnessed the signing of US$71.8 billion in strategic cooperation agreements on 18 November, spanning smart cities, manufacturing, infrastructure funding, and energy technology. The Danantara sovereign wealth fund plays a central role in three of the four partnerships.

Analyst: The headline figure is extraordinary — US$71.8 billion exceeds Indonesia's entire annual realised FDI, which was approximately US$47.8 billion in 2025. The largest component is a US$50 billion memorandum of understanding for the development of 10 future smart cities, signed between a Danantara affiliate, PT Kota Masa Depan Indonesia, and a Shaanxi-led Chinese consortium. Investment Minister and Danantara CEO Rosan Roeslani called it "one of the historical projects that we will commence next year." The second deal is US$10 billion with Hisense for joint R&D laboratories, talent development, and home appliance manufacturing. A third US$10 billion commitment was signed between Danantara and China's State Development and Investment Corporation for infrastructure funding. The fourth, worth US$1.8 billion, is between Pertamina and Beijing Petrochemical Engineering for coal-to-ethanol technology transfer.

Lead presenter: How much of this is likely to materialise?

Analyst: That is the critical question. The US$50 billion smart city commitment — representing 70 per cent of the total — is a memorandum of understanding, not a binding contract. Building 10 entirely new smart cities from scratch is unprecedented even by Chinese infrastructure standards. The timeline, consortium membership, and financing structure are all unspecified. But the broader strategic context is important: these China deals come just days after the Indonesia-EU CEPA signing, suggesting Prabowo is aggressively hedging Indonesia's economic partnerships between the United States, Europe, and China. The Hisense deal signals China's interest in using Indonesia as an export platform for consumer electronics — potentially competing with Japanese and Korean brands across ASEAN. And the coal-to-ethanol technology transfer with Pertamina raises questions about economic viability at a time when global energy transitions favour renewable pathways. Whether these MoUs convert into actual capital deployment depends on regulatory certainty, land availability, and Indonesia's ability to provide power and water infrastructure at the scale required.

Bank Indonesia Surprises with Rate Hike to 6% as Rupiah Defence Overrides Growth Concerns

印尼央行意外加息至6%，捍卫卢比优先于增长考量

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Staying with Indonesia — Bank Indonesia has surprised markets with a 25-basis-point rate hike to 6.00 per cent on 18 November, bringing cumulative tightening in 2026 to 125 basis points. Most economists had expected a hold.

Analyst: Governor Destry Damayanti described it as a "pre-emptive move, intended to anchor future inflation" amid expectations the US Federal Reserve may hike again in December. The central bank cited global pressures including geopolitical flashpoints, AI stock valuation risks, and the MSCI reclassification of Indonesia from emerging market to frontier status — which has driven sustained capital outflows from Indonesian equities. The deposit facility rate was raised to 5.25 per cent and the lending facility rate to 6.75 per cent. Senior Deputy Governor Aida Budiman said the policy combined rate increases with unconventional instruments, including hedging-cost incentives to attract foreign capital. When asked whether the hike would disrupt growth, Budiman pointed to commercial credit growing 13.21 per cent in October — well above the 8 to 12 per cent projection range — as evidence of economic resilience.

Lead presenter: How does this compare with the rest of ASEAN?

Analyst: Indonesia and the Philippines now have the most aggressive tightening stories in the region, both with 125 basis points of cumulative hikes in 2026. But the economic contexts are very different. Indonesia's Q3 GDP grew 5.47 per cent — robust by any measure — while the Philippines managed only 2.1 per cent. BI's rate hike is defensive, aimed at currency preservation rather than demand suppression. The 25-basis-point increment — smaller than the 50-basis-point moves in May and June — suggests BI is trying to signal resolve without shocking the economy. But the forward guidance is hawkish: Budiman expects one more Fed hike before year-end, which creates conditions for BI to follow if the rupiah weakens further, potentially pushing the rate to 6.25 per cent by December. The irony is that this rate hike was announced on the same day as the US$71.8 billion in China investment deals — higher borrowing costs could slow the domestic component of precisely those partnerships.

主播：继续关注印尼——印尼央行11月18日意外加息25个基点至6.00%，出乎大多数预期按兵不动的经济学家意料。2026年累计紧缩幅度达到125个基点。

分析师：行长德斯特里·达玛延蒂将此描述为"先发制人之举，旨在锚定未来通胀"，背景是市场预期美联储可能在12月再次加息。央行援引地缘政治冲突、AI股票估值风险以及MSCI将印尼从新兴市场重新分类为前沿市场——这导致了印尼股票市场持续的资本外流。存款便利利率上调至5.25%，贷款便利利率上调至6.75%。副行长艾达·布迪曼表示，该政策将加息与非常规工具相结合，包括降低对冲成本的激励措施以吸引外资。当被问及加息是否会扰乱增长时，布迪曼指出10月商业信贷增长13.21%——远高于8%至12%的预测区间——作为经济韧性的证据。

分析师：印尼和菲律宾现在是该地区最激进的紧缩案例，2026年都累计加息125个基点。但经济背景截然不同。印尼第三季度GDP增长5.47%——以任何标准衡量都很强劲——而菲律宾仅2.1%。央行的加息是防御性的，旨在维护货币而非抑制需求。25个基点的幅度——小于5月和6月的50个基点——表明央行试图在不冲击经济的情况下展示决心。但前瞻指引是鹰派的：布迪曼预计美联储年底前还将加息一次，这为央行在卢比进一步走弱时跟进创造了条件，可能在12月前将利率推至6.25%。讽刺的是，这次加息与718亿美元中国投资协议在同一天宣布——更高的借贷成本可能恰恰减缓这些合作伙伴关系中的国内组成部分。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Malaysia now — where Deputy Prime Minister Zahid has told a 58-member Chinese business delegation that Malaysia "does not really encourage the opening of new" data centres. Instead, Chinese investors should partner with or acquire existing operators. It is a significant shift for ASEAN's most enthusiastic data centre destination.

Analyst: The numbers explain why. Malaysia has 117 data centre projects already approved, with 24 more pending. HLIB estimates the pipeline at 13 gigawatts of power capacity — roughly equivalent to the entire electricity generation capacity of a mid-sized ASEAN economy. The practical constraint is not investor demand but infrastructure delivery: Tenaga Nasional must add generation and transmission capacity to match the construction timeline, and water supply in Johor, where many facilities are concentrated, has periodically fallen short even without data centre demand. Zahid's instruction was pointed: "It is better if they work with existing companies, do it together or take over." The government is prioritising execution of approved projects over new approvals.

Lead presenter: How do the US chip export controls factor in?

Analyst: They add a second layer of complexity. The Bureau of Industry and Security's interim rules impose a 750-chip ceiling on advanced AI processor shipments to Malaysia, requiring licences above that threshold. This creates a two-tier market. US hyperscalers — AWS, Microsoft, Google — receive exempt status and can continue importing H100 and Blackwell chips without licensing friction. But independent operators and Chinese-linked ventures face heightened scrutiny. Kenanga Research assessed that the curbs will reshape rather than derail the boom, shifting the pipeline toward US hyperscalers. The strategic implication is that Malaysia's data centre sector is being structurally reorganised around American cloud providers, with Chinese operators relegated to lower-tier facilities. This mirrors what is happening in Thailand, but Malaysia's explicit policy discouragement of new builds adds a supply-side constraint that other countries have not imposed. The question now is whether this policy slowdown will affect the construction boom that drove 16.7 per cent non-residential building growth in Q3.

中文主播稿

主播：转向马来西亚——副总理扎希对58名中国商业代表团表示，马来西亚"并不鼓励开设新的"数据中心。中国投资者应与现有运营商合作或进行收购。对于东盟最热情的数据中心目的地来说，这是一个重大转变。

分析师：数字解释了原因。马来西亚已有117个数据中心项目获批，另有24个待审。HLIB估计管线电力容量为13吉瓦——大约相当于一个中等规模东盟经济体的全部发电能力。实际约束不是投资者需求而是基础设施交付：国家电力公司马来西亚国能必须增加发电和输电能力以匹配建设时间表，而柔佛——许多设施集中的地方——的供水即使在没有数据中心需求的情况下也曾出现不足。扎希的指示很明确："最好与现有公司合作，共同推进或收购。"政府正在优先执行已批准项目而非新增审批。

主播：美国芯片出口管制如何影响？

分析师：它增加了第二层复杂性。美国工业与安全局的临时规则对先进AI处理器出口实施750芯片上限，超出部分需申请许可证。这创造了双层市场。美国超大规模运营商——AWS、微软、谷歌——享受豁免地位，可以继续无障碍进口H100和Blackwell芯片。但独立运营商和中资关联企业面临更严格审查。肯纳格研究评估认为，这些限制将重塑而非摧毁数据中心热潮，使管线转向美国超大规模运营商。战略含义是马来西亚数据中心行业正围绕美国云提供商进行结构性重组，中国运营商被降至较低级别设施。这与泰国的情况类似，但马来西亚对新建项目的明确政策限制增加了其他国家尚未施加的供给侧约束。现在的问题是这一政策放缓是否会影响推动第三季度非住宅建筑16.7%增长的建设热潮。

Thailand Q3 GDP Accelerates to 2.9% on AI Export Surge but Ranks Fifth Among Six ASEAN Economies

THAILAND · ECONOMY / 泰国 · 经济

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Thailand's economy grew 2.9 per cent in the third quarter, accelerating from 2.4 per cent in Q2 but slightly missing the 3.0 per cent consensus. The result places Thailand fifth among six major ASEAN economies — ahead of only the Philippines. What is behind this underperformance?

Analyst: The paradox is that Thailand has one of the region's strongest export performances. Merchandise exports grew 18.3 per cent, and exports excluding gold surged 25.2 per cent — a 21-quarter high — concentrated in computers, electronics, and optical transceivers for AI infrastructure. Private investment rose 16.2 per cent, up from 13.5 per cent in Q2, with foreign investment commitments reportedly exceeding the previous year by 30 per cent. So the external sector is booming. But the domestic economy is stagnant. Private consumption grew just 2.5 per cent, constrained by high household debt and tight SME lending conditions, with commercial bank lending to small businesses actually contracting.

Lead presenter: So Thailand has a two-track economy?

Analyst: Precisely — and that two-track gap is wider in Thailand than anywhere else in ASEAN. Vietnam, which is similarly export-dependent, achieves broad-based growth because its manufacturing sector is more labour-intensive and employs a larger share of the workforce. Thailand's high-tech exports — servers, optical networking, semiconductors — generate high value but limited direct employment. The Bank of Thailand maintains its policy rate at 1.00 per cent, the lowest in ASEAN, reflecting this domestic weakness: even as exports boom, monetary policy cannot tighten without crushing households and SMEs. The NESDC has effectively lowered its full-year forecast to approximately 2.5 per cent, implicitly acknowledging that the AI export surge alone cannot offset the structural drag from household debt, weak agricultural incomes, and credit-starved small businesses. Flooding in several provinces is expected to weigh further on Q4 agricultural output. The 2027 outlook at 2.0 to 3.0 per cent suggests no acceleration without a meaningful tourism and consumption recovery.

中文主播稿

主播：泰国第三季度经济增长2.9%，较第二季度的2.4%加速，但略低于3.0%的共识预期。这一结果使泰国在六个主要东盟经济体中排名第五——仅高于菲律宾。表现不佳的原因是什么？

分析师：悖论在于泰国拥有该地区最强劲的出口表现之一。商品出口增长18.3%，剔除黄金的出口飙升25.2%——创21个季度新高——集中在计算机、电子产品和AI基础设施光学收发器。私人投资增长16.2%，高于第二季度的13.5%，外国投资承诺据报超过前一年30%。所以外部部门正在繁荣。但国内经济停滞不前。私人消费仅增长2.5%，受高家庭债务和中小企业贷款条件收紧制约，商业银行对小企业贷款实际上在收缩。

主播：所以泰国是双轨经济？

分析师：完全正确——而且这种双轨差距在泰国比东盟任何其他地方都大。越南虽然同样依赖出口，但由于其制造业更加劳动密集型并雇用了更大比例的劳动力，因此实现了广泛增长。泰国的高科技出口——服务器、光网络、半导体——产生高价值但直接就业有限。泰国央行维持政策利率在东盟最低的1.00%，反映了这种国内疲弱：即使出口繁荣，货币政策也无法收紧而不打击家庭和中小企业。NESDC实际上已将全年预测下调至约2.5%，隐含地承认仅靠AI出口激增无法抵消家庭债务、农业收入疲弱和信贷匮乏的中小企业带来的结构性拖累。多省洪灾预计将进一步拖累第四季度农业产出。2027年2.0%至3.0%的展望表明，如果旅游业和消费没有实质性复苏，预计不会加速。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Vietnam's National Assembly has approved the 2027 socio-economic development plan with an extraordinary near-unanimous vote — 467 of 469 deputies. The plan targets at least 10 per cent GDP growth and per capita GDP of approximately US$6,100. This would make Vietnam the fastest-growing economy in Asia for two consecutive years.

Analyst: The sectoral targets are ambitious across the board: manufacturing and processing at 12.3 per cent, services at approximately 9.8 per cent, and agriculture at 3.8 per cent. The industrial production index is targeted at 11.5 to 12.5 per cent growth. The plan also calls for total retail sales growth of 14 per cent, e-commerce growth of 15 to 17 per cent, merchandise export growth of 15 per cent, and the digital economy reaching 23.5 per cent of GDP. Separately, the National Assembly approved a 2027 budget deficit target of 4.5 per cent of GDP, with average inflation also at 4.5 per cent. The near-unanimous vote reflects the political consensus behind General Secretary Tô Lâm's growth agenda.

Lead presenter: Is 10 per cent actually achievable?

Analyst: The arithmetic is daunting. Vietnam's growth through the first three quarters of 2026 was approximately 7.4 per cent — strong by global standards but well short of the 10 per cent target for this year. Achieving 10 per cent for 2026 would require roughly 12.5 per cent in Q4 — extremely ambitious. If 2026 falls short of 10 per cent, the base for 2027 becomes even more demanding. The 4.5 per cent budget deficit signals that the government intends fiscal expansion as a growth lever, which is feasible given public debt declining from 41.3 per cent of GDP in 2020 to 30.5 per cent in 2025 — that is genuine fiscal headroom. And the first investment-grade rating from R&I could lower borrowing costs. But there are risks: the World Bank found that AI-related goods contributed over 70 per cent of Vietnam's export growth. That concentration is a vulnerability — a correction in AI demand would hit disproportionately. The requirement for 30 provinces to achieve double-digit growth creates pressure on provincial governments to approve investments that may not be environmentally or socially sustainable. And inflation at 4.57 per cent in the first ten months of 2026 already exceeds the 4.5 per cent target.

中文主播稿

主播：越南国会以近乎全票——469名代表中467票赞成——通过了2027年社会经济发展计划。计划设定GDP增长目标为至少10%，人均GDP约为6,100美元。这将使越南连续两年成为亚洲增长最快的经济体。

分析师：各行业目标全面雄心勃勃：制造和加工12.3%，服务业约9.8%，农业3.8%。工业生产指数目标增长11.5%至12.5%。计划还要求社会零售商品销售总额增长14%，电子商务增长15%至17%，商品出口增长15%，数字经济占GDP的23.5%。国会另外批准了2027年预算赤字目标为GDP的4.5%，平均通胀也为4.5%。近乎全票通过反映了总书记苏林增长议程背后的政治共识。

主播：10%的目标实际上能实现吗？

分析师：算术令人生畏。越南2026年前三季度增长约为7.4%——以全球标准衡量强劲，但远低于今年10%的目标。要实现2026年全年10%需要约12.5%的第四季度增长——极为雄心勃勃。如果2026年未达到10%，2027年的基数将更加苛刻。4.5%的预算赤字表明政府打算将财政扩张作为增长杠杆，鉴于公共债务从2020年占GDP的41.3%降至2025年的30.5%，这是可行的——那是真正的财政空间。R&I首次投资级评级也可能降低借贷成本。但存在风险：世界银行发现AI相关商品贡献了越南出口增长的70%以上。这种集中度是一个脆弱性——AI需求的回调将带来不成比例的打击。要求30个省实现两位数增长给省级政府施加了批准可能在环境或社会方面不可持续的投资的压力。而且2026年前十个月4.57%的通胀已经超过了4.5%的目标。

Philippines Inflation Hits 7.8% as Analysts Warn BSP Must Keep Hiking Despite 2.1% GDP Growth

English presenter script

Lead presenter: And finally to the Philippines, where the economic picture is the most troubling in ASEAN. Headline inflation has hit 7.8 per cent — a three-year high — while core inflation has accelerated to 5.4 per cent. GDP growth in Q3 was just 2.1 per cent. That is the stagflation combination that central bankers dread.

Analyst: BPI chief economist Emilio Neri has laid out the dilemma with unusual clarity. He calculates the real policy rate — the benchmark rate minus inflation — at approximately negative 2.3 per cent. This means that despite 125 basis points of cumulative hikes bringing the BSP's rate to 5.50 per cent, monetary policy remains effectively accommodative because inflation has outrun the rate increases. As Neri put it: "Rate hikes don't grow rice, but pausing could harvest inflation." The core inflation acceleration to 5.4 per cent is more alarming than the headline because it shows that price pressures have become embedded beyond volatile food and energy items. Barclays has forecast that inflation could reach 8.0 per cent in November and sees the possibility of another jumbo 50-basis-point hike at the BSP's 17 December meeting.

Lead presenter: How does the Philippines compare with its ASEAN neighbours?

Analyst: The contrast is now extreme and growing. Malaysia has 1.9 per cent inflation with 5.5 per cent growth. The Philippines has 7.8 per cent inflation with 2.1 per cent growth — that is four times the inflation at less than half the growth. Vietnam is growing above 7 per cent, Indonesia at 5.47 per cent. Only the Philippines is in the stagflationary quadrant. The Q3 GDP breakdown shows broad-based weakness: household consumption grew just 2.4 per cent — the weakest since 2010 outside the pandemic — investment contracted 0.2 per cent, and agriculture fell 4.8 per cent. Agriculture's contraction directly feeds food prices and rural incomes, creating a feedback loop where supply-side weakness drives demand-side inflation. The BSP's December meeting will be a defining moment: a 50-basis-point hike to 6.00 per cent would signal willingness to accept recession risk to break the inflation cycle, while a 25-basis-point increment may be seen as insufficient. ING identifies the Philippines as having the highest inflation in Asia. Citigroup has already applied the stagflation label.

中文主播稿

主播：最后转向菲律宾——东盟中经济图景最令人担忧的国家。整体通胀达到7.8%的三年高位，核心通胀加速至5.4%。第三季度GDP仅增长2.1%。这就是央行最恐惧的滞胀组合。

分析师：BPI首席经济学家埃米利奥·内里以异常清晰的方式阐述了这一困境。他计算的实际政策利率——基准利率减去通胀——约为负2.3%。这意味着尽管累计加息125个基点将BSP利率提至5.50%，货币政策实际上仍然是宽松的，因为通胀已经超越了加息幅度。正如内里所说："加息不能种出大米，但暂停可能收获通胀。"核心通胀加速至5.4%比整体数字更令人担忧，因为它表明价格压力已经超越波动性食品和能源项目而变得根深蒂固。巴克莱预测11月通胀可能达到8.0%，并认为BSP可能在12月17日会议上再次超大幅加息50个基点。

主播：菲律宾与东盟邻国相比如何？

分析师：对比现在极为悬殊且不断扩大。马来西亚通胀1.9%伴随5.5%的增长。菲律宾通胀7.8%伴随2.1%的增长——四倍的通胀率、不到一半的增长。越南增长超过7%，印尼5.47%。只有菲律宾处于滞胀象限。第三季度GDP构成显示全面疲弱：家庭消费仅增长2.4%——疫情外2010年以来最弱——投资收缩0.2%，农业下降4.8%。农业收缩直接影响食品价格和农村收入，形成供给侧疲弱驱动需求侧通胀的反馈循环。BSP的12月会议将是决定性时刻：50个基点加息至6.00%将表明愿意接受衰退风险以打破通胀周期，而25个基点的增加可能被视为不够充分。ING认定菲律宾拥有亚洲最高通胀。花旗集团已经贴上了滞胀标签。
