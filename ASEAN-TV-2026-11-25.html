Singapore Raises 2026 GDP Forecast to About 6% After Q3 Growth Hits 6.1% on AI and Export Boom

新加坡在第三季度GDP增长6.1%后将2026年全年预测上调至约6%，AI和出口热潮推动增长

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. Our top story tonight — Singapore's Ministry of Trade and Industry has raised the country's 2026 GDP growth forecast to around 6 per cent, up from the previous 4.5 to 5.5 per cent range. This follows a third-quarter expansion of 6.1 per cent year-on-year, beating the advance estimate of 5.4 per cent and the market consensus of 5.6 per cent. This marks the third upward revision to the growth forecast this year — from 1 to 3 per cent in November 2025, to 2 to 4 per cent in February, to 4.5 to 5.5 per cent in August, and now to around 6 per cent.

Analyst: This is extraordinary for a mature, high-income economy. Six per cent is the kind of growth rate that developing economies aspire to, and it confirms Singapore as perhaps the single largest beneficiary of the global AI infrastructure buildout among developed economies. The composition is revealing: the dominant drivers are wholesale trade in AI-related machinery, electronics manufacturing, and precision engineering — all directly connected to the global data centre and AI chip supply chain. Singapore produces approximately 10 per cent of the world's chips and about 20 per cent of global semiconductor manufacturing equipment, which explains why the AI boom translates so directly into GDP. The serial forecast upgrades — from 1 to 3 per cent to 6 per cent in a single year — illustrate how dramatically the AI demand surge exceeded initial expectations. Retail trade and food and beverage services actually contracted in Q3, which the MTI attributed to Singaporeans shifting spending abroad as outbound travel picks up — an interesting leakage that partially offsets export-driven gains.

Lead presenter: What is the outlook for 2027?

Analyst: The MTI is projecting a significant moderation — 2.5 to 4.5 per cent for 2027. Permanent Secretary Dr Beh Swan Gin explicitly flagged concerns about the sustainability of the global AI cycle as a potential trigger for sharper corrections in financial markets. If AI-related capital expenditure by hyperscale operators slows — whether due to overbuilding, shifting from training to inference workloads, or a reassessment of AI returns — Singapore's growth could decelerate sharply. At 6 per cent, Singapore is currently growing faster than Malaysia at 5.5 per cent, Indonesia at approximately 5 per cent, and dramatically faster than Thailand at 2.5 per cent and the Philippines at 2.1 per cent. For a US$500 billion economy, this is a remarkable performance, but one that is heavily concentrated in a single demand driver.

Vietnam's National Assembly Sets Unprecedented 10% GDP Growth Target for 2027 with US$228 Billion Investment Plan

越南国会为2027年设定史无前例的10%以上GDP增长目标，配套2,280亿美元投资计划

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Vietnam now — where the National Assembly has set what may be the most ambitious macroeconomic target in ASEAN's recent history. On 7 November, 467 of 469 deputies voted to adopt a resolution targeting GDP growth of at least 10 per cent for 2027, with a GDP per capita target of approximately US$6,100 and a CPI inflation target of about 4.5 per cent. The resolution describes 2027 as "a year of acceleration and breakthroughs."

Analyst: To put this in perspective, the IMF projects Vietnam growing at 7.5 per cent in 2027 — so the National Assembly is targeting growth approximately 2.5 percentage points above what the international community considers achievable. Vietnam has not achieved 10 per cent growth since the early 1990s during the doi moi reform boom, when the economy was a fraction of its current size. The investment plan reveals how Hanoi intends to reach the target: total social investment of US$228 to 243 billion, representing 36 to 38 per cent of GDP. But the state budget covers only US$67 billion — the remaining US$160 to 175 billion must come from private domestic enterprises, foreign direct investment, and the stock market. The FDI disbursement target is US$30 to 32 billion, a significant acceleration from the US$24 billion disbursed in the first nine months of 2026.

Lead presenter: Can they finance this?

Analyst: This is the critical question. National savings would need to jump from 34.9 per cent of GDP to approximately 39.4 per cent in a single year to finance the investment without inflationary pressure, according to IMF figures. If savings do not keep pace, the gap must be filled by foreign capital inflows or domestic credit expansion — both carrying risks. The sector targets are ambitious across the board: industry and construction at 12.2 per cent, manufacturing at 12.3 per cent, services at 9.8 per cent. Thirty of Vietnam's 34 provinces are targeting at least 10 per cent growth. Major infrastructure priorities include the North-South high-speed railway, two new international airports, and expressway completion. The near-unanimous vote reflects the Party's determination to demonstrate that Vietnam's development model can deliver growth rates comparable to China's peak years. Whether this is achievable or aspirational will define Vietnam's economic trajectory for the next several years.

中文主播稿

主播：转向越南——国会设定了可能是东盟近年来最雄心勃勃的宏观经济目标。11月7日，469名代表中有467名投票通过决议，为2027年设定至少10%的GDP增长目标，人均GDP目标约6,100美元，CPI通胀目标约4.5%。决议将2027年描述为"加速和突破之年"。

分析师：从背景看，国际货币基金组织预测越南2027年增长7.5%——国会的目标比国际社会认为可实现的高出约2.5个百分点。越南自1990年代初革新开放改革繁荣期以来未曾实现10%的增长，当时经济体量仅为现在的零头。投资计划揭示了河内如何打算达到目标：社会投资总额2,280亿至2,430亿美元，占GDP的36%至38%。但国家预算仅覆盖670亿美元——其余的1,600亿至1,750亿美元必须来自国内私人企业、外商直接投资和股市。FDI到位目标300亿至320亿美元，较2026年前九个月的240亿美元显著加速。

主播：能融到这些资金吗？

分析师：这是关键问题。根据IMF数据，国民储蓄需要在一年内从GDP的34.9%跃升至约39.4%才能在不产生通胀压力的情况下为投资提供资金。如果储蓄跟不上，缺口需要由外国资本流入或国内信贷扩张来填补——两者都有风险。各部门目标全面雄心勃勃：工业和建筑12.2%，制造业12.3%，服务业9.8%。34个省市中有30个将增长目标设定为至少10%。主要基础设施优先事项包括南北高速铁路、两个新国际机场和高速公路完工。近乎一致的投票反映了党证明越南发展模式能够实现与中国巅峰时期相当增长率的决心。这是可实现的还是愿景性的，将决定越南未来数年的经济走向。

MSCI Denies Reprieve for Indonesia as Reclassification Consultation Threatens Emerging-Market Status

MSCI否认对印尼放软立场，重新分类磋商威胁新兴市场地位

INDONESIA · CAPITAL MARKETS / 印尼 · 资本市场

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Indonesia now — where the MSCI reclassification threat has escalated. MSCI issued a formal statement on 24 November denying reports that it had softened its stance on Indonesia's potential downgrade from Emerging Markets status. The denial came after Bloomberg reported private talks between MSCI CEO Henry Fernandez and President Prabowo Subianto in which Fernandez was said to have indicated MSCI "did not intend to downgrade" Indonesia. MSCI stated there has been no change to its consultation process.

Analyst: This is the most consequential event facing Indonesian capital markets since the 1997-98 Asian Financial Crisis. A downgrade from Emerging Markets to standalone status would mechanically force billions of dollars in passive fund outflows, as the hundreds of billions tracking the MSCI Emerging Markets Index would no longer include Indonesian equities. The denial of a reprieve following what appeared to be direct presidential diplomacy is a significant escalation — MSCI is signalling that political engagement at the highest level will not substitute for market structure reforms. The consultation runs through 30 April 2027, with the final decision due on or before 30 June. MSCI's concerns centre on opaque shareholding structures, suspected coordinated trading, and limited free float — what it calls "profound investability concerns."

Lead presenter: What reforms has Indonesia put in place?

Analyst: Indonesian regulators have implemented several measures: enhanced disclosure for holders of more than 1 per cent, a High Shareholding Concentration framework, and a roadmap to raise the minimum public free float to 15 per cent. These are directionally correct but face a credibility gap — MSCI's concern is about consistent implementation, not just the existence of rules. Meanwhile, a growing number of listed companies are reportedly seeking to go private under the tighter framework, which creates a perverse dynamic: reforms designed to improve market quality could reduce market breadth and reinforce the concentration concerns MSCI flagged. The Jakarta Composite is down more than 40 per cent year-to-date with tens of trillions of rupiah in net foreign selling. This is not just a capital markets issue — it is a test of Indonesia's institutional credibility with global investors. Indonesia was upgraded to Emerging Markets in 2010; losing that status sixteen years later would be a devastating reversal.

中文主播稿

主播：转向印尼——MSCI重新分类威胁已升级。MSCI于11月24日发布正式声明，否认已对印尼可能从新兴市场地位降级一事放软立场。此前彭博社报道了MSCI首席执行官亨利·费尔南德斯与普拉博沃·苏比安托总统之间的私人会谈，据报费尔南德斯表示MSCI"无意降级"印尼。MSCI声明磋商程序没有变化。

分析师：这是自1997-98年亚洲金融危机以来印尼资本市场面临的最具后果性的事件。从新兴市场降至独立市场地位将机械性地迫使数十亿美元的被动基金流出，因为追踪MSCI新兴市场指数的数千亿美元资产将不再包含印尼股票。在总统级别的外交干预之后缓解被否认是重大升级——MSCI在发出信号：最高层的政治接触不能替代市场结构改革。磋商期持续至2027年4月30日，最终决定在6月30日或之前作出。MSCI的担忧集中在不透明的持股结构、涉嫌协调交易和有限的流通股——其所谓的"深刻的可投资性担忧"。

主播：印尼实施了哪些改革？

分析师：印尼监管机构已实施多项措施：加强对持有上市公司1%以上股份持有者的披露要求、高持股集中度框架，以及将最低公开流通股比例提高至15%的路线图。方向正确但面临可信度差距——MSCI关注的是一致的执行，而非仅有规则。同时，越来越多的上市公司正在寻求在更严格框架下私有化，创造了悖论动态：旨在改善市场质量的改革可能减少市场广度并强化MSCI所标记的集中度担忧。雅加达综合指数年初至今下跌超过40%，外资净卖出数十万亿印尼盾。这不仅是资本市场问题——而是对印尼在全球投资者眼中制度可信度的考验。印尼在2010年升级为新兴市场；十六年后失去这一地位将是毁灭性的逆转。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Malaysia now — where Q3 GDP growth came in at 5.5 per cent year-on-year, putting the country on track for above 5 per cent growth for a third consecutive year. Manufacturing led at 7.7 per cent, driven by strong global AI-related demand across both E&E and non-E&E segments. BNM Governor Datuk Seri Abdul Rasheed Ghaffour stated the economy is "poised to enter 2027 from a position of strength."

Analyst: Malaysia stands out in ASEAN for its combination of robust growth and contained inflation — a trifecta that only Singapore currently matches. Manufacturing growth of 7.7 per cent reflects Malaysia's successful positioning in the global AI supply chain: the E&E sector accounts for approximately 40 per cent of total exports and is a direct beneficiary of the data centre and semiconductor boom. The private investment surge of 9.4 per cent is particularly significant because it suggests AI-driven demand is translating into physical capacity expansion — new semiconductor packaging plants, data centre construction, and related infrastructure. Foreign direct investment reached RM14.3 billion in Q3. Business loan growth accelerated from 7.3 to 8.8 per cent, suggesting credit is flowing to productive investment rather than speculation.

Lead presenter: How does Malaysia's inflation compare with the rest of the region?

Analyst: This is Malaysia's key comparative advantage. At 1.9 per cent headline and 1.7 per cent core, Malaysia has the lowest inflation among the major ASEAN economies with high growth. The contrast with the Philippines is now a factor of four on inflation — Malaysia at 1.9 per cent versus the Philippines at 7.8 per cent — while delivering more than double the growth rate. This gives Bank Negara significant policy space: unlike the BSP in the Philippines, which is forced to raise rates into a recession, BNM can maintain an accommodative stance that supports continued expansion. BNM projects 4.2 to 5.2 per cent growth for 2027 — a moderate slowdown but still among ASEAN's top performers. The main risk is external: a correction in the global AI cycle would hit E&E exports directly. The mining sector contraction of 6.1 per cent reflects structural factors in petroleum production but is offset by the broader economy's diversification into higher-value manufacturing and services.

中文主播稿

主播：转向马来西亚——第三季度GDP同比增长5.5%，使该国有望连续第三年实现超5%的增长。制造业以7.7%的增长领先，受全球AI相关需求在电子电气和非电子电气领域的强劲表现驱动。BNM总裁拿督斯里阿卜杜尔·拉希德·加弗尔表示经济"正以强势姿态进入2027年"。

分析师：马来西亚在东盟中脱颖而出，因为它兼具稳健增长和温和通胀——目前只有新加坡能同时做到。制造业7.7%的增长反映了马来西亚在全球AI供应链中的成功定位：电子电气行业占总出口约40%，直接受益于数据中心和半导体热潮。私人投资9.4%的飙升尤为重要，表明AI驱动的需求正在转化为实际产能扩张——新的半导体封装工厂、数据中心建设和相关基础设施。第三季度外国直接投资达143亿令吉。商业贷款增长从7.3%加速至8.8%，表明信贷正流向生产性投资而非投机。

主播：马来西亚的通胀与区域其他国家相比如何？

分析师：这是马来西亚的关键比较优势。在1.9%的整体和1.7%的核心通胀下，马来西亚在高增长的主要东盟经济体中拥有最低的通胀。与菲律宾的对比在通胀上现为四倍——马来西亚1.9%对菲律宾7.8%——同时提供两倍以上的增长率。这给予BNM显著的政策空间：与被迫在衰退中加息的菲律宾BSP不同，BNM可以维持支持持续扩张的宽松立场。BNM预测2027年增长4.2%至5.2%——适度放缓但仍位居东盟表现最佳之列。主要风险是外部的：全球AI周期修正将直接冲击电子电气出口。采矿业6.1%的收缩反映了石油生产的结构性因素，但被更广泛经济向更高价值制造和服务的多元化所抵消。

Thai Baht Hits 19-Month Low as Tourism Declines and Rate Gap with United States Widens to Three Percentage Points

泰铢跌至19个月低点，旅游业下滑且与美国利差扩大至3个百分点

ASEAN BUSINESS BRIEF
THAILAND · ECONOMY / 泰国 · 经济
SCRIPT READY · VIDEO NOT RENDERED
稿件已就绪 · 视频未生成

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Thailand now — where the baht has fallen to 33.93 per US dollar, its weakest level in approximately 19 months, down 6.7 per cent against the dollar year-to-date. The decline reflects a widening rate differential — the Bank of Thailand's benchmark at 1.00 per cent versus the Federal Reserve's 3.75 to 4.00 per cent range, creating a gap of approximately 3 percentage points. And tourism is adding to the pressure: international arrivals for January to October fell 4.59 per cent, with receipts down 2.85 per cent.

Analyst: Thailand is becoming a case study in structural divergence within ASEAN. The 3-percentage-point rate gap makes Thai assets unattractive to yield-seeking global capital, but the Bank of Thailand cannot raise rates without further suppressing an already weak domestic economy. This is the trap the K-shaped recovery has created. On one side, the AI-linked export sector is growing — the Board of Investment reports 1.71 trillion baht in investment applications, up 28 per cent, with approximately 70 per cent AI-related. But this growth is concentrated in a narrow slice of the economy that generates limited domestic employment and income spillovers. On the other side, tourism — historically Thailand's most reliable growth engine — is contracting, with the revised target now 31.5 million visitors versus 32.97 million in 2025. The Association of Thai Travel Agents warns that Chinese arrivals could fall 20 per cent in November and December.

Lead presenter: Can the central bank do anything?

Analyst: Assistant Governor Don Nakornthab was unusually candid: he described the monetary stance as "very, very accommodative" but said this was "needed because the economy has been quite weak." At 1.00 per cent, the policy rate is already historically low, and cutting further would accelerate currency depreciation and potentially import inflation. MUFG notes that Thailand's terms of trade are at their weakest in 27 years, meaning that even though export volumes are rising, the prices Thailand receives relative to what it pays for imports are deteriorating. Governor Vitai Ratanakorn said interest rates alone cannot fix structural problems — an accurate observation that raises the question of whether Thailand's political establishment has the appetite for the structural reforms in education, productivity, SME financing, and agricultural modernisation that would be needed to lift the economy's potential growth rate back above 3 per cent. At 2.5 per cent growth, Thailand is now an outlier in a region where peers are growing at 5 to 6 per cent.

中文主播稿

主播：转向泰国——泰铢跌至1美元兑33.93泰铢，约19个月来最弱水平，年初至今兑美元下跌6.7%。下跌反映了利差扩大——泰国银行基准利率1.00%对美联储3.75%至4.00%区间，差距约3个百分点。旅游业也增加了压力：1月至10月国际入境游客同比下降4.59%，收入下降2.85%。

分析师：泰国正在成为东盟内部结构性分化的案例研究。3个百分点的利差使泰国资产对寻求收益的全球资本缺乏吸引力，但泰国银行无法在不进一步抑制本已疲弱的国内经济的情况下加息。这是K型复苏所造成的陷阱。一方面，与AI相关的出口部门正在增长——投资委员会报告投资申请1.71万亿泰铢，增长28%，其中约70%与AI相关。但这种增长集中在经济的狭窄切片中，产生有限的国内就业和收入溢出效应。另一方面，旅游——历来是泰国最可靠的增长引擎——正在收缩，修订后的目标为3,150万游客，而2025年为3,297万。泰国旅行社协会警告11月和12月中国游客可能减少20%。

主播：央行能做什么？

分析师：助理行长唐·纳空塔异常坦率：将货币立场描述为"非常非常宽松"，但表示这是"必要的因为经济一直相当疲弱"。在1.00%的水平上，政策利率已处于历史低位，进一步降息将加速货币贬值并可能输入通胀。MUFG指出泰国的贸易条件处于27年来最弱水平，意味着即使出口量增加，收到的价格相对于进口支付的价格正在恶化。行长维泰·拉达纳功表示利率无法解决结构性问题——准确的观察引发了泰国政治建制是否有意愿进行提升潜在增长率所需的结构性改革（教育、生产率、中小企业融资、农业现代化）的问题。以2.5%的增长率，泰国现在是一个同行增长5%至6%的地区中的异常值。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: And finally to the Philippines — where the stagflation picture continues to deepen. ING's regional research head Deepali Bhargava forecasts that the BSP will raise its benchmark rate by 25 basis points at the 17 December meeting, downshifting from the 50-basis-point hike in October. The benchmark currently stands at 5.50 per cent after 125 basis points of cumulative tightening in 2026. October headline CPI rose to 7.8 per cent, up from 7.2 per cent in September, while core inflation accelerated to 5.4 per cent from 4.8 per cent — marking six consecutive months above the BSP's target.

Analyst: The core inflation acceleration from 4.8 to 5.4 per cent is the most alarming figure because it confirms that price pressures have become generalised across the economy. When core inflation is itself accelerating — driven by transport fares and restaurant and hotel services — businesses are passing higher input costs through to consumers across a wide range of goods and services, and inflation expectations risk becoming unmoored. Rice inflation remains at 20.7 per cent year-on-year — the single most politically sensitive number in Philippine economic data because rice is the staple food for the majority of the population. ING's expectation of a downshift to 25 basis points reflects a judgement that the BSP has front-loaded its tightening response. But the fundamental problem remains: the inflation-adjusted policy rate is still approximately minus 2.3 per cent, meaning that despite cumulative hikes, real borrowing costs remain below inflation and monetary policy is effectively still accommodative.

Lead presenter: How does this compare with the rest of ASEAN?

Analyst: The contrast is now extreme. Malaysia has 1.9 per cent inflation with 5.5 per cent growth. The Philippines has 7.8 per cent inflation with 2.1 per cent growth — four times the inflation at less than half the growth. Only the Philippines is in the stagflationary quadrant. Barclays forecasts inflation could breach 8.0 per cent in November. The PSEi has fallen 28 per cent year-to-date, trading at approximately 9.5 times forward earnings — the lowest valuation in over a decade — with PHP 123 billion in net foreign selling. The World Bank has urged structural reforms in agricultural supply chains, stating that food price inflation is a supply-side problem requiring supply-side solutions. The rate hike tool cannot increase rice production or fix typhoon-damaged supply chains. Oil prices easing and a firmer peso provide some short-term relief, but these are external factors over which the Philippines has no control. The question remains whether the government has specific structural reform plans for agriculture or is relying primarily on monetary policy to control what is fundamentally a supply-driven inflation episode.

中文主播稿

主播：最后转向菲律宾——滞胀形势继续加深。ING亚太区研究主管迪帕莉·巴尔加瓦预测BSP将在12月17日会议上加息25个基点，从10月的50个基点缩减。基准利率在2026年累计紧缩125个基点后目前为5.50%。10月整体CPI上升至7.8%，高于9月的7.2%，核心通胀从4.8%加速至5.4%——连续六个月高于BSP目标。

分析师：核心通胀从4.8%加速至5.4%是最令人担忧的数字，确认价格压力已在整个经济中全面化。当核心通胀本身也在加速——受交通费和餐饮住宿服务推动——企业正将更高的投入成本传导给各类商品和服务的消费者，通胀预期可能脱锚。大米通胀维持在同比20.7%——菲律宾经济数据中政治上最敏感的数字，因为大米是大多数人口的主食。ING对25个基点缩减的预期反映了BSP已前置紧缩应对的判断。但根本问题仍在：经通胀调整的政策利率仍约为负2.3%，意味着尽管累计加息，实际借贷成本仍低于通胀，货币政策实际上仍为宽松。

主播：与东盟其他国家相比如何？

分析师：对比现在极为悬殊。马来西亚通胀1.9%伴随5.5%增长。菲律宾通胀7.8%伴随2.1%增长——四倍的通胀率、不到一半的增长。只有菲律宾处于滞胀象限。巴克莱预测11月通胀可能突破8.0%。PSEi年初至今下跌28%，前瞻市盈率约9.5倍——十多年来最低——外资净卖出1,230亿比索。世界银行敦促农业供应链结构性改革，指出食品价格通胀是需要供给侧解决方案的供给侧问题。加息工具无法增加大米产量或修复台风破坏的供应链。油价回落和比索走强提供了一些短期缓解，但这些是菲律宾无法控制的外部因素。问题仍在于政府是否有针对农业的具体结构性改革计划，还是主要依赖货币政策来控制本质上是供给驱动的通胀事件。
