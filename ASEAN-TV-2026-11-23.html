Thailand and United States Sign First ASEAN Reciprocal Trade Agreement, Cutting Baseline Tariff from 36% to 19%

泰美签署首份东盟对等贸易协定，基准关税从36%降至19%

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. Our top story tonight — a landmark trade deal. Thailand and the United States signed the first Accelerated Reciprocal Trade agreement between Washington and an ASEAN member state on 17 November in Manila, on the sidelines of APEC trade ministers' meetings. Thai Commerce Minister Pichai Naripthaphan and US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer signed the agreement, which cuts the US baseline reciprocal tariff on Thai goods from 36 per cent to 19 per cent, with 1,907 product lines designated for zero-tariff treatment upon full implementation.

Analyst: This is the most consequential trade development in ASEAN this year — it sets a template that every other member state will now be measured against. The bilateral trade covered is approximately US$68 billion annually. Thai agricultural exports to the United States — worth about US$2.6 billion per year including rice, rubber, cassava, sugar, and processed seafood — receive immediate tariff reductions under an early harvest provision. Implementation follows a 24-month phased schedule: phase one at 90 days covers agriculture, phase two at 12 months extends to electronics, automotive parts, and textiles, and phase three at 24 months covers all 1,907 designated product lines. Thailand has committed to reciprocal market access for US soybeans, wheat, and dairy, as well as enhanced IP protections for pharmaceuticals and digital services.

Lead presenter: How significant is the reduction from 36 to 19 per cent?

Analyst: Context is essential here. Before the reciprocal tariff regime, most Thai goods entered the United States at rates between 0 and 6 per cent under MFN or GSP preferences. So 19 per cent, while dramatically lower than 36, is still a significant cost increase compared to the pre-2025 regime. The Federation of Thai Industries made exactly this point — 19 per cent is not a return to normality, it is a managed reduction from a punitive rate to a merely disadvantageous one. The real prize is those 1,907 zero-tariff product lines, which will determine which sectors genuinely benefit. The USTR's framing of this as "a model for future reciprocal trade arrangements in the Indo-Pacific" signals that Vietnam, Indonesia, and the Philippines should expect similar structural terms — reciprocal agricultural access, IP protections, digital services commitments — which will create domestic political friction in each country. The 24-month timeline also creates a window of uncertainty: electronics and automotive parts, Thailand's highest-value exports, do not get zero-tariff treatment until month 12 at the earliest, meaning Thai-based manufacturers operate under the 19 per cent rate for a full year while competitors in countries that conclude their own ARTs faster could gain an advantage.

中文主播稿

主播：各位晚上好。今晚头条——一项里程碑式的贸易协定。泰国与美国于11月17日在马尼拉APEC贸易部长会议期间签署了华盛顿与东盟成员国之间的首份加速对等贸易（ART）协定。泰国商务部长披猜·纳里帕潘与美国贸易代表杰米森·格里尔签署了该协定，将美国对泰国商品的基准对等关税从36%降至19%，1,907个产品线在全面实施后将享受零关税待遇。

分析师：这是今年东盟最重要的贸易进展——它确立了一个每个其他成员国都将以此为标杆的模板。涵盖的双边贸易额约为每年680亿美元。泰国对美农产品出口——每年约26亿美元，包括大米、橡胶、木薯、糖和加工海产品——根据早期收获条款获得即时关税减免。实施按24个月分阶段推进：第一阶段90天后涵盖农产品，第二阶段12个月后扩展至电子产品、汽车零部件和纺织品，第三阶段24个月后覆盖全部1,907个指定产品线。泰国承诺对美国大豆、小麦和乳制品给予对等市场准入，并加强药品和数字服务的知识产权保护。

主播：从36%降至19%意义有多大？

分析师：背景至关重要。在对等关税制度之前，大多数泰国商品以最惠国或普惠制优惠下0%至6%的税率进入美国。因此19%虽然大幅低于36%，但相比2025年前的制度仍是显著的成本增加。泰国工业联合会明确指出了这一点——19%不是回归常态，而是从惩罚性税率到仅仅不利税率的管理性降低。真正的奖品是那1,907个零关税产品线，它们将决定哪些行业真正受益。美国贸易代表办公室将此定位为"印太地区未来对等贸易安排的典范"，意味着越南、印尼和菲律宾应预期类似条款——对等农业准入、知识产权保护、数字服务承诺——这将在每个国家引发国内政治摩擦。24个月的时间表也创造了不确定窗口：电子产品和汽车零部件——泰国最高价值出口——最早要到第12个月才能获得零关税待遇，意味着泰国制造商将在19%的税率下运营整整一年。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Vietnam now — where foreign direct investment numbers have reached a level not seen in 17 years. The Ministry of Planning and Investment reports that registered FDI in the first ten months of 2026 hit US$54.85 billion, a 74 per cent year-on-year surge. Disbursed FDI reached US$24.08 billion, up 13.1 per cent.

Analyst: These are extraordinary numbers. To put them in perspective, US$54.85 billion in registered FDI in just ten months already exceeds full-year records from previous years. Singapore leads source countries with approximately 31 per cent of registered capital, followed by South Korea, Japan, China, and Hong Kong. Manufacturing absorbs 65 per cent of the total, with electronics and semiconductor investments alone exceeding US$20 billion — driven by continued supply chain diversification from China and expansion by Samsung, LG, and Foxconn in northern Vietnam. The MPI expects full-year registered FDI to exceed US$60 billion, which would set a new annual record.

Lead presenter: There is also a significant rating development?

Analyst: Yes — Rating and Investment Information, the Japanese credit rating agency, assigned Vietnam its first investment-grade sovereign rating of BBB- in November. This is genuinely consequential: it opens the door to inclusion in investment-grade bond indices, potentially reducing borrowing costs by 50 to 100 basis points. However, R&I has limited global influence compared to Moody's, S&P, and Fitch, none of which have yet assigned Vietnam investment-grade status. The practical impact depends on whether the global agencies follow. There is also a critical gap to watch: the disbursement ratio — actual capital deployed versus committed — is 44 per cent, lower than Vietnam's historical average of 50 to 55 per cent. This suggests that an increasing share of commitments are either front-loaded announcements for multi-year projects or conditional on infrastructure delivery. Vietnam's power grid has experienced capacity constraints, northern industrial zones are approaching full occupancy, and labour availability in manufacturing hubs is tightening. The question is whether infrastructure can keep pace with the investment pipeline.

中文主播稿

主播：转向越南——外商直接投资数字达到了17年来未见的水平。计划投资部数据显示，2026年前十个月注册FDI达548.5亿美元，同比飙升74%。实际到位FDI达240.8亿美元，同比增长13.1%。

分析师：这些数字令人瞩目。从历史角度看，仅十个月的548.5亿美元注册FDI已超过此前各年的全年纪录。新加坡以约31%的注册资本份额领先，其次是韩国、日本、中国和香港。制造业吸收了总额的65%，仅电子和半导体投资就超过200亿美元——受中国供应链持续多元化以及三星、LG和富士康在越南北部扩张推动。MPI预计全年注册FDI将超过600亿美元，将创年度新纪录。

主播：还有一项重要的评级进展？

分析师：是的——日本信用评级机构评级投资信息公司（R&I）于11月授予越南首个投资级主权评级BBB-。这确实具有重大意义：它为纳入投资级债券指数打开了大门，可能将借贷成本降低50至100个基点。然而，R&I与穆迪、标普和惠誉相比全球影响力有限，后三者均未授予越南投资级评级。实际影响取决于全球机构是否跟进。还有一个关键差距值得关注：到位率——实际部署资本与承诺资本之比——为44%，低于越南约50%至55%的历史平均水平。这表明越来越多的承诺要么是多年期项目的前置公告，要么取决于尚未完成的基础设施交付。越南电网出现容量限制，北部工业区接近满负荷，制造业中心的劳动力供给趋紧。问题在于基础设施能否跟上投资管线的步伐。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Malaysia has delivered a Q3 GDP result that beat expectations — 5.5 per cent year-on-year growth, above the 5.3 per cent consensus. It makes Malaysia the second-fastest growing major ASEAN economy in the quarter, behind only Vietnam. What is driving this performance?

Analyst: The expansion is impressively broad-based. Manufacturing grew 6.8 per cent, led by a 13 per cent surge in electronics and electrical products linked to the global AI infrastructure buildout. Construction expanded 16.7 per cent — its fastest pace in over a decade — driven overwhelmingly by data centre facilities in Johor and Selangor, where 117 projects are already approved. Services grew 5.9 per cent on wholesale and retail trade, financial services, and IT. The only weak spot was mining and quarrying, which contracted 2.1 per cent on lower oil and gas production volumes.

Lead presenter: And monetary policy is accommodative by regional standards?

Analyst: Remarkably so. Bank Negara Malaysia held the overnight policy rate at 3.00 per cent, citing stable momentum and benign inflation. Malaysia's headline inflation was 1.9 per cent in October — the lowest among major ASEAN economies. This is Malaysia's most significant competitive advantage in the current cycle. The inflation differential means real interest rates in Malaysia are positive at approximately 1.1 per cent, while Indonesia and the Philippines have negative real rates despite far higher nominal rates of 6.00 and 5.50 per cent respectively. BNM has the luxury of supporting domestic consumption and investment without the currency or inflation pressures forcing other central banks to tighten aggressively. PETRONAS Gas added to the positive picture with Q3 net profit of RM630.54 million, up 42 per cent year-on-year, providing fiscal support at a time when the government's RON95 petrol subsidy rationalisation remains politically sensitive. The question is sustainability — whether the 16.7 per cent construction growth rate can hold as the government discourages new data centre approvals while prioritising execution of the existing pipeline.

SINGAPORE · MONETARY POLICY / 新加坡 · 货币政策

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Singapore's central bank has held its policy stance unchanged, and the inflation picture is the most benign in ASEAN. The Monetary Authority of Singapore maintained the slope, width, and centre of the S$NEER policy band following earlier tightening in April and July. Core inflation — the MAS's preferred gauge — has fallen below 2 per cent for the first time since the second quarter of 2021.

Analyst: This is what a successful soft landing looks like. Core inflation between 1.5 and 2.0 per cent, GDP growth of 5.4 per cent, unemployment at 1.9 per cent near historical lows. The contrast with regional peers is stark: Singapore's core inflation is below 2 per cent while the Philippines is at 5.4 per cent; Singapore's growth is 5.4 per cent while Thailand manages only 2.9 per cent. Manufacturing expanded 11.4 per cent, driven by semiconductors, pharmaceuticals, and biomedical output. Financial services grew 6.2 per cent on wealth management inflows and capital markets activity. The Ministry of Trade and Industry narrowed the full-year growth forecast to 4.0 to 5.0 per cent, up from the earlier 3.0 to 5.0 per cent range.

Lead presenter: What explains Singapore's outperformance?

Analyst: Two structural advantages. First, MAS's exchange-rate-centred monetary policy framework — using the S$NEER as the primary tool rather than interest rates — provides a natural inflation buffer through currency appreciation. This keeps imported costs low without the domestic demand destruction that rate hikes cause in other economies. Singapore can fight inflation and support growth simultaneously, which is impossible for interest-rate-based central banks. Second, Singapore's deliberate positioning at the top of the technology value chain — semiconductors, pharmaceuticals, biomedical products — means its manufacturing demand is less cyclical and higher-margin than the commodity-driven manufacturing elsewhere in ASEAN. MAS Managing Director Chia Der Jiun said the stance "remains appropriate" and that MAS would continue monitoring US monetary policy and geopolitical risks. With a trade-to-GDP ratio exceeding 300 per cent, Singapore remains the region's leading indicator: when Singapore's trade-dependent growth slows, it typically foreshadows export weakness across ASEAN with a one-to-two-quarter lag.

中文主播稿

主播：新加坡央行维持政策立场不变，通胀形势为东盟中最温和。新加坡金融管理局在此前4月和7月紧缩后，维持新元名义有效汇率政策区间的斜率、宽度和中心不变。核心通胀——MAS偏好的指标——自2021年第二季度以来首次降至2%以下。

分析师：这就是成功软着陆的样子。核心通胀1.5%至2.0%，GDP增长5.4%，失业率1.9%接近历史低位。与区域同行的对比极为鲜明：新加坡核心通胀低于2%而菲律宾为5.4%；新加坡增长5.4%而泰国仅2.9%。制造业在半导体、制药和生物医药产出推动下扩张11.4%。金融服务在财富管理资金流入和资本市场活动支撑下增长6.2%。贸易和工业部将全年增长预测收窄至4.0%至5.0%，高于此前3.0%至5.0%的区间。

主播：如何解释新加坡的优异表现？

分析师：两个结构性优势。第一，MAS以汇率为中心的货币政策框架——使用S$NEER作为主要工具而非利率——通过货币升值提供天然的通胀缓冲。这在不造成其他经济体加息带来的国内需求萎缩的情况下保持进口成本低廉。新加坡可以同时对抗通胀和支持增长，这对以利率为基础的央行是不可能的。第二，新加坡有意定位于技术价值链顶端——半导体、制药、生物医药产品——意味着其制造业需求周期性较弱，利润率高于东盟其他地方以大宗商品驱动的制造业。MAS常务董事谢德润表示立场"仍然适当"，MAS将继续关注美国货币政策和地缘政治风险。贸易占GDP比率超过300%的新加坡仍是该地区的领先指标：当新加坡依赖贸易的增长放缓时，它通常预示东盟出口疲弱将在一到两个季度后出现。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Indonesia now — where the capital markets picture is growing increasingly alarming. MSCI has removed nine Indonesian stocks from its global indexes in the November semi-annual review, with zero additions. This is the fourth consecutive review in which no Indonesian company was added. The deletions include Barito Renewables Energy and Bumi Resources Minerals.

Analyst: This is a devastating result that deepens a self-reinforcing downward spiral. Each barren review reduces Indonesia's weight in MSCI indexes, which triggers mechanical selling by passive funds that track those indexes, which depresses stock prices and market capitalisation further, making future additions even less likely. The Jakarta Composite Index has fallen 41.59 per cent year-to-date — the worst-performing major stock market in Asia. Net foreign selling has reached Rp61 trillion, approximately US$3.8 billion, driven by MSCI rebalancing, the strong US dollar, and risk aversion following Bank Indonesia's surprise rate hike to 6 per cent.

Lead presenter: What about the reclassification risk?

Analyst: That is the existential threat. MSCI placed Indonesia on its annual market classification review watchlist in June 2026, raising the possibility of reclassification from emerging market to frontier market status by June 2027. This would remove Indonesia from the investment universe of hundreds of emerging market funds that are prohibited from holding frontier market securities — it would be an institutional capital exodus. The irony is that Indonesia's real economy is performing well — Q3 GDP grew 5.47 per cent — but capital markets are pricing in this structural risk. The OJK has implemented stabilisation measures including asymmetric auto-rejection limits and enhanced short-selling surveillance, while the Indonesia Stock Exchange has proposed reforms to foreign ownership disclosure and trading halt mechanisms. OJK Chairman Mahendra Siregar described conditions as "reflecting global headwinds rather than fundamental weakness." But the reforms may come too late — Indonesia has approximately seven months to demonstrate meaningful improvements before MSCI's June 2027 reclassification review. The practical question is whether regulatory reforms can reverse a two-year momentum of capital outflows and declining index weight.

中文主播稿

主播：转向印尼——资本市场形势日益令人担忧。MSCI在11月半年度审议中从全球指数移除九只印尼股票，零新增。这是连续第四次审议没有印尼公司被纳入。被移除的包括Barito Renewables Energy和Bumi Resources Minerals。

分析师：这是一个毁灭性的结果，加深了自我强化的下行螺旋。每次空手而归的审议都会降低印尼在MSCI指数中的权重，触发跟踪这些指数的被动基金的机械性抛售，进一步压低股价和市值，使未来纳入更不可能。雅加达综合指数年初至今下跌41.59%——亚洲表现最差的主要股市。外资净卖出达61万亿印尼盾（约38亿美元），受MSCI再平衡、美元走强以及印尼央行意外加息至6%后的风险厌恶推动。

主播：重新分类风险呢？

分析师：那是生存性威胁。MSCI于2026年6月将印尼列入年度市场分类审议观察名单，提出了2027年6月前从新兴市场重新分类为前沿市场的可能性。这将把印尼从数百只被禁止持有前沿市场证券的新兴市场基金的投资范围中移除——那将是机构资本的大撤离。讽刺的是，印尼实体经济表现良好——第三季度GDP增长5.47%——但资本市场正在为这一结构性风险定价。OJK已实施稳定措施，包括非对称自动拒绝限制和加强卖空监控，印尼证券交易所也提出了外资持股披露和交易暂停机制的改革。OJK主席马亨德拉·西雷加尔将状况描述为"反映全球逆风而非基本面疲弱"。但改革可能为时已晚——印尼在MSCI 2027年6月重新分类审议前大约有七个月时间来展示实质性改善。实际问题在于监管改革能否扭转两年来资本外流和指数权重下降的势头。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: And finally to the Philippines, where the economic picture is now the most troubling in ASEAN. Moody's Analytics has cut its 2026 GDP forecast to 2.3 per cent from 3.5 per cent, following Q3 growth of just 2.1 per cent — the weakest quarterly result since the pandemic. At the same time, headline inflation has hit 7.8 per cent and food inflation has reached 10.2 per cent.

Analyst: This is the stagflation combination that central bankers dread — high inflation with weak growth. BPI chief economist Emilio Neri put it most clearly: "Rate hikes don't grow rice, but pausing could harvest inflation." The core inflation acceleration to 5.4 per cent is particularly alarming because it shows price pressures have become embedded beyond volatile food and energy items. The real policy rate — the benchmark rate minus inflation — is approximately negative 2.3 per cent. Despite 125 basis points of cumulative hikes bringing the BSP's rate to 5.50 per cent, monetary policy is effectively still accommodative because inflation has outrun the rate increases. Barclays forecasts inflation could hit 8.0 per cent in November and sees the possibility of another 50-basis-point hike at the BSP's December meeting.

Lead presenter: How does this compare with the rest of ASEAN?

Analyst: The contrast is now extreme. Malaysia has 1.9 per cent inflation with 5.5 per cent growth. The Philippines has 7.8 per cent inflation with 2.1 per cent growth — four times the inflation at less than half the growth. Only the Philippines is in the stagflationary quadrant. The Q3 GDP breakdown shows broad-based weakness: household consumption grew just 2.4 per cent — the weakest since 2010 outside the pandemic — investment contracted 0.2 per cent, and agriculture fell 4.8 per cent. Agriculture's contraction directly feeds food prices, creating a feedback loop. MSCI has also removed Meralco and PLDT — two of the Philippines' most liquid blue chips — from its global indexes, while the PSEi has fallen 28 per cent year-to-date with PHP 123 billion in net foreign selling. The World Bank has separately urged structural reforms in agricultural supply chains, including reducing rice import restrictions, stating that "food price inflation in the Philippines is a supply-side problem that requires supply-side solutions." The 9.5 times forward earnings on the PSEi is cheap by any historical measure, but cheapness does not attract capital when the macro trajectory is deteriorating — value investors require a credible catalyst, and the Philippines currently offers none.

中文主播稿

主播：最后转向菲律宾——目前东盟中经济图景最令人担忧的国家。穆迪分析将其2026年GDP预测从3.5%下调至2.3%，此前第三季度GDP仅增长2.1%——疫情以来最弱的季度表现。与此同时，整体通胀达到7.8%，食品通胀达10.2%。

分析师：这是央行最恐惧的滞胀组合——高通胀伴随弱增长。BPI首席经济学家埃米利奥·内里说得最清楚："加息不能种出大米，但暂停可能收获通胀。"核心通胀加速至5.4%尤其令人担忧，因为它显示价格压力已超越波动性食品和能源项目而变得根深蒂固。实际政策利率——基准利率减去通胀——约为负2.3%。尽管累计加息125个基点将BSP利率提至5.50%，货币政策实际上仍是宽松的，因为通胀已经超越了加息幅度。巴克莱预测11月通胀可能达到8.0%，并认为BSP可能在12月会议上再次加息50个基点。

主播：与东盟其他国家相比如何？

分析师：对比现在极为悬殊。马来西亚通胀1.9%伴随5.5%增长。菲律宾通胀7.8%伴随2.1%增长——四倍的通胀率、不到一半的增长。只有菲律宾处于滞胀象限。第三季度GDP构成显示全面疲弱：家庭消费仅增长2.4%——疫情外2010年以来最弱——投资收缩0.2%，农业下降4.8%。农业收缩直接推升食品价格，形成反馈循环。MSCI还从全球指数中移除了Meralco和PLDT——菲律宾流动性最强的两只蓝筹股，PSEi年初至今下跌28%，外资净卖出1,230亿比索。世界银行另外敦促农业供应链结构性改革，包括减少大米进口限制，指出"菲律宾的食品价格通胀是供给侧问题，需要供给侧解决方案"。PSEi 9.5倍的前瞻市盈率以任何历史标准衡量都很便宜，但当宏观轨迹恶化时，仅靠估值低廉并不能吸引资本——价值投资者需要可信的催化剂，而菲律宾目前没有提供任何催化剂。
