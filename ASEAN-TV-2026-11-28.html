美国贸易代表依据301条款提议对马来西亚加征18–25%关税，政府力争将税率上限控制在19%

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. We begin in Malaysia — where a major trade escalation is threatening the country's export-driven growth model. The US Trade Representative has proposed tariffs of 18 to 25 per cent on Malaysian goods following Section 301 investigations covering 14 economies. The investigations examined concerns about industrial expansion policies, preferential financing, and measures related to excess manufacturing capacity. Currently, only the 10 per cent tariff imposed in July 2026 is in effect. Final tariff levels are expected in the first quarter of 2027, with written submissions due by 5 January and public hearings beginning 25 January.

Analyst: The timing of this is critical. Malaysia's economy is performing at its best in years — Q3 GDP growth of 5.5 per cent, record trade figures driven by electronics exports, and booming AI-related manufacturing investment. But that very success is making Malaysia a larger trade target. The country's trade surplus with the United States has been widening, and that is precisely what Section 301 investigations are designed to address. The 18 to 25 per cent range is a negotiating bracket, not a final determination. MITI has responded firmly, stating it will fight to ensure total tariffs do not exceed 19 per cent, and maintaining that Malaysia's manufacturing has no excess capacity that distorts global markets. The ministry also indicated it will review goods imported from third countries — a signal to Washington that Malaysia is willing to address concerns about Chinese manufacturing capacity being redirected through ASEAN countries.

Lead presenter: What about the Agreement on Reciprocal Trade that was signed last year?

Analyst: That is a significant complication. The ART was signed on 26 October 2025, but MITI confirmed in February 2026 that it has not been ratified due to new developments in the US. The agreement was supposed to provide the institutional framework for managing precisely this kind of trade tension. Without ratification, Malaysia is negotiating without that safety net. Economist Geoffrey Williams has warned that even the proposed 18 per cent rate — slightly below the previously negotiated 19 per cent ceiling — does not guarantee a favourable outcome, because duties could ultimately reach 25 per cent. His observation that bilateral trade hit record levels under the earlier arrangement but Malaysia's surplus widened is the key dynamic — when you are running a large and growing surplus with the world's largest economy, you attract scrutiny regardless of the underlying economics. The semiconductor and E&E sector is particularly vulnerable because it sits at the intersection of two US policy concerns: trade surplus reduction and AI chip supply chain control. MITI faces the challenge of negotiating on both fronts simultaneously with an administration that has shown willingness to use economic leverage aggressively.

主播：各位晚上好。我们从马来西亚开始——一场重大贸易升级正在威胁该国出口驱动的增长模式。美国贸易代表在完成对14个经济体的301条款调查后，提议对马来西亚商品征收18%至25%的关税。调查审查了关于产业扩张政策、优惠融资和与产能过剩相关措施的担忧。目前仅有2026年7月征收的10%关税生效。最终关税水平预计于2027年第一季度公布，书面意见提交截止日为1月5日，公开听证会于1月25日开始。

分析师：时机至关重要。马来西亚经济正处于多年来最佳状态——第三季度GDP增长5.5%、电子出口驱动的创纪录贸易数据、以及蓬勃的AI相关制造业投资。但正是这种成功使马来西亚成为更大的贸易目标。该国对美贸易顺差一直在扩大，而这正是301条款调查旨在解决的问题。18%至25%的范围是谈判区间而非最终裁定。MITI坚定回应，表示将力争确保总关税不超过19%，并坚持马来西亚制造业不存在扭曲全球市场的过剩产能。该部还表示将审查从第三国进口的商品——向华盛顿发出信号，表明马来西亚愿意解决中国制造产能通过东盟国家转移的担忧。

主播：去年签署的对等贸易协定情况如何？

分析师：这是一个重大复杂因素。ART于2025年10月26日签署，但MITI在2026年2月确认因美国出现新情况尚未获批准。该协定本应为管理这种贸易紧张提供制度框架。没有批准，马来西亚在没有安全网的情况下进行谈判。经济学家杰弗里·威廉姆斯警告，即使拟议的18%税率略低于此前谈判的19%上限，也不能保证有利结果，因为税率最终可能达到25%。他观察到双边贸易在早期安排下创纪录但马来西亚顺差扩大——这是关键动态——当你与世界最大经济体保持巨额且不断增长的顺差时，无论基本经济状况如何都会引来审视。半导体和电子电气行业尤其脆弱，因为它处于美国两项政策关切的交汇处：削减贸易顺差和AI芯片供应链管控。MITI面临同时在两条战线与一个已展示出激进使用经济杠杆意愿的政府谈判的挑战。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Indonesia now — where Bank Indonesia has raised rates again and issued an unusually detailed warning about the challenges ahead. The central bank hiked the BI Rate by 25 basis points in November to 6 per cent, bringing cumulative tightening in 2026 to 125 basis points. The rupiah has weakened approximately 9.87 per cent against the US dollar year-to-date — the most aggressive tightening cycle in the region outside the Philippines.

Analyst: What makes this significant is not just the rate hike itself, but the accompanying message. Speaking at the 2026 Bank Indonesia Annual Meeting, Governor Destry Damayanti listed five specific structural factors driving persistent global uncertainty: unresolved geopolitical conflicts with no clear timeline, growing protectionism that could accelerate economic fragmentation, rising public debt reducing fiscal space, persistently high global interest rates strengthening the US dollar, and — notably — artificial intelligence developments that could add inflation pressure, drive up energy prices, and increase financial market volatility. This is one of the first times a major ASEAN central bank governor has explicitly flagged AI energy demand as a potential inflationary force. The 125 basis points of tightening from 4.75 per cent to 6 per cent places Indonesia in an uncomfortable position. The rate hikes are primarily designed to defend the rupiah rather than control domestic inflation, which remains relatively contained. This creates a tension: the economy needs support, but the currency needs defence.

Lead presenter: Can Indonesia still hit its 6 per cent growth target for 2027?

Analyst: That is the central question Governor Destry was implicitly addressing. Q3 GDP growth of 5.47 per cent shows solid momentum, the manufacturing PMI rose to 53.98 in October, and domestic demand remains supportive. But achieving 6 per cent would require a step-up from current levels precisely when monetary policy is tightening and global headwinds are intensifying. BI has set the 2027 rupiah assumption at 17,800 to 18,200 per dollar — implying further depreciation from current levels. The rupiah's nearly 10 per cent decline reflects Indonesia's structural vulnerability as a large current account deficit economy dependent on commodity exports and portfolio capital inflows. The rate hikes have slowed but not reversed the depreciation, suggesting interest rate differentials alone are insufficient when global risk appetite deteriorates. The comparison within ASEAN is instructive: Indonesia is tightening aggressively while Thailand sits at 1 per cent and Vietnam at 4.5 per cent — reflecting fundamentally different external vulnerability profiles. Whether BI will pause at 6 per cent or continue tightening into 2027 depends on the rupiah's trajectory, which in turn depends on the very global factors Governor Destry warned about.

中文主播稿

主播：转向印尼——印尼央行再次加息并发出关于未来挑战的异常详细警告。央行11月将BI利率上调25个基点至6%，2026年累计紧缩125个基点。卢比年初至今对美元贬值约9.87%——这是除菲律宾外该地区最激进的紧缩周期。

分析师：重要的不仅是加息本身，还有随附的信息。戴丝特丽·达玛扬蒂行长在2026年印尼央行年会上列出了推动全球持续不确定性的五个具体结构性因素：没有明确时间表的未解决地缘政治冲突、可能加速经济碎片化的日益增长保护主义、不断上升的公共债务减少财政空间、持续高企的全球利率加强美元，以及——值得注意的是——人工智能发展可能增加通胀压力、推高能源价格和加剧金融市场波动。这是东盟主要央行行长首次明确将AI能源需求标记为潜在通胀力量之一。从4.75%到6%的125个基点紧缩使印尼处于不舒适的位置。加息主要旨在保卫卢比而非控制国内通胀，后者仍相对温和。这制造了紧张关系：经济需要支持，但货币需要防御。

主播：印尼还能实现2027年6%的增长目标吗？

分析师：这正是戴丝特丽行长隐含在讨论中的核心问题。第三季度5.47%的GDP增长显示稳健势头，制造业PMI在10月升至53.98，国内需求仍具支撑性。但在货币政策收紧和全球逆风加剧之际实现6%需要从当前水平提升。BI已将2027年卢比假设设定为每美元17,800至18,200——暗示从当前水平进一步贬值。卢比近10%的贬值反映了印尼作为依赖大宗商品出口和组合资本流入的大型经常账户赤字经济体的结构性脆弱性。加息减缓但未逆转贬值，表明当全球风险偏好恶化时仅利率差异不足以稳定货币。东盟内部的比较具有启示性：印尼在积极紧缩，而泰国利率为1%、越南4.5%——反映了根本不同的外部脆弱性。BI是否将在6%暂停还是继续紧缩取决于卢比走势，而卢比走势又取决于戴丝特丽行长所警告的那些全球因素。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Vietnam now — where the country's ambitious international financial centre has taken a significant operational step. The Executive Authority of the Vietnam International Financial Centre in Ho Chi Minh City has recognised its first 10 holding companies as Members, with a combined registered charter capital of US$33.63 million and total capital expected to be mobilised through 2027 of approximately US$2.7 billion. The certificates were presented at the Autumn Economic Forum 2026 in the presence of Prime Minister Le Minh Hung.

Analyst: This is the moment when VIFC-HCMC transitions from legislation to operational reality. The 10 founding Members span infrastructure, energy, fintech, logistics, and asset management — reflecting Vietnam's strategic priorities as its economy grows at nearly 10 per cent annually. The largest commitments include Saigon Marina IFC at US$816 million for technology infrastructure, AG&P Holding Vietnam — a Singapore-based company — at US$425 million for the LNG-to-power-and-data-centre value chain with plans to scale to US$750 million to US$1 billion by 2030, and EQuest Capital at US$325 million through a cooperation agreement with Standard Chartered Bank for education infrastructure. Keppel Asset Management is studying co-investment platforms targeting up to US$1 billion from third-party capital. The Keppel and Standard Chartered involvement is strategically significant — these are established international names that lend credibility to a centre that is still building its institutional foundations.

Lead presenter: What is notably absent from this first round?

Analyst: Banks. The recognition round covers holding companies only. Banks, securities companies, and investment funds will be considered in subsequent rounds once licensing regulations for conditional business lines are issued. This is the most critical gap. Without licensed banks operating within the VIFC framework, the centre cannot perform the core functions of an international financial centre — cross-border capital intermediation, foreign currency settlement, and international securities trading. Vietnam is pushing to fast-track bank licensing, with regulations on investment fund admission expected this month. The comparison with regional competitors is instructive. Singapore's financial centre took decades to build under a comprehensive regulatory framework. Hong Kong benefits from Common Law foundations. The VIFC is attempting to compress this development timeline dramatically — launching operational transactions within months of its legal establishment. Whether the regulatory framework can evolve quickly enough without compromising standards is the central question. The US$33.63 million in registered capital against US$2.7 billion in projected mobilisation is a leverage ratio that relies entirely on the centre's credibility as a capital-raising platform. That credibility will be tested as the VIFC moves from recognition ceremonies to actual cross-border transactions.

中文主播稿

主播：转向越南——该国雄心勃勃的国际金融中心迈出重要运营步伐。越南国际金融中心胡志明市执行机构已认可首批10家控股公司为中心会员，合计注册资本3,363万美元，预计到2027年动员和投资的总资本约27亿美元。认可证书在2026年秋季经济论坛上颁发，黎明洪总理出席。

分析师：这是VIFC-HCMC从立法过渡到运营现实的时刻。10家创始会员涵盖基础设施、能源、金融科技、物流和资产管理——反映了越南在经济以接近10%速度增长时的战略重点。最大的承诺包括西贡滨海IFC 8.16亿美元用于技术基础设施，AG&P控股越南（新加坡注册公司）4.25亿美元用于液化天然气到电力和数据中心价值链并计划到2030年扩大到7.5亿至10亿美元，以及EQuest Capital 3.25亿美元通过与渣打银行的合作协议用于教育基础设施。吉宝资产管理正在研究目标达10亿美元第三方资本的联合投资平台。吉宝和渣打的参与具有战略意义——这些是成熟的国际知名机构，为仍在建设制度基础的中心提供了信誉支撑。

分析师：银行。本轮认可仅涵盖控股公司。银行、证券公司和投资基金将在附条件行业许可法规颁布后的后续轮次中考虑。这是最关键的缺口。没有在VIFC框架内运营的持牌银行，中心无法执行国际金融中心的核心功能——跨境资本中介、外币结算和国际证券交易。越南正在推动加快银行许可，投资基金准入规定预计本月发布。与区域竞争对手的比较具有启示性。新加坡金融中心在全面监管框架下花了数十年建设。香港受益于普通法基础。VIFC试图大幅压缩这一发展时间线——在法律建立后数月内启动运营交易。监管框架能否在不损害标准的情况下足够快地演变是核心问题。3,363万美元注册资本对应27亿美元预计动员额，这一杠杆比率完全依赖中心作为融资平台的信誉。从认可仪式过渡到实际跨境交易时，这一信誉将受到检验。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Thailand now — where a prominent economic research house is warning of a deeply divided recovery. The SCB Economic Intelligence Center has warned that Thailand faces a K-shaped recovery: AI-related exports and foreign investment are booming in specific industries, while domestic demand, SMEs, and lower-income households continue to lag. SCB EIC projects GDP growth at just 2.2 per cent in 2026 and 2.1 per cent in 2027 — both well below the country's growth potential.

Analyst: The K-shape label captures something important about Thailand's economic reality. Third-quarter GDP grew 2.9 per cent, placing Thailand fifth among the six major ASEAN economies — ahead only of the stagflation-trapped Philippines. For context: Vietnam grew 9.95 per cent, Singapore 6.1 per cent, Malaysia 5.5 per cent, Indonesia 5.47 per cent. Thailand at 2.9 per cent is barely above one-third of the ASEAN average. The external-facing economy is performing well — AI-related electronics exports, data centre FDI, and digital infrastructure investment are supporting headline numbers. But SCB EIC's critical finding is that these industries rely heavily on imported capital goods, raw materials, and intermediate products, so less value is added domestically. Links with Thai suppliers and workers remain limited, meaning export and investment gains have not translated into broad-based income and employment improvement.

Lead presenter: Is monetary policy making things worse?

Analyst: The Bank of Thailand's policy rate at 1 per cent — the lowest in ASEAN — reflects its acknowledgment that the economy is too fragile for normalisation. SCB EIC expects the rate to stay there through the end of 2027. But even this accommodative stance has not translated into credit flow for SMEs, which face tighter risk assessments from commercial banks. SMEs and domestic-demand businesses are caught in a squeeze — weak income, falling margins, liquidity problems, tighter credit, and intensifying competition. Lower-income households face slow income growth, high debt, and few financial buffers. The core structural problem is that Thailand is capturing a share of the global AI supply chain but extracting relatively little domestic value from it. The multiplier effect that should theoretically flow from exports and FDI to wages and consumption is significantly attenuated. SCB EIC's recommended solutions are strategically correct — using foreign investment to create domestic value through higher-value activities, strengthening links between foreign investors and Thai suppliers, and supporting struggling businesses. But these require policy execution that has historically been challenging in Thailand. The risk is that the K-shaped pattern becomes self-reinforcing: the AI-linked export sector thrives while the domestic economy stagnates, creating a two-speed Thailand that widens inequality and undermines long-term social stability.

中文主播稿

主播：转向泰国——一家知名经济研究机构警告深度分化的复苏。汇商银行经济智能中心（SCB EIC）警告泰国面临K型复苏：AI相关出口和外国投资在特定行业蓬勃发展，而国内需求、中小企业和低收入家庭继续落后。SCB EIC预测2026年GDP增长仅2.2%，2027年2.1%——均远低于该国增长潜力。

分析师：K型标签捕捉了泰国经济现实的重要特征。第三季度GDP增长2.9%，在六个主要东盟经济体中排名第五——仅高于陷入滞胀的菲律宾。作为参照：越南增长9.95%，新加坡6.1%，马来西亚5.5%，印尼5.47%。泰国2.9%仅略高于东盟平均水平的三分之一。面向外部的经济表现良好——AI相关电子出口、数据中心FDI和数字基础设施投资在支撑整体数据。但SCB EIC的关键发现是这些行业严重依赖进口资本品、原材料和中间产品，因此国内附加值较少。与泰国供应商和工人的联系仍然有限，意味着出口和投资收益未转化为广泛的收入和就业改善。

主播：货币政策是否在加剧问题？

分析师：泰国银行1%的政策利率——东盟最低——反映了其承认经济过于脆弱无法正常化。SCB EIC预计利率将维持至2027年底。但即使这种宽松立场也未转化为中小企业的信贷流动，后者面临商业银行更严格的风险评估。中小企业和依赖国内需求的企业被困在困境中——收入疲弱、利润率下降、流动性问题、信贷收紧和竞争加剧。低收入家庭面临收入增长缓慢、高负债和少量金融缓冲。核心结构性问题是泰国正在获取全球AI供应链份额但从中提取的国内价值相对较少。理论上应从出口和FDI流向工资和消费的乘数效应被显著削弱。SCB EIC建议的解决方案在战略上是正确的——利用外资通过更高价值活动创造国内价值、加强外国投资者与泰国供应商的联系、支持困难企业。但这些需要在泰国历来具有挑战性的政策执行。风险在于K型格局变得自我强化：AI关联出口部门繁荣而国内经济停滞，创造双速泰国，扩大不平等并削弱长期社会稳定。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Singapore now — where Deputy Prime Minister Gan Kim Yong has used a major regional forum to call for deeper ASEAN economic integration in response to rising protectionism. Speaking at the SPH Media Asia Future Summit on 27 November, Gan argued that artificial intelligence, geopolitical shocks, and geo-economic contestation between Washington and Beijing make ASEAN integration especially urgent. The upgraded ASEAN Trade in Goods Agreement — ATIGA — is set to be rolled out by the end of 2026.

Analyst: This speech functions as an advance policy statement. Singapore takes the rotating ASEAN chairmanship in 2027 — the year ASEAN marks its 60th anniversary — and Gan is signalling that trade integration will be the centrepiece of that chairmanship agenda. The backdrop is a rapidly deteriorating trade environment. In the past two weeks alone, the US has announced Section 301 tariff proposals covering 14 economies including multiple ASEAN members, and expanded AI chip export controls to Southeast Asia. For a region that has built its economic model on open trade and export-led growth, this represents a fundamental challenge. The upgraded ATIGA is the most concrete deliverable. The original agreement, implemented in 2010, eliminated tariffs on 99 per cent of intra-ASEAN trade, but its effectiveness has been undermined by non-tariff barriers, complex rules of origin, and inconsistent implementation. The upgrade addresses these gaps — and if effectively rolled out, it could reduce intra-ASEAN trade costs and partially offset rising external tariffs by making it easier for producers to source inputs from within the bloc.

Lead presenter: Will ASEAN actually be able to integrate fast enough to respond to US tariffs?

Analyst: That is the defining question for 2027. ASEAN integration has historically progressed slowly — consensus-based decision-making and sovereignty sensitivities limit the pace. Gan called for clear, predictable rules, respect for laws and commitments, and firm action against wrongdoing. He pointed to a customs mutual recognition arrangement that would give trusted traders faster clearances across participating ASEAN countries, and noted the Digital Economy Framework Agreement signed earlier this month. Singapore's interest in this agenda is not purely ideological. With AI-related exports driving the city-state to 6 per cent growth and non-oil domestic exports forecast to grow 30 to 32 per cent, Singapore has a direct commercial interest in maintaining open trade flows. The question is whether the external pressure from US tariffs will accelerate integration — by making the costs of fragmentation more visible — or slow it down, by creating bilateral pressures that individual countries prioritise over regional initiatives. Each ASEAN member faces different tariff proposals and different chip control implications. Whether they can respond collectively rather than individually will determine whether ASEAN's economic architecture is resilient enough for the trade environment that is now emerging.

主播：转向新加坡——副总理颜金勇在重要区域论坛上呼吁深化东盟经济一体化以应对不断上升的保护主义。颜金勇在11月27日新报业媒体亚洲未来峰会上发言，认为人工智能、地缘政治冲击以及华盛顿与北京之间的地缘经济竞争使东盟一体化尤为紧迫。升级版东盟货物贸易协定（ATIGA）定于2026年底推出。

分析师：这次演讲起到先行政策声明的作用。新加坡将在2027年——东盟成立60周年——担任轮值主席国，颜金勇在表明贸易一体化将是主席国议程的核心。背景是快速恶化的贸易环境。仅过去两周，美国就宣布了覆盖14个经济体包括多个东盟成员的301条款关税提议，并将AI芯片出口管制扩展到东南亚。对于一个将经济模式建立在开放贸易和出口导向增长基础上的地区，这代表根本性挑战。升级版ATIGA是最具体的可交付成果。2010年实施的原协定消除了99%的东盟内部贸易关税，但有效性被非关税壁垒、复杂的原产地规则和不一致的实施所削弱。升级解决了这些差距——如果有效推出，可以降低东盟内部贸易成本并通过使生产商更容易从集团内部采购投入来部分抵消外部关税上升。

主播：东盟能否真正足够快地一体化以应对美国关税？

分析师：这是2027年的决定性问题。东盟一体化历来进展缓慢——基于共识的决策和主权敏感性限制了步伐。颜金勇呼吁清晰可预测的规则、尊重法律和承诺、对不当行为采取坚决行动。他提到海关互认安排将为受信任贸易商提供更快通关，并提到本月早些时候签署的数字经济框架协定。新加坡对这一议程的兴趣并非纯粹意识形态的。AI相关出口推动城市国家增长至6%，非石油国内出口预计增长30%至32%，新加坡在维持开放贸易流方面有直接商业利益。问题在于美国关税带来的外部压力是否会加速一体化——通过使碎片化成本更加明显——还是减缓一体化，通过制造个别国家优先于区域倡议的双边压力。每个东盟成员面临不同的关税提议和不同的芯片管制影响。它们能否集体而非单独应对将决定东盟的经济架构是否足够应对正在形成的贸易环境。

Lead presenter: And finally to the Philippines — where the central bank has identified a structural weakness that underpins the country's broader economic challenges. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas has flagged weak export performance as the primary driver behind the widening balance of payments deficit. The BSP warns that external finances will remain under pressure through 2027, with both the BOP and current account deficits expected to widen as global trade headwinds intensify.

Analyst: This is a remarkable admission from the central bank. The BSP is essentially acknowledging that the Philippines' economic model — historically built on remittances, domestic consumption, and services — faces a fundamental challenge in an era when ASEAN's growth is being driven by manufacturing export competitiveness, particularly in the AI semiconductor supply chain. The Philippines returned to a dollar deficit after a period of surplus, marking the end of a streak of positive BOP positions. The country's merchandise trade deficit is structural, not cyclical — it imports far more goods than it exports, and the gap is widening as global supply chain reconfiguration benefits manufacturing-oriented ASEAN peers. Vietnam's exports rose 24.5 per cent through September. Malaysia's monthly trade hit record levels. Singapore's factory output surged 29.1 per cent. The Philippines has not participated meaningfully in any of these trends.

Lead presenter: How does this connect to the wider economic picture we have been reporting on?

Analyst: It is the structural underpinning of everything else. A widening BOP deficit puts structural downward pressure on the peso, which feeds import-driven inflation — particularly food and energy, which the Philippines imports extensively. This creates a vicious cycle: peso weakness drives inflation, forcing BSP to maintain high interest rates at 5.50 per cent, which suppresses domestic demand and growth — Q3 GDP at just 2.1 per cent — which further weakens business confidence and investment, which limits development of the export manufacturing base that could address the trade deficit. The Philippines is now the only major ASEAN economy simultaneously experiencing high inflation at 7.8 per cent, low growth at 2.1 per cent, a balance of payments deficit, and a stock market decline of 28 per cent year-to-date. The comparison with Vietnam is particularly sobering: two decades ago, the two countries had comparable GDP per capita. Vietnam's decision to prioritise manufacturing export development has produced 9.95 per cent Q3 growth and a massive trade surplus. The Philippines' different path has produced 2.1 per cent growth and widening deficits. The BSP has explicitly noted that the Philippines lacks the manufacturing export base that drives growth in Vietnam, Malaysia, and Thailand. Breaking the cycle requires structural reform — developing manufacturing exports, attracting production-oriented FDI, and improving infrastructure. These are multi-year transformations that cannot be achieved through monetary policy alone, and whether the Marcos administration can deliver them before the 2028 midterms is the question the markets are watching.
