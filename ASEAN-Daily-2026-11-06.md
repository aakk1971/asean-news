## OCBC’s nine-month profit reaches S$6.24bn; insurance weighs on the quarter

### 华侨银行前九个月净利达62.4亿新元，保险投资拖累单季表现

Singapore · 新加坡

Released 6 Nov 2026; reporting period July–September and January–September 2026. Singapore. Publication time not stated in the release.

2026年11月6日公布；涵盖第三季度及前九个月，新加坡。公告未注明发布时间。

**Verified reporting:** OCBC reported third-quarter net profit of S$2.04bn, up 3% from a year earlier but down 8% from the preceding quarter. Nine-month profit rose 10% to S$6.24bn. The bank attributed the quarterly decline to weaker investment performance at Great Eastern, offsetting record banking-operations profit. Its non-performing-loan ratio was 0.8%.

**已核实事实：** 华侨银行第三季净利20.4亿新元，同比增3%、环比降8%；前九个月净利同比增10%至62.4亿新元。银行称，大东方投资表现转弱，抵消银行业务创纪录盈利；不良贷款率为0.8%。

**Our analysis:** The headline combines two different trends: banking earnings held up while insurance investment results weakened. Businesses assessing regional credit conditions should look beyond group profit to lending, funding costs and asset quality.

**Unknowns & next:** Future insurance investment returns and the speed of asset and funding repricing remain uncertain. Watch subsequent margin and credit-quality disclosures.

原创分析：银行业务与保险投资表现分化，评估区域信贷环境还须观察融资成本和资产质量。下一步关注息差及信贷披露，保险投资回报仍有不确定性。

[OCBC · 3Q26 issuer results release](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2026/OCBC%203Q26%20Results%20Press%20Release.pdf)

## Indonesia’s reserves edge higher, with government borrowing among the drivers

### 印尼外汇储备小幅上升，政府外债提款是驱动因素之一

Indonesia · 印度尼西亚

Released 6 Nov 2026; stock measured at end-October 2026. Jakarta. Page displays 10:00 AM, without an explicit timezone.

2026年11月6日公布；数据时点为10月底，雅加达。页面显示上午10时，未明确时区。

**Verified reporting:** Bank Indonesia put end-October foreign reserves at US$146.8bn, against US$146.3bn in September. It cited government foreign-loan drawdowns and tax and service receipts, alongside debt repayments and rupiah stabilisation. Reserves covered 5.3 months of imports, or 5.2 months including government external-debt service.

**已核实事实：** 印尼央行公布，10月底外汇储备为1,468亿美元，9月底为1,463亿美元。央行列举政府外债提款、税收及服务收入等因素，同时提到偿债和印尼盾稳定措施；储备可覆盖5.3个月进口。

**Our analysis:** A larger reserve stock provides a buffer for external payments, but the funding mix matters. Borrowing-related inflows should be distinguished from recurring export receipts when assessing durability.

**Unknowns & next:** The release does not quantify each flow’s contribution. Watch later reserve, external-debt and balance-of-payments data before attributing the increase to stronger exports.

原创分析：储备增加有助应对外部支付，但借款流入与持续出口收入的性质不同。公告未量化各项贡献，下一步须对照外债及国际收支数据。

[Bank Indonesia · No.28/242/DKom](https://www.bi.go.id/id/publikasi/ruang-media/news-release/Pages/sp_2824226.aspx)

## New-home sales contract even as prices continue to rise slowly

### 印尼新房销售收缩，房价仍温和上涨

Indonesia · 印度尼西亚

Released 6 Nov 2026; survey period Q3 2026, Indonesia’s primary residential market. Page displays 11:00 AM; timezone not explicit.

2026年11月6日公布；调查涵盖第三季度印尼一手住宅市场。页面显示上午11时，未明确时区。

**Verified reporting:** Bank Indonesia’s survey showed primary-market residential prices rising 0.58% year on year in Q3, versus 0.69% in Q2. Sales fell 8.43% year on year, with declines across all home types. Internal funds supplied 73.94% of developers’ financing, while mortgages accounted for 70.68% of consumer purchase financing.

**已核实事实：** 印尼央行调查显示，第三季一手住宅价格同比升0.58%，低于第二季的0.69%；销售同比降8.43%，各类房屋均下滑。开发商融资中73.94%来自内部资金，消费者购房融资中70.68%为住房贷款。

**Our analysis:** Slow price growth can coexist with weak transactions. Developers’ cash collection and mortgage availability may be more useful near-term operating signals than the price index alone.

**Unknowns & next:** The release does not establish a single cause of weaker sales. Watch the full survey’s regional breakdown and subsequent mortgage and transaction data.

原创分析：价格微升并不代表交易活跃；开发商回款及按揭可得性值得关注。公告未确定销量下滑的单一原因，下一步观察地区差异和后续交易数据。

[Bank Indonesia · No.28/244/DKom](https://www.bi.go.id/id/publikasi/ruang-media/news-release/Pages/sp_2824426.aspx)

## Philippine farm and fisheries production value falls 5% in real terms

### 菲律宾农业及渔业实际产值同比下降5%

Philippines · 菲律宾

Released 6 Nov 2026; reference period July–September 2026, nationwide Philippines. Release time not provided.

2026年11月6日公布；统计期为7至9月，涵盖菲律宾全国，未提供发布时间。

**Verified reporting:** The Philippine Statistics Authority reported agriculture and fisheries production value of PHP389.42bn at constant 2018 prices in Q3, down 5.0% year on year. It attributed the decline to crops and fisheries; livestock and poultry production values increased.

**已核实事实：** 菲律宾统计局公布，按2018年不变价格计算，第三季农业及渔业产值为3,894.2亿比索，同比降5%。该局称，作物及渔业产值下降，畜牧和家禽产值则增加。

**Our analysis:** This is an inflation-adjusted production measure. It signals weaker real output value, but does not directly establish retail food inflation or farm profitability. Food processors and distributors should examine individual commodities and sourcing options.

**Unknowns & next:** The headline does not isolate weather, disease or price effects on individual businesses. Watch commodity-level tables, food prices and subsequent harvest data.

原创分析：这是剔除价格变动后的产值指标，不能直接等同零售食品涨价或农户利润变化。食品企业须查看分品类供给，下一步关注商品明细和收成数据。

[Philippine Statistics Authority · 2026-359](https://psa.gov.ph/content/value-production-philippine-agriculture-and-fisheries-declines-50-percent-third-quarter)

## Employment rises to 50.32 million; underemployment eases

### 菲律宾就业人数增至5,032万，就业不足率回落

Philippines · 菲律宾

Released 6 Nov 2026; survey reference month September 2026, nationwide Philippines. Publication time not supplied.

2026年11月6日公布；调查参考月份为9月，涵盖菲律宾全国，未提供发布时间。

**Verified reporting:** PSA reported 50.32 million employed people in September, compared with 49.79 million in August and 49.60 million a year earlier. Underemployment was 11.0%, against 11.9% in August and 11.1% a year earlier.

**已核实事实：** 菲律宾统计局公布，9月就业人数为5,032万，8月为4,979万，上年同期为4,960万。就业不足率为11.0%，低于8月的11.9%及上年同期的11.1%。

**Our analysis:** The combination points to more people working and a smaller share seeking additional hours. It does not, by itself, show stronger real wages or household purchasing power.

**Unknowns & next:** The causes of the month-to-month changes are not established by these headline figures. Watch hours worked, sector composition and subsequent survey revisions.

原创分析：就业增加、希望增加工时的占比下降，但不能据此判断实际工资或购买力上升。下一步观察工时、行业构成及数据修订。

[Philippine Statistics Authority · September labour-force release](https://psa.gov.ph/content/number-employed-persons-rose-5032-million-underemployment-declined-september-2026)
