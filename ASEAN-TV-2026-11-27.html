Danantara Reports US$634 Billion in Assets and Rp203.4 Trillion Net Profit in First Audited Consolidated Accounts

印尼主权基金Danantara首份经审计合并财报：资产6340亿美元，净利润203.4万亿印尼盾

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. We begin tonight in Indonesia where the Daya Anagata Nusantara sovereign wealth fund — Danantara — has released its first-ever audited consolidated financial statements, and the numbers are staggering. The fund reports total assets of US$634 billion and consolidated net profit of Rp203.4 trillion — approximately US$14 billion — for the 2025 financial year. These are the first accounts since the government transferred its stakes in major state-owned enterprises to Danantara in March 2025, giving the market its first transparent look at the fund's combined balance sheet.

Analyst: These numbers demand context. The US$634 billion asset figure makes Danantara one of the largest sovereign wealth entities in the world by reported assets — comparable in scale to Norway's Government Pension Fund or Abu Dhabi's ADIA. But the comparison requires caution. Unlike those funds, which hold diversified portfolios of global financial assets, Danantara's balance sheet is dominated by the consolidated assets of Indonesia's state-owned enterprises — Bank Mandiri, BRI, Pertamina, Telkom, PLN, and others. The assets include everything from bank loan books to oil refineries to telecommunications towers. The Rp203.4 trillion net profit is similarly a consolidation of SOE earnings rather than investment returns in the traditional sovereign fund sense. BRI and Bank Mandiri alone would account for a substantial share of that profit figure. What is genuinely significant is the act of publication itself. By releasing audited consolidated accounts, Danantara is establishing a transparency benchmark that its critics — and there have been many — argued it would never meet. The accounts were audited by an international firm, which provides a degree of independent verification.

Lead presenter: How has the market responded?

Analyst: With cautious optimism. The release of audited accounts addresses one of the core concerns that contributed to the Jakarta Composite's sharp decline when Danantara was first announced — the fear that the fund would operate as an opaque vehicle for politically directed investment without accountability. The fact that Danantara has published comprehensive financials within its first year of operation is ahead of market expectations. However, significant questions remain. First, the accounts consolidate entities with very different risk profiles — a commercial bank and a state electricity utility have fundamentally different balance sheet dynamics. Second, the market wants to see not just backward-looking accounts but forward-looking investment criteria: how will Danantara allocate capital across its portfolio companies, and what governance mechanisms prevent political interference in investment decisions? CEO Rosan Roeslani has stated that the fund will prioritise commercial returns, but the Rp162 trillion deployment announced earlier — across energy, food, and hajj infrastructure — suggests that national policy objectives will play a significant role in capital allocation. The critical test comes in the next twelve months: will Danantara's investment decisions consistently generate value, or will the fund face pressure to subsidise politically important but commercially marginal projects? For international investors considering Indonesia, these accounts are a necessary but not sufficient condition for confidence.

中文主播稿

主播：各位晚上好。今晚我们从印尼开始——主权财富基金Daya Anagata Nusantara（即Danantara）发布了首份经审计的合并财务报表，数据令人震撼。该基金报告总资产6,340亿美元，2025财年合并净利润为203.4万亿印尼盾——约140亿美元。这是政府于2025年3月将主要国企股份转入Danantara以来的首份财报，使市场首次透明地看到了基金的合并资产负债表。

分析师：这些数字需要放在背景下理解。6,340亿美元的资产规模使Danantara按报告资产计成为全球最大的主权财富实体之一——与挪威政府养老基金或阿布扎比ADIA的规模相当。但这种比较需要谨慎。与那些持有多元化全球金融资产组合的基金不同，Danantara的资产负债表主要由印尼国企的合并资产构成——曼迪利银行、BRI、Pertamina、Telkom、PLN等。资产涵盖从银行贷款账簿到炼油厂到电信铁塔的一切。203.4万亿印尼盾的净利润同样是国企盈利的合并数据，而非传统主权基金意义上的投资回报。仅BRI和曼迪利银行就可能占据利润数字的相当份额。真正重要的是发布本身。通过发布经审计的合并账目，Danantara正在建立一个透明度基准——其批评者——而且不乏其人——曾认为它永远无法达到。账目由国际会计师事务所审计，提供了一定程度的独立验证。

主播：市场反应如何？

分析师：谨慎乐观。经审计账目的发布回应了雅加达综合指数在Danantara首次宣布时急剧下跌的核心担忧之一——担心该基金将作为不透明的政治导向投资工具运作而没有问责。Danantara在运营首年内就发布了全面财务数据，超出了市场预期。然而，重大问题仍然存在。首先，账目合并了风险特征非常不同的实体——商业银行和国有电力公用事业具有根本不同的资产负债表动态。其次，市场希望看到的不仅是回顾性账目，还有前瞻性投资标准：Danantara将如何在旗下公司之间配置资本，以及什么治理机制防止政治干预投资决策？首席执行官罗桑·鲁斯拉尼表示基金将优先考虑商业回报，但此前宣布的162万亿印尼盾部署——涵盖能源、粮食和朝觐基础设施——表明国家政策目标将在资本配置中发挥重要作用。关键考验在未来十二个月：Danantara的投资决策是否能持续创造价值，还是基金将面临补贴政治上重要但商业上边际的项目的压力？对于考虑印尼的国际投资者来说，这些账目是信心的必要但非充分条件。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Malaysia — where the collision between US technology export controls and Southeast Asia's data centre boom is creating real-time policy dilemmas. Malaysia has issued directives requiring data centre operators to ensure they do not provide services to entities on the US blacklist — a compliance obligation that is forcing operators to scrutinise their customer base. Simultaneously, Nvidia has paused shipments of advanced AI servers to Malaysian facilities while it conducts its own compliance review. This comes even as approved data centre investments continue to surge, with service exports reaching RM11.7 billion in 2025 and approved investments rising 44.5 per cent in the first nine months of 2026.

Analyst: This story captures the central tension in Malaysia's AI-driven growth model. The country has positioned itself as ASEAN's leading data centre hub — Microsoft, Google, Oracle, Amazon Web Services, and ByteDance have all committed billions to Malaysian data centre construction. The appeal is clear: reliable power supply, land availability outside congested Singapore, a skilled technical workforce, and government incentives. But the US export control regime — which restricts the sale of advanced AI chips to certain countries and entities — creates a compliance layer that Malaysia cannot ignore. The directive to data centre operators is Malaysia's attempt to stay on the right side of US controls without alienating Chinese technology companies that are also significant investors. This is a delicate balancing act. ByteDance's US$2.1 billion investment in Johor is one of the largest single data centre commitments in ASEAN, and Chinese cloud operators are expanding across the region.

Lead presenter: What about Nvidia's pause?

Analyst: Nvidia's decision to pause server shipments is significant because it reveals how US corporate compliance decisions can have immediate operational consequences in third countries. Nvidia is under heightened scrutiny from the US Commerce Department following reports that its chips were reaching restricted end-users through intermediary countries, including Malaysia. The pause is precautionary — Nvidia is reviewing its Malaysian customers' end-user certifications before resuming shipments. For Malaysian data centre operators, the delay creates scheduling problems: construction timelines for hyperscale facilities are measured in months, and GPU server delivery is on the critical path. Any delay ripples through the entire project timeline. Bank Negara Malaysia has stated that the overall data centre investment pipeline remains strong, noting that the sector's contribution to service exports has tripled since 2023. The 44.5 per cent increase in approved investments in the first nine months of 2026 suggests that the structural demand for Southeast Asian compute capacity continues to grow despite the compliance friction. The key risk for Malaysia is reputational: if the country is perceived as a leakage point for restricted technology, the US could impose tighter controls that would make Malaysia a less attractive destination for the very investment it is trying to attract. The government's directive to operators is an effort to prevent precisely that outcome — demonstrating compliance proactively rather than waiting for enforcement action.

S&P and Deutsche Bank Cut Philippines 2026 Growth Forecast to 2.4%, Slowest in Southeast Asia

标普和德意志银行将菲律宾2026年增长预测下调至2.4%，东南亚最慢

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To the Philippines now — where the growth outlook continues to deteriorate. S&P Global Ratings has cut its 2026 GDP growth forecast for the Philippines to just 2.4 per cent, down from 4.0 per cent, making the country the slowest-growing major economy in Southeast Asia. Deutsche Bank has issued a similar downgrade. S&P also projects a modest recovery to 5.0 per cent in 2027, but that figure assumes inflation moderates and the central bank can begin easing — assumptions the market increasingly questions. Meanwhile, the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas signalled on November 25 that it may adopt a less aggressive tightening stance, suggesting the rate cycle may be approaching a peak.

Analyst: The 2.4 per cent forecast is a dramatic downgrade that positions the Philippines as a clear outlier in ASEAN. For perspective, Vietnam is growing at 9 per cent, Malaysia at around 5.5 per cent, Indonesia at approximately 5 per cent, and even Thailand — which has its own structural challenges — is projected at 2.7 per cent. What makes the Philippine situation distinctive is the combination of three mutually reinforcing headwinds. First, persistent supply-driven inflation — running above 7 per cent for most of 2026 — that is eroding real household incomes and consumer purchasing power. Second, an aggressive monetary tightening cycle that has lifted the policy rate but failed to bring inflation under control because the price pressures are supply-side: food costs, transport costs, energy costs. Third, weakening external demand as global trade uncertainty weighs on electronics exports, which account for approximately 55 per cent of Philippine merchandise exports.

Lead presenter: What is the significance of the BSP's signal about less aggressive tightening?

Analyst: The BSP's November 25 signal is a tacit acknowledgment that further rate hikes would do more harm than good. With GDP growth at 2.1 per cent in Q3 and inflation stubbornly supply-driven, additional rate increases would suppress demand without addressing the root cause of price pressures. Governor Remolona has been making this argument publicly — that monetary policy alone cannot solve a supply-side inflation problem. The signal suggests the BSP is preparing to pause its tightening cycle, holding the benchmark rate at the current level while waiting for supply-side interventions to take effect. For the peso, this is a double-edged development: a pause removes the support of higher rates at a time when the US Federal Reserve is maintaining elevated rates, potentially widening the yield differential and putting depreciation pressure on the currency. The Philippine economy faces a growth trap: too much tightening crushes the already fragile recovery, too little allows inflation to stay elevated and erodes competitiveness. S&P's 5.0 per cent forecast for 2027 requires a significant improvement in all three headwinds simultaneously — lower food inflation, stable energy costs, and recovering export demand. That is a scenario, not a baseline. The risk is that 2027 growth also disappoints, prolonging what is becoming the most challenging macroeconomic period for the Philippines since the 2008 global financial crisis.

中文主播稿

主播：转向菲律宾——增长前景继续恶化。标普全球评级已将菲律宾2026年GDP增长预测从4.0%下调至仅2.4%，使该国成为东南亚增长最慢的主要经济体。德意志银行也发布了类似的下调。标普同时预测2027年将温和恢复至5.0%，但该数字假设通胀放缓且央行可以开始宽松——市场对这些假设越来越质疑。与此同时，菲律宾央行11月25日暗示可能采取不那么激进的紧缩立场，表明加息周期可能接近顶峰。

分析师：2.4%的预测是一次戏剧性的下调，将菲律宾定位为东盟的明显异类。作为参照，越南增长9%，马来西亚约5.5%，印尼约5%，即使是面临自身结构性挑战的泰国也预计在2.7%。菲律宾局面的独特之处在于三个相互强化的逆风的组合。第一，持续的供给驱动型通胀——2026年大部分时间超过7%——正在侵蚀实际家庭收入和消费者购买力。第二，激进的货币紧缩周期提高了政策利率，但未能控制住通胀，因为价格压力来自供给端：食品成本、交通成本、能源成本。第三，全球贸易不确定性拖累电子产品出口需求走弱——电子产品占菲律宾商品出口约55%。

主播：BSP关于减少激进紧缩的信号有何意义？

分析师：BSP 11月25日的信号是对进一步加息弊大于利的默认。在第三季度GDP增长2.1%且通胀顽固地由供给驱动的情况下，额外加息将抑制需求而不能解决价格压力的根源。雷莫洛纳行长一直在公开提出这一论点——货币政策无法单独解决供给侧通胀问题。信号表明BSP正准备暂停紧缩周期，将基准利率维持在当前水平，同时等待供给侧干预措施生效。对比索来说，这是一把双刃剑：在美联储维持高利率之际暂停加息消除了较高利率的支撑，可能扩大收益率差异并对货币造成贬值压力。菲律宾经济面临增长陷阱：过度紧缩压垮本已脆弱的复苏，紧缩不足则通胀居高不下并侵蚀竞争力。标普对2027年5.0%的预测要求三个逆风同时显著改善——食品通胀下降、能源成本稳定以及出口需求恢复。这是一个情景，而非基准假设。风险在于2027年增长同样令人失望，延长自2008年全球金融危机以来菲律宾最具挑战性的宏观经济时期。

IMF完成2026年越南第四条磋商，九个月GDP增长9.01%之际警告过热风险

VIETNAM · ECONOMY / 越南 · 经济
SCRIPT READY · VIDEO NOT RENDERED

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Vietnam now — where the International Monetary Fund has just completed its 2026 Article IV consultation, delivering a report that acknowledges Vietnam's remarkable growth momentum while flagging significant overheating risks. The numbers speak for themselves: third-quarter GDP growth reached 9.95 per cent year-on-year, bringing the nine-month average to 9.01 per cent. The National Assembly has now formally approved an unprecedented 10 per cent GDP growth target for 2027 — a figure that would make Vietnam comfortably the fastest-growing major economy in Asia.

Analyst: The IMF's Article IV review is the most authoritative periodic health check for any economy, and this one lands at a pivotal moment. Vietnam is experiencing the strongest sustained growth in its modern economic history. The 9.95 per cent Q3 figure is extraordinary — it exceeds China's peak growth rates during its mid-2000s boom and puts Vietnam on a trajectory that few economies have sustained for more than a year or two. The growth is driven by several converging forces: the manufacturing export boom, particularly in electronics and semiconductors as supply chains continue to diversify away from China; a surge in foreign direct investment with registered FDI approaching US$55 billion through the first ten months; rapid expansion of the digital economy; and strong domestic consumption fuelled by rising urban incomes. The IMF's overheating warning focuses on credit growth, real estate prices, and labour market tightness. Bank credit is expanding at approximately 14 to 15 per cent year-on-year, and the real estate sector in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi is showing signs of speculative froth — apartment prices have doubled in some prime districts over the past three years.

Lead presenter: Is 10 per cent growth in 2027 achievable?

Analyst: The 10 per cent target is the most ambitious growth objective set by any significant economy in recent memory. The National Assembly approved it in October alongside a total social investment target of US$228 to 243 billion. Mathematically, it requires manufacturing output growth above 12 per cent, service sector growth of 8 to 9 per cent, and agriculture at around 4 per cent — all simultaneously. The question is whether Vietnam's infrastructure and institutional capacity can support growth at this pace without generating destabilising imbalances. The IMF has identified three specific risks. First, rapid credit growth could fuel asset price bubbles and weaken bank balance sheets if the cycle turns. Second, the labour market is tightening in industrial zones, with wage inflation beginning to erode the cost advantage that attracted manufacturing investment in the first place. Third, infrastructure bottlenecks — particularly in power supply, port capacity, and urban transport — are becoming binding constraints. Vietnam added approximately 4,000 megawatts of installed power capacity in 2025, but industrial demand is growing faster. The State Bank of Vietnam faces a delicate calibration challenge: tightening too early could choke the investment boom, but waiting too long risks allowing imbalances to build to dangerous levels. What the IMF is really signalling is that Vietnam should prioritise sustainable 7 to 8 per cent growth over spectacular 10 per cent growth — that the quality of growth matters more than the quantity at this stage of development.

中文主播稿

主播：转向越南——国际货币基金组织刚刚完成了2026年第四条磋商，发布了一份报告，在肯定越南显著增长势头的同时标记了重大过热风险。数据不言自明：第三季度GDP同比增长达9.95%，九个月平均增速为9.01%。国会已正式批准了史无前例的2027年GDP增长10%的目标——这一数字将使越南轻松成为亚洲增长最快的主要经济体。

分析师：IMF第四条审查是对任何经济体最权威的定期体检，而这一次恰逢关键时刻。越南正经历现代经济史上最强劲的持续增长。9.95%的第三季度数据非同寻常——它超过了中国在2000年代中期繁荣期间的峰值增长率，使越南走上了少有经济体能持续超过一两年的轨迹。增长由多股汇聚力量驱动：制造业出口繁荣，特别是电子和半导体领域，供应链持续从中国多元化；外商直接投资激增，前十个月注册外资接近550亿美元；数字经济快速扩张；以及城市收入增长带动的强劲国内消费。IMF的过热警告聚焦于信贷增长、房地产价格和劳动力市场紧缩。银行信贷同比扩张约14%至15%，胡志明市和河内的房地产行业显示投机泡沫迹象——部分黄金地段公寓价格在过去三年翻了一番。

主播：2027年10%的增长目标可以实现吗？

分析师：10%的目标是近年来任何重要经济体设定的最雄心勃勃的增长目标。国会于10月批准了该目标，同时设定了2,280亿至2,430亿美元的社会投资总额目标。从数学上看，这要求制造业产出增长超过12%、服务业增长8%至9%、农业增长约4%——同时实现。问题在于越南的基础设施和制度能力能否在不产生破坏稳定的失衡的情况下支撑这一速度的增长。IMF确定了三个具体风险。第一，快速信贷增长可能助长资产价格泡沫，如果周期转向将削弱银行资产负债表。第二，工业区劳动力市场正在趋紧，工资通胀开始侵蚀最初吸引制造业投资的成本优势。第三，基础设施瓶颈——特别是电力供应、港口能力和城市交通——正成为约束性制约。越南在2025年新增约4,000兆瓦装机容量，但工业需求增长更快。越南国家银行面临微妙的校准挑战：过早紧缩可能扼杀投资热潮，但等待太久有让失衡累积到危险水平的风险。IMF真正传达的信号是，越南应优先考虑可持续的7%至8%增长，而非壮观的10%增长——在这一发展阶段，增长质量比数量更重要。

Thailand Launches Crypto-to-Baht Payment Sandbox Allowing Foreign Tourists to Convert Digital Assets for Spending

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Thailand — where regulators are breaking new ground with a sandbox programme that allows foreign tourists to convert cryptocurrency holdings into Thai baht for spending. The Securities and Exchange Commission launched the pilot in partnership with licensed digital asset exchanges, enabling visitors to convert Bitcoin, Ethereum, and stablecoins into baht via QR code payment terminals at participating merchants. The programme is designed to capture tourist spending that might otherwise bypass the traditional banking system entirely.

Analyst: This is a genuinely innovative regulatory approach and it reflects Thailand's pragmatic stance toward digital assets — which stands in contrast to the outright bans or restrictive frameworks adopted by several other Asian regulators. The logic is straightforward: Thailand receives approximately 30 to 35 million foreign visitors annually, and a growing share of younger, digitally native travellers hold a portion of their wealth in cryptocurrency. Without a legal framework for crypto-to-fiat conversion, those visitors either avoid spending their crypto holdings in Thailand or use informal channels that generate no tax revenue and carry money-laundering risks. The sandbox creates a regulated pathway. Tourists can convert crypto at licensed exchanges, receive baht in a linked mobile wallet, and spend via QR code — the same infrastructure that underpins Thailand's already extensive PromptPay network. Merchants receive settlement in baht, eliminating their cryptocurrency exposure. The SEC has capped individual conversion amounts and requires full know-your-customer verification, addressing anti-money-laundering concerns.

Lead presenter: How significant is this for Thailand's tourism economy?

Analyst: The direct revenue impact in the near term is modest — cryptocurrency-spending tourists are a niche within a niche. But the strategic significance is larger. Thailand's tourism sector has been struggling: arrivals have not recovered to the pre-pandemic peak of 40 million, the 2026 target has been revised down to approximately 31.5 million, and spending per visitor has been declining as the mix shifts toward budget-conscious travellers. The crypto sandbox is part of a broader effort to position Thailand as a digital-asset-friendly destination that attracts higher-spending visitors — particularly from markets where crypto adoption is high, such as South Korea, Japan, and Russia. Beyond tourism, the sandbox has implications for Thailand's financial services sector. If the pilot succeeds, it could be expanded to cover domestic consumers, creating a regulated bridge between the crypto economy and the traditional financial system. Thailand already has one of the highest rates of cryptocurrency ownership in Asia — approximately 15 to 20 per cent of internet users hold some form of digital asset. A regulated conversion pathway could bring that activity into the formal economy. The risks are manageable within a sandbox framework: conversion caps limit exposure, KYC requirements address compliance, and the programme can be suspended if problems emerge. The more interesting question is whether this positions Thailand ahead of Singapore, which has taken a more cautious approach to retail crypto services, in the competition to become ASEAN's digital asset hub. Regulatory innovation is a form of competitive advantage, and Thailand is demonstrating willingness to move first.

中文主播稿

主播：转向泰国——监管机构正以沙盒项目开辟新领域，允许外国游客将加密货币持仓转换为泰铢用于消费。证券交易委员会与持牌数字资产交易所合作推出了试点项目，使游客能够通过参与商户的QR码支付终端将比特币、以太坊和稳定币转换为泰铢。该项目旨在捕获否则可能完全绕过传统银行系统的游客消费。

分析师：这是一种真正创新的监管方法，反映了泰国对数字资产的务实立场——与其他几个亚洲监管机构采取的全面禁令或限制性框架形成对比。逻辑很直接：泰国每年接待约3,000万至3,500万外国游客，越来越多的年轻数字原住民旅行者将部分财富以加密货币形式持有。如果没有加密货币兑法币转换的法律框架，这些游客要么避免在泰国花费其加密资产，要么使用不产生税收且存在洗钱风险的非正式渠道。沙盒创造了一个受监管的路径。游客可以在持牌交易所转换加密货币，在关联的移动钱包中接收泰铢，并通过QR码消费——与泰国已经广泛使用的PromptPay网络相同的基础设施。商户以泰铢结算，消除了其加密货币敞口。SEC设定了个人转换金额上限并要求完整的客户身份验证，以应对反洗钱关切。

主播：这对泰国旅游经济有多重要？

分析师：短期内直接收入影响不大——加密货币消费游客是小众中的小众。但战略意义更大。泰国旅游业一直在挣扎：入境人数未恢复到疫情前4,000万的峰值，2026年目标已下调至约3,150万，且随着旅客构成向预算意识更强的旅行者转移，人均消费一直在下降。加密沙盒是更广泛努力的一部分，旨在将泰国定位为数字资产友好目的地，吸引消费更高的游客——特别是来自加密货币采用率高的市场，如韩国、日本和俄罗斯。在旅游业之外，沙盒对泰国金融服务业也有影响。如果试点成功，可以扩展到覆盖国内消费者，在加密经济和传统金融体系之间创建受监管的桥梁。泰国已经拥有亚洲最高的加密货币持有率之一——约15%至20%的互联网用户持有某种形式的数字资产。受监管的转换路径可以将这些活动纳入正规经济。在沙盒框架内风险可控：转换上限限制了敞口，KYC要求解决了合规问题，如果出现问题可以暂停项目。更有趣的问题是，这是否使泰国在成为东盟数字资产中心的竞争中领先于对零售加密服务采取更谨慎态度的新加坡。监管创新是一种竞争优势，泰国正在展示率先行动的意愿。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: And finally — a story that captures the broader trend shaping ASEAN economies. S&P Global's latest purchasing managers survey shows that new orders across ASEAN's manufacturing sector have hit a record high, driven primarily by demand linked to artificial intelligence infrastructure. The ASEAN Manufacturing PMI continues to signal expansion, with the new orders sub-index reaching its highest reading since the survey began. Singapore's PMI remains in expansion territory, and the data suggests the AI-related manufacturing boom is broadening across the region.

Analyst: This is the clearest statistical evidence yet that the AI investment cycle is not just a Silicon Valley phenomenon — it is physically reshaping ASEAN manufacturing. The record new orders reading reflects a confluence of demand streams. First, direct AI hardware demand: servers, networking equipment, cooling systems, and power infrastructure for data centres across Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, and Thailand. The hyperscale buildout by Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and Oracle in the region is translating into orders for locally manufactured components and sub-assemblies. Second, semiconductor-related demand: advanced packaging, testing, and assembly operations in Malaysia and Singapore are running at or near capacity. Third, the downstream effect on traditional manufacturing: factories across ASEAN are investing in automation and AI-enabled quality control, generating demand for sensors, robotics, and industrial software.

Lead presenter: Is this sustainable given the global trade uncertainty?

Analyst: The sustainability question has two dimensions. On the demand side, the structural case is strong. Global capital expenditure on AI infrastructure is projected to exceed US$300 billion annually through 2028, and ASEAN is capturing an increasing share because it offers lower costs than Northeast Asia, political neutrality relative to the US-China rivalry, and an improving talent pool. The region's share of global data centre capacity has roughly doubled since 2022. Singapore serves as the financial and connectivity hub, Malaysia provides the scale and cost-effective land, Vietnam and Thailand are growing as secondary nodes, and Indonesia's large domestic market attracts investment for local deployment. On the risk side, US export controls remain the primary concern. As we discussed with the Malaysia story, any tightening of chip export restrictions could disrupt supply chains across the region. Additionally, a global AI investment slowdown — if the return on AI capital expenditure disappoints — could reduce orders across the board. The PMI data captures orders already placed, not future intentions, and the survey's forward-looking components show some moderation in expectations. Nevertheless, the structural reallocation of manufacturing from China to ASEAN continues regardless of the AI cycle, and the two trends — supply chain diversification and AI demand — are mutually reinforcing. For ASEAN as a whole, this manufacturing boom is the most positive macro development in a decade, providing a growth engine that benefits multiple countries simultaneously and creates a pathway to higher-value industrial activity.

中文主播稿

主播：最后——一个捕捉塑造东盟经济更广泛趋势的故事。标普全球最新的采购经理人调查显示，东盟制造业新订单创历史新高，主要由人工智能基础设施相关需求驱动。东盟制造业PMI继续发出扩张信号，新订单分项指数达到调查开始以来的最高读数。新加坡PMI保持在扩张区间，数据表明AI相关制造业繁荣正在整个地区扩展。

分析师：这是迄今为止最清晰的统计证据，表明AI投资周期不仅仅是硅谷现象——它正在物理性地重塑东盟制造业。创纪录的新订单读数反映了多股需求流的汇合。第一，直接AI硬件需求：马来西亚、新加坡、印尼和泰国各地数据中心的服务器、网络设备、冷却系统和电力基础设施。微软、谷歌、亚马逊和甲骨文在该地区的超大规模建设正转化为对本地制造的组件和子组件的订单。第二，半导体相关需求：马来西亚和新加坡的先进封装、测试和组装业务正在满负荷或接近满负荷运行。第三，对传统制造业的下游效应：东盟各地工厂正投资于自动化和AI驱动的质量控制，产生对传感器、机器人和工业软件的需求。

主播：鉴于全球贸易不确定性，这可持续吗？

分析师：可持续性问题有两个维度。在需求端，结构性论据强劲。全球AI基础设施资本支出预计到2028年将每年超过3,000亿美元，东盟正在获取越来越大的份额，因为它提供了比东北亚更低的成本、相对于中美竞争的政治中立性，以及不断改善的人才库。该地区在全球数据中心容量中的份额自2022年以来大约翻了一番。新加坡作为金融和连接枢纽，马来西亚提供规模和具有成本效益的土地，越南和泰国作为二级节点增长，印尼庞大的国内市场吸引本地部署投资。在风险方面，美国出口管制仍是主要关切。正如我们在马来西亚报道中讨论的，任何芯片出口限制的收紧都可能扰乱整个地区的供应链。此外，全球AI投资放缓——如果AI资本支出回报令人失望——可能全面减少订单。PMI数据捕捉的是已下达的订单，而非未来意向，调查的前瞻性成分显示预期有所放缓。尽管如此，从中国到东盟的制造业结构性重新配置无论AI周期如何都在继续，这两个趋势——供应链多元化和AI需求——相互强化。对东盟整体而言，这一制造业繁荣是十年来最积极的宏观发展，提供了同时惠及多个国家的增长引擎，并创造了通往更高价值工业活动的路径。
