English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. Our lead story tonight — Indonesia's sovereign wealth fund Danantara has released its first audited financial report, and the numbers are striking. The fund disclosed a total asset base of approximately US$634 billion and net profit of Rp259.6 trillion — that's US$14.2 billion — for the 2025 fiscal year. Revenue surged approximately 400 per cent compared to the aggregate pre-consolidation baseline of the constituent state-owned enterprises. CEO Rosan Roeslani presented the results at an investor briefing, stating the fund aims to establish itself as a world-class sovereign wealth fund benchmarked against Singapore's GIC and Temasek by 2028 to 2029.

Analyst: These are landmark numbers that require careful context. The US$634 billion asset base places Danantara among the world's largest sovereign wealth funds by assets under management — comparable to Abu Dhabi's ADIA and significantly larger than Malaysia's Khazanah or the Philippines' Maharlika Fund. But this comparison needs a critical caveat: Danantara's assets are overwhelmingly equity stakes in operating SOEs rather than liquid financial assets. The comparison with GIC or Temasek — which manage diversified global portfolios — is structurally different. The Rp259.6 trillion net profit is largely a consolidation of earnings the constituent SOEs were already generating. BRI alone reported net profit exceeding Rp60 trillion in 2025, and Bank Mandiri contributed a similar order of magnitude. The 400 per cent revenue surge is an artefact of consolidation accounting — Danantara is now reporting the combined revenues of entities that previously filed separately.

Lead presenter: So what's the real test of whether Danantara is adding value?

Analyst: The meaningful test is whether Danantara can generate returns above what the SOEs would have delivered independently — through better capital allocation, coordinated investment strategies, or operational improvements. The governance question is the central risk factor. Indonesia's history with state-managed investment vehicles includes both successes and costly failures. Danantara concentrates enormous economic power in a single entity with direct connections to the presidency. The 2028-2029 timeline to achieve world-class status requires not just financial performance but transparent governance, independent board oversight, and demonstrated resistance to political interference. International co-investors — pension funds, endowments, and other sovereign wealth funds — will be watching the governance framework as closely as the financial returns. The Jakarta Post has reported that transparency questions remain, with analysts noting the audit's scope, methodology, and the degree of independent oversight have not been fully disclosed. For the first nine months of 2026, Danantara reported net profit of Rp203.4 trillion, suggesting it is on track to exceed the full-year 2025 result — but the benchmark question remains: is this value creation or value consolidation?

中文主播稿

主播：各位晚上好。今晚头条——印尼主权财富基金达南塔拉发布了首份经审计财务报告，数据引人注目。该基金披露总资产规模约6,340亿美元，2025财年净利润259.6万亿印尼盾——约142亿美元。收入较合并前各组成国企的基线水平飙升约400%。首席执行官罗桑·鲁斯拉尼在投资者简报会上展示了业绩，表示该基金旨在2028至2029年建立与新加坡GIC和淡马锡对标的世界级主权财富基金。

分析师：这些里程碑式的数字需要仔细解读。6,340亿美元的资产规模使达南塔拉跻身全球最大的主权财富基金之列，与阿布扎比的ADIA相当，远大于马来西亚的国库控股或菲律宾的马哈利卡基金。但这种比较需要关键警示：达南塔拉的资产绝大多数是运营国企的股权而非流动金融资产。与管理多元化全球投资组合的GIC或淡马锡的比较存在结构性差异。259.6万亿印尼盾的净利润很大程度上是各组成国企本已产生的收益的合并。仅BRI一家2025年净利润就超过60万亿印尼盾，曼迪利银行贡献了类似量级。安塔拉引用的收入飙升400%是合并会计的产物——达南塔拉现在报告的是此前分别申报的实体的合并收入。

主播：那么判断达南塔拉是否在创造价值的真正标准是什么？

分析师：有意义的检验是达南塔拉能否产生超过国企独立运营时的回报——通过更好的资本配置、协调投资策略或运营改进。治理问题是核心风险因素。印尼国家管理投资载体的历史包括成功和昂贵的失败。达南塔拉将巨大的经济权力集中在与总统直接关联的单一实体中。2028-2029年达到世界级地位的时间表不仅需要财务业绩，还需要透明治理、独立董事会监督和证明抵御政治干预的能力。国际联合投资者将像关注财务回报一样密切关注治理框架。雅加达邮报报道透明度问题仍然存在，分析师指出审计的范围、方法论和独立监督程度尚未充分披露。2026年前九个月，达南塔拉报告净利润203.4万亿印尼盾，表明有望超过2025年全年业绩——但基准问题仍在：这是价值创造还是价值合并？

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Malaysia now — where a major disruption is threatening the country's data centre ambitions. Nvidia has halted shipments of AI server systems to Malaysia and other Southeast Asian countries following expanded US export controls on advanced AI chips. The suspension affects complete server systems containing Nvidia's H200 and B200 series accelerators, which now require individual export licences. The expanded controls, previously focused on China, now cover ASEAN destinations including Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, and Indonesia.

Analyst: The timing of this is devastating for Malaysia. The country has spent two years positioning itself as a global data centre hub, attracting commitments from Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Nvidia itself, and ByteDance. Malaysia has approved over 117 data centre projects with combined investment exceeding US$50 billion, many dependent on Nvidia's latest AI accelerators. And just days ago, the October trade data showed Malaysia smashing its trade record — driven overwhelmingly by E&E exports. The core tension is that Malaysia's data centre proposition was built on two pillars that are now in conflict: cost-competitive access to advanced chips, and openness to investment from all sources including Chinese technology companies. The US controls are explicitly designed to prevent Chinese entities from accessing advanced AI computing through third-country data centres.

Lead presenter: What is the Malaysian government doing about this?

Analyst: Investment and Trade Minister Tengku Zafrul has confirmed the government is in active discussions with US counterparts to negotiate a framework allowing continued shipments to verified commercial operators while addressing US national security concerns. The practical impact depends entirely on how the licensing regime is administered. If individual validated licences are granted efficiently for verified commercial operators — hyperscale cloud providers, sovereign AI projects — the disruption may be manageable. If the process is slow or politically contingent, the competitive damage could be lasting. Share prices of data centre-related companies have already fallen sharply. The Edge reports several planned investments are pausing pending licensing clarity. The comparison with Malaysia's semiconductor position is instructive: testing and packaging operations are less affected because they handle chips after manufacture, while data centres consume the chips the controls target. The same AI supply chain driving Malaysia's record trade is now bifurcated — manufacturing booms, but deployment faces a regulatory chokepoint controlled by Washington. Industry analysts warn the controls could redirect investment toward US-allied destinations with pre-approved chip access — Japan, South Korea, and Australia.

中文主播稿

主播：转向马来西亚——一场重大干扰正威胁该国的数据中心雄心。在美国扩大对先进AI芯片出口管制后，英伟达已暂停向马来西亚和其他东南亚国家发运AI服务器系统。暂停影响包含英伟达H200和B200系列加速器的完整服务器系统，这些产品现需要单独的出口许可证。此前主要针对中国的扩大管制，现在覆盖包括马来西亚、泰国、越南和印尼在内的东盟目的地。

分析师：对马来西亚而言时机极为不利。该国花了两年时间将自己定位为全球数据中心枢纽，吸引了微软、谷歌、亚马逊、英伟达本身和字节跳动的投资承诺。马来西亚已批准超过117个数据中心项目，合计投资超过500亿美元，许多依赖英伟达最新AI加速器。就在数天前，10月贸易数据显示马来西亚打破贸易纪录——主要由电子电气出口驱动。核心矛盾在于马来西亚的数据中心价值主张建立在两个现在相互冲突的支柱上：具有成本竞争力的先进芯片获取渠道，以及对包括中国科技公司在内的所有来源投资的开放性。美国管制明确旨在防止中国实体通过第三国数据中心获取先进AI计算能力。

主播：马来西亚政府正在采取什么措施？

分析师：投资贸易部长东姑扎弗鲁确认政府正与美国对等方积极讨论，以谈判一个框架，允许向经验证的商业运营商继续发货，同时解决美国国家安全担忧。实际影响完全取决于许可制度的执行方式。如果为经验证的商业运营商高效授予单独验证许可证，干扰可能可控。如果流程缓慢或受政治因素影响，竞争损害可能是持久的。数据中心相关公司股价已大幅下跌。The Edge报道数个计划投资正暂停等待许可明确。与马来西亚半导体地位的比较具有启示性：测试和封装业务受影响较小，因为它们在制造后处理芯片，而数据中心消耗管制所针对的芯片。推动马来西亚创纪录贸易的同一AI供应链现在被分割——制造环节繁荣，但部署环节面临华盛顿控制的监管瓶颈。行业分析师警告管制可能将投资转向具有预先批准芯片获取渠道的美国盟友目的地——日本、韩国和澳大利亚。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To the Philippines now — where the economic outlook has deteriorated significantly. S&P Global Ratings has slashed its growth forecast to 2.4 per cent for 2026, down from 4.1 per cent set in June, with the downgrade extending through 2028. Deutsche Bank joined the downgrade wave on 24 November, warning that the country faces prolonged stagflation. Third-quarter GDP came in at just 2.1 per cent year-on-year — the weakest since the pandemic recovery — while October inflation remained elevated at 7.8 per cent.

Analyst: The twin downgrades from S&P and Deutsche Bank within 48 hours of each other send a powerful signal. When two of the most influential providers of economic forecasts independently reach the same conclusion, it reflects a consensus that the data has moved decisively against the country. The extension through 2028 is the most concerning element — this is not a cyclical dip forecast; it is a projection of structural underperformance for at least two more years. The comparison with ASEAN peers has become stark. Malaysia is growing at 5.5 per cent with 1.9 per cent inflation. Vietnam posted 9.95 per cent growth in Q3. Singapore is at 6 per cent. The Philippines is the only major ASEAN economy simultaneously experiencing high inflation and low growth — the textbook definition of stagflation.

Lead presenter: Can monetary policy fix this?

Analyst: That is precisely the problem — it cannot. The BSP has raised rates by 125 basis points in 2026, bringing the benchmark to 5.50 per cent, yet inflation keeps accelerating. This confirms the inflation is primarily supply-driven: rice prices are up 20.7 per cent, transport costs are surging from fuel pass-throughs, and typhoon damage has disrupted agricultural supply chains. Rate hikes cannot increase rice production or repair typhoon-damaged infrastructure. Meanwhile, those same rate hikes are suppressing the demand side — consumer spending is weakening, business investment is contracting, and the PSEi has fallen 28 per cent year-to-date. The Philippines is caught in a classic stagflation trap. S&P cited weakening government spending, persistent supply-side inflation, and tightening financial conditions as the primary drivers. BSP Governor Remolona has signalled rate hikes may moderate, acknowledging that monetary policy has limited further capacity to address what is fundamentally a supply-side problem. The risk is that slowing hikes before inflation convincingly declines could unanchor inflation expectations. The World Bank and IMF have emphasised that structural reform in agriculture — not monetary tightening — is the solution. Whether the Marcos administration can deliver those reforms before the 2028 midterms is the central question.

中文主播稿

主播：转向菲律宾——经济前景显著恶化。标普全球评级已将2026年增长预测从6月设定的4.1%大幅下调至2.4%，降级延伸至2028年。德意志银行于11月24日加入降级浪潮，警告该国面临长期滞胀。第三季度GDP同比仅增2.1%——疫情复苏以来最弱——同时10月通胀高企于7.8%。

分析师：标普和德意志银行在48小时内的双重下调发出了强烈信号。当两个最具影响力的经济预测提供者独立得出相同结论时，反映了数据已决定性地不利于该国的共识。延伸至2028年是最令人担忧的因素——这不是周期性下降预测，而是至少两年结构性表现不佳的预判。与东盟同行的比较已经变得鲜明。马来西亚以1.9%通胀增长5.5%。越南第三季度增长9.95%。新加坡增长6%。菲律宾是唯一同时经历高通胀和低增长的主要东盟经济体——教科书定义的滞胀。

主播：货币政策能解决这个问题吗？

分析师：问题恰恰在于——不能。BSP在2026年已加息125个基点至5.50%，但通胀持续加速。这确认通胀主要是供给驱动的：大米价格上涨20.7%，交通成本因燃料传导飙升，台风破坏了农业供应链。加息无法增加大米产量或修复基础设施。同时，加息正在抑制需求端——消费支出走弱、商业投资收缩、PSEi年初至今下跌28%。菲律宾陷入经典滞胀陷阱。标普将政府支出减弱、持续的供给侧通胀和金融条件收紧列为主要驱动因素。BSP行长雷莫洛纳暗示加息步伐可能放缓，承认货币政策在解决本质上是供给侧问题方面能力有限。风险在于在通胀令人信服地走上下降通道之前放慢加息可能导致通胀预期脱锚。世界银行和IMF强调农业结构性改革——而非货币紧缩——才是解决方案。马科斯政府能否在2028年中期选举前推动改革是核心问题。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Vietnam now — where the economy continues to post the fastest growth rates in ASEAN. The IMF has just completed its 2026 Article IV consultation mission against the backdrop of exceptional numbers: nine-month GDP growth of 9.01 per cent and third-quarter GDP growth of 9.95 per cent — the fastest quarterly expansion since the COVID recovery. Exports rose 24.5 per cent year-on-year through September, led by electronics and AI-related equipment.

Analyst: Vietnam's 9.95 per cent Q3 growth is an exceptional result by any standard — the fastest in four years. The nine-month average of 9.01 per cent shows this is not a one-quarter anomaly but a sustained acceleration driven by structural factors: China-plus-one supply chain diversification, the global AI investment cycle, and Vietnam's cost competitiveness. The manufacturing sector is the engine — double-digit expansion driven by surging exports in electronics, semiconductors, and AI-related equipment. The IMF's Article IV arrives at a critical juncture. The Fund's traditional concern is whether rapid growth is building imbalances. Several indicators warrant monitoring: inflation at 4.52 per cent is approaching the government's 4.5 per cent ceiling. Credit growth at 14 to 15 per cent — roughly 1.5 times nominal GDP growth — raises banking risk questions. Early signs of price acceleration are appearing in industrial zones near major FDI projects.

Lead presenter: How does this connect to the 10 per cent growth target for 2027?

Analyst: This is the direct link. The National Assembly's unprecedented 10 per cent target for 2027 would be historically unprecedented for Vietnam and requires manufacturing expansion, FDI disbursement acceleration, and robust domestic consumption all firing simultaneously. The State Bank of Vietnam has maintained a relatively accommodative stance with the refinancing rate at 4.5 per cent, supporting credit growth. The IMF's assessment of overheating risks will be closely watched — if the Fund flags significant concerns, it could influence the central bank's monetary stance and make the 10 per cent target harder to achieve. Vietnam's challenge is managing the transition from successful growth acceleration to sustainable growth. The infrastructure, financial system, and institutional capacity must keep pace with an economy expanding at nearly 10 per cent. Registered FDI through October hit US$54.85 billion, surging 74 per cent — the highest since 2008 — giving Vietnam a substantial project pipeline entering 2027. But the gap between registered and disbursed FDI — conversion historically runs at 35 to 40 per cent — means absorption capacity will be tested as never before.

中文主播稿

主播：转向越南——该国经济继续录得东盟最快增长率。IMF刚刚完成2026年第四条磋商任务，背景是exceptional的数据：前九月GDP增长9.01%，第三季度GDP增长9.95%——新冠复苏以来最快的季度扩张。截至9月出口同比增长24.5%，电子和AI相关设备领先。

分析师：越南第三季度9.95%的增长以任何标准衡量都是exceptional的结果——四年来最快。九个月9.01%的平均值表明这不是单一季度的异常，而是由结构性因素驱动的持续加速：中国加一供应链多元化、全球AI投资周期和越南成本竞争力。制造业是引擎——电子、半导体和AI相关设备出口激增推动两位数扩张。IMF的第四条磋商正值关键节点。基金组织的传统关注是快速增长是否正在积累失衡。几个指标值得监测：通胀4.52%正逼近政府4.5%的上限。14%至15%的信贷增长——约为名义GDP增长的1.5倍——引发银行风险问题。主要FDI项目附近的工业区出现价格加速的早期迹象。

主播：这与2027年10%的增长目标有何关联？

分析师：这是直接联系。国会史无前例的2027年10%目标对越南来说将是历史前所未有的，需要制造业扩张、FDI到位加速和国内消费稳健增长同时发力。越南国家银行维持相对宽松的立场，再融资利率4.5%，支持信贷增长。IMF对过热风险的评估将受到密切关注——如果标记重大担忧，可能影响央行货币立场，使10%目标更难实现。越南的挑战是管理从成功的增长加速到可持续增长的过渡。基础设施、金融体系和制度能力须跟上接近10%年增长率扩张的经济。截至10月注册外资达548.5亿美元，飙升74%——2008年以来最高——为越南进入2027年提供了大量项目储备。但注册到到位外资的差距——转化率历来约35%至40%——意味着吸收能力将面临前所未有的考验。

Thailand Launches TouristDigiPay Sandbox Allowing Foreign Visitors to Spend Stablecoins as Baht via QR Codes

泰国推出TouristDigiPay沙盒，允许外国游客通过二维码将稳定币兑换泰铢消费

ASEAN BUSINESS BRIEF
THAILAND · FINTECH / 泰国 · 金融科技
SCRIPT READY · VIDEO NOT RENDERED
稿件已就绪 · 视频未生成

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Thailand now — where a novel regulatory experiment is bridging cryptocurrency and mainstream retail payments. Thailand's SEC has launched the TouristDigiPay sandbox, allowing foreign tourists to convert USDT stablecoins into Thai baht for retail spending through QR code payments. The first approved app, WORPT, enables tourists to load stablecoins and spend at participating merchants through Thailand's PromptPay infrastructure. Merchants receive settlement in baht, eliminating crypto volatility risk on their end.

Analyst: This is a genuinely innovative regulatory approach. The design is clever in several respects. By using Thailand's existing PromptPay QR infrastructure, the sandbox avoids building parallel crypto payment rails — merchants receive baht through familiar channels, lowering adoption barriers to near zero on the retail side. By restricting the service to foreign tourists with passport verification, the SEC limits domestic regulatory complexity and frames the initiative as tourism promotion rather than broad crypto deregulation. The choice of USDT is pragmatic — it is the most widely held stablecoin globally. However, it exposes the system to Tether's specific risks, including ongoing questions about reserve backing. The sandbox establishes strict parameters: only foreign passport holders, only major-currency stablecoins, and full AML and KYC requirements on all transactions.

Lead presenter: Will this help Thailand's struggling tourism sector?

Analyst: The tourism connection is direct but modest.
