ENGLISH

Good afternoon. This is ASEAN Business Brief for November sixth.

In Singapore, OCBC reports quarterly profit of two point zero four billion Singapore dollars. That is up three percent from a year earlier, but down eight percent from the previous quarter. The bank points to weaker investment performance at Great Eastern. [Report: ocbc]

Indonesia’s foreign reserves edged up to one hundred and forty-six point eight billion US dollars at the end of October. Government foreign borrowing was among the drivers. [Report: reserves]

But the housing picture was softer. Bank Indonesia says new-home sales fell eight point four three percent from a year earlier, while prices rose only slightly. [Report: housing]

In the Philippines, agriculture and fisheries production value fell five percent in real terms in the third quarter. Separately, September employment reached fifty point three two million, with underemployment easing. These reports measure different parts of the economy. [Reports: agriculture, jobs]

简体中文

下午好，欢迎收看《东盟商业简报》，今天是十一月六日。

首先关注新加坡。华侨银行第三季度净利为二十点四亿新元，同比增加百分之三，环比下降百分之八。银行表示，大东方投资表现转弱，拖累了集团单季业绩。[对应报道：ocbc]

在印度尼西亚，十月底外汇储备小幅升至一千四百六十八亿美元。政府外债提款是推动因素之一。[对应报道：reserves]

不过，住宅市场交易偏弱。印尼央行调查显示，新房销售同比下降百分之八点四三，房价仅温和上涨。[对应报道：housing]

再看菲律宾。第三季度农业及渔业实际产值同比下降百分之五。另一份报告显示，九月就业人数达到五千零三十二万，就业不足率回落。两份数据反映经济的不同方面。[对应报道：agriculture、jobs]

本台分析：观察企业和经济，除了盈利与价格，还要看现金流、实际生产和就业质量。完整报道列明依据及仍待核实的问题。以上为精选简报，各国覆盖情况请参阅报纸版。
