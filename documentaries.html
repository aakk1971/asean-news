The routes behind ASEAN trade

东盟贸易背后的物流路线

A distribution-centre opening is a starting point for examining how ports, roads and warehouses connect businesses.

从配送中心开幕出发，探讨港口、道路与仓储如何连接企业。

The central question / 核心问题

What evidence would show that a new hub improves delivery or creates local business?

PORTS → ROADS → HUB → BUSINESS

What the existing report says / 现有报道内容

Malaysia’s Prime Minister’s Office published Anwar Ibrahim’s speech at the opening of Daiso’s global distribution centre in Pulau Indah. The speech described it as Daiso’s largest worldwide and linked the choice to Malaysia’s ports, roads and logistics capabilities. These scale and rationale claims are attributed to the speech.

马来西亚首相署发布安华出席大创全球配送中心开幕活动的讲稿。讲稿将英达岛设施称为大创全球最大配送中心，并提及港口、道路及物流能力；这些规模及选址说法均归属于讲稿。

Evidence / 证据

Event: 5 Nov 2026, 11:00 AM, Pulau Indah, Selangor. PMO transcript dated 5 Nov; publication time unknown. Event outside this edition’s 24-hour window. 活动：2026年11月5日上午11时，雪兰莪州英达岛。首相署讲稿日期为11月5日，发布时刻不详；活动已超出本期24小时窗口。

Official evidence / 官方依据：Malaysia Prime Minister’s Office · opening speech

Proposed chapters / 章节安排

01 · Follow the goods / 跟随货物

Ports → roads → distribution hub → businesses / 港口 → 道路 → 配送中心 → 企业。Conceptual route, not measured cargo flows.

02 · Read the evidence / 核查证据

Distinguish statements made at an opening from independently verified operating results. / 区分开幕致辞与经独立核实的运营成果。

03 · Measure the outcome / 衡量结果

Seek throughput, delivery-time, procurement and employment records before claiming benefits. / 核实吞吐量、时效、采购及就业记录。

Before filming / 拍摄前待办

Original location footage, company operating data, logistics interviews, image and footage permissions.

When prices rise but buyers retreat

当房价上涨，买家却退场

A data-led examination of the difference between housing prices, sales and access to finance.

以数据解释房价、销量与融资可得性之间的区别。

The central question / 核心问题

Why can a positive price index coexist with shrinking sales?

PRICE +0.58% YoY | SALES −8.43% YoY

What the existing report says / 现有报道内容

Bank Indonesia’s survey showed primary-market residential prices rising 0.58% year on year in Q3, versus 0.69% in Q2. Sales fell 8.43% year on year, with declines across all home types. Internal funds supplied 73.94% of developers’ financing, while mortgages accounted for 70.68% of consumer purchase financing.

印尼央行调查显示，第三季一手住宅价格同比升0.58%，低于第二季的0.69%；销售同比降8.43%，各类房屋均下滑。开发商融资中73.94%来自内部资金，消费者购房融资中70.68%为住房贷款。

Evidence / 证据

Released 6 Nov 2026; survey period Q3 2026, Indonesia’s primary residential market. Page displays 11:00 AM; timezone not explicit. 2026年11月6日公布；调查涵盖第三季度印尼一手住宅市场。页面显示上午11时，未明确时区。

Official evidence / 官方依据：Bank Indonesia · No.28/244/DKom

Proposed chapters / 章节安排

01 · Separate the measures / 区分指标

Price growth and sales growth are different series. Do not plot them as one trend. / 房价涨幅与销量变化并非同一指标。

02 · Examine financing / 观察融资

Investigate developer cash collection and mortgage availability. / 进一步调查开发商回款及按揭可得性。

03 · Compare locations / 比较地区

Regional data and interviews are needed before explaining the causes. / 解释成因前须有地区数据与采访支持。

Before filming / 拍摄前待办

Full regional survey tables, buyer and developer interviews, housing footage and financing context.

Interviewees and footage have not been secured. No runtime or publication date has been confirmed. / 尚未落实受访者与影像素材，时长及发布日期未定。

Inside the earnings headline

拆解银行盈利标题

An explainer on how banking operations and insurance investment results contribute to a group result.

解释银行业务与保险投资表现如何影响集团业绩。

The central question / 核心问题

What does a quarterly earnings headline reveal, and what does it leave unanswered?

Q3 NET PROFIT S$2.04bn | 9M S$6.24bn

What the existing report says / 现有报道内容

OCBC reported third-quarter net profit of S$2.04bn, up 3% from a year earlier but down 8% from the preceding quarter. Nine-month profit rose 10% to S$6.24bn. The bank attributed the quarterly decline to weaker investment performance at Great Eastern, offsetting record banking-operations profit. Its non-performing-loan ratio was 0.8%.

华侨银行第三季净利20.4亿新元，同比增3%、环比降8%；前九个月净利同比增10%至62.4亿新元。银行称，大东方投资表现转弱，抵消银行业务创纪录盈利；不良贷款率为0.8%。

Evidence / 证据

Released 6 Nov 2026; reporting period July–September and January–September 2026. Singapore. Publication time not stated in the release. 2026年11月6日公布；涵盖第三季度及前九个月，新加坡。公告未注明发布时间。

Official evidence / 官方依据：OCBC · 3Q26 issuer results release

Proposed chapters / 章节安排

01 · Check the period / 对齐期间

Quarterly and nine-month figures refer to different windows. / 单季与前九个月涵盖不同期间。

02 · Separate businesses / 区分业务

Compare operating performance with investment-return effects using segment disclosures. / 对照分部披露区分运营与投资回报。

03 · Follow the cash / 追踪现金流

Examine margins, funding costs and credit quality before drawing broader conclusions. / 进一步观察息差、融资成本及信贷质量。

Before filming / 拍摄前待办

Segment accounts, attributable expert commentary and original finance-sector visuals.
