English presenter script

OCBC reported third-quarter net profit of S$2.04bn, up 3% from a year earlier but down 8% from the preceding quarter. Nine-month profit rose 10% to S$6.24bn. The bank attributed the quarterly decline to weaker investment performance at Great Eastern, offsetting record banking-operations profit. Its non-performing-loan ratio was 0.8%.

Our analysis: The headline combines two different trends: banking earnings held up while insurance investment results weakened. Businesses assessing regional credit conditions should look beyond group profit to lending, funding costs and asset quality.

What comes next: Future insurance investment returns and the speed of asset and funding repricing remain uncertain. Watch subsequent margin and credit-quality disclosures.

English presenter script

Bank Indonesia put end-October foreign reserves at US$146.8bn, against US$146.3bn in September. It cited government foreign-loan drawdowns and tax and service receipts, alongside debt repayments and rupiah stabilisation. Reserves covered 5.3 months of imports, or 5.2 months including government external-debt service.

Our analysis: A larger reserve stock provides a buffer for external payments, but the funding mix matters. Borrowing-related inflows should be distinguished from recurring export receipts when assessing durability.

What comes next: The release does not quantify each flow’s contribution. Watch later reserve, external-debt and balance-of-payments data before attributing the increase to stronger exports.

English presenter script

Bank Indonesia’s survey showed primary-market residential prices rising 0.58% year on year in Q3, versus 0.69% in Q2. Sales fell 8.43% year on year, with declines across all home types. Internal funds supplied 73.94% of developers’ financing, while mortgages accounted for 70.68% of consumer purchase financing.

Our analysis: Slow price growth can coexist with weak transactions. Developers’ cash collection and mortgage availability may be more useful near-term operating signals than the price index alone.

What comes next: The release does not establish a single cause of weaker sales. Watch the full survey’s regional breakdown and subsequent mortgage and transaction data.

English presenter script

The Philippine Statistics Authority reported agriculture and fisheries production value of PHP389.42bn at constant 2018 prices in Q3, down 5.0% year on year. It attributed the decline to crops and fisheries; livestock and poultry production values increased.

Our analysis: This is an inflation-adjusted production measure. It signals weaker real output value, but does not directly establish retail food inflation or farm profitability. Food processors and distributors should examine individual commodities and sourcing options.

What comes next: The headline does not isolate weather, disease or price effects on individual businesses. Watch commodity-level tables, food prices and subsequent harvest data.
