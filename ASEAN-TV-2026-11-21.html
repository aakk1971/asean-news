Indonesia and EU Sign Landmark CEPA in Brussels, Eliminating 96% of Tariffs on €28.9 Billion Trade

印尼与欧盟在布鲁塞尔签署里程碑式CEPA，消除289亿欧元贸易96%的关税

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. Our lead story tonight — a landmark moment for EU-ASEAN trade relations. Indonesia and the European Union are signing the Indonesia-EU Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement in Brussels today, 21 November. It is the EU's first comprehensive trade deal with an ASEAN member since the Vietnam agreement entered into force in 2020.

Analyst: This agreement has been nine years in the making — negotiations started in July 2016 and concluded on 23 September 2025. The scale is significant: it eliminates 96 per cent of Indonesian tariffs within five years, reaching 98.5 per cent when fully implemented. Eighty per cent of tariffs are liberalised at entry into force, delivering estimated savings of approximately €600 million annually in customs duties. Bilateral goods trade stood at €28.9 billion in 2025, with services trade at €9.3 billion and EU foreign direct investment stock in Indonesia at €25.1 billion. Key provisions include the removal of Indonesian tariffs on EU motor vehicles — currently as high as 50 per cent — within five years, and Indonesia must align its vehicle regulations with UNECE standards by 2033. The agreement also protects 221 EU geographical indications in Indonesia and 72 Indonesian geographical indications in the EU.

Lead presenter: Palm oil was the most contentious issue. How was it resolved?

Analyst: Through a creative compromise. Rather than the EU imposing unilateral sustainability standards — which Indonesia saw as protectionism targeting its most important agricultural export — the agreement establishes a Protocol on Palm Oil that creates a bilateral cooperation framework and dialogue platform for sustainable palm oil. Rice, sugar, and ethanol are excluded from tariff liberalisation entirely. The timing is critical for Indonesia: it is set to lose EU Generalised Scheme of Preferences tariff benefits on 1 January 2027. Without the CEPA, Indonesian exports would face higher tariffs at precisely the moment the country needs stable market access to offset headwinds from the MSCI reclassification and rupiah weakness. For ASEAN more broadly, a successful IEU-CEPA could catalyse EU trade negotiations with Thailand, the Philippines, and Malaysia — all of which have suspended or ongoing EU trade talks.

中文主播稿

主播：各位晚上好。今晚头条——欧盟与东盟贸易关系的里程碑时刻。印尼和欧盟今日11月21日在布鲁塞尔签署《印尼-欧盟全面经济伙伴关系协定》。这是自2020年越欧自贸协定生效以来，欧盟与东盟成员国达成的首个全面贸易协定。

分析师：这项协议历时九年——谈判自2016年7月启动，2025年9月23日完成。规模令人瞩目：五年内消除96%的印尼关税，完全实施后覆盖98.5%的关税税目。80%的关税在协议生效时即自由化，预计每年为企业节省约6亿欧元的关税成本。2025年双边货物贸易额为289亿欧元，服务贸易93亿欧元，欧盟在印尼的FDI存量为251亿欧元。关键条款包括五年内取消印尼对欧盟汽车的关税——目前高达50%——以及印尼须在2033年前将车辆法规与UNECE标准对齐。协议还保护221个欧盟地理标志和72个印尼地理标志。

主播：棕榈油是最具争议的问题。如何解决的？

分析师：通过创造性的妥协。欧盟没有强加单方面可持续发展标准——印尼视之为针对其最重要农产品出口的保护主义——而是建立了《棕榈油议定书》，创建了可持续棕榈油的双边合作框架和对话平台。大米、糖和乙醇被完全排除在关税自由化之外。对印尼来说时机至关重要：其GSP优惠将于2027年1月1日到期。没有CEPA，印尼对欧盟出口将面临更高关税，而此时该国正需要稳定的市场准入来抵消MSCI重新分类和卢比贬值带来的逆风。对更广泛的东盟而言，IEU-CEPA的成功可能催化欧盟与泰国、菲律宾和马来西亚的贸易谈判——它们都有暂停或正在进行的欧盟贸易谈判。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Singapore has posted trade numbers that are extraordinary even by this year's elevated standards. Non-oil domestic exports surged 79.8 per cent year-on-year in October — the seventh consecutive month above 20 per cent — with the trade surplus ballooning to S$19.3 billion.

Analyst: To put that surplus in context — S$19.3 billion is more than double September's S$9.03 billion and far exceeded the S$7.9 billion analyst consensus. The 79.8 per cent headline itself shattered the median forecast of 37.7 per cent. The composition tells the story: electronics NODX rose 73.9 per cent, led by disk media products surging 189.6 per cent to approximately S$1.1 billion, personal computers climbing 81 per cent to around S$700 million, and integrated circuits growing 53.1 per cent. Non-electronics NODX rose even faster at 81.9 per cent, with ship and boat structures and pharmaceuticals together surging 253.1 per cent. By destination, shipments to the United States jumped 214.6 per cent — underscoring how much of this boom is driven by American tech companies building AI infrastructure across Asia.

Lead presenter: EnterpriseSG's full-year NODX forecast is 14 to 16 per cent. The ten-month average is 33.6 per cent. That is a remarkable gap.

Analyst: Indeed — the official forecast is being exceeded by a factor of more than two. A substantial upward revision seems inevitable. Singapore is the financial and logistics hub through which the global AI buildout physically flows — semiconductors, servers, networking equipment. The strategic question is whether this is cyclical, like the semiconductor supercycles of the past, or structural — a permanent elevation in demand. There are lumpy one-off elements: EnterpriseSG noted a low base effect in ship structures, and pharmaceuticals can be volatile. But even stripping those out, the underlying trend across electronics and non-electronics categories is exceptionally broad-based. The 48.1 per cent growth in non-oil re-exports adds further confirmation that Singapore is capturing trade flows, not just producing goods.

中文主播稿

主播：新加坡公布了即使以今年已经很高的标准衡量也堪称非凡的贸易数据。10月非石油国内出口同比飙升79.8%——连续第七个月增长超过20%——贸易顺差膨胀至193亿新元。

分析师：将这一顺差放在背景下看——193亿新元是9月90.3亿新元的两倍多，远超分析师预期的79亿新元。79.8%的增速本身也大幅超出37.7%的中位数预期。构成讲述了整个故事：电子产品NODX增长73.9%，由磁盘媒体产品飙升189.6%至约11亿新元、个人电脑增长81%至约7亿新元、集成电路增长53.1%引领。非电子产品NODX增长更快，达81.9%，其中船舶和船艇结构以及药品合计飙升253.1%。按目的地看，对美国出口飙升214.6%——凸显了这场繁荣在多大程度上由美国科技公司在亚洲建设AI基础设施所驱动。

主播：EnterpriseSG的全年NODX预测是14%至16%。十个月平均值是33.6%。差距惊人。

分析师：确实如此——官方预测正被超出两倍以上。大幅上调几乎不可避免。新加坡是全球AI建设实物流通的金融和物流枢纽——半导体、服务器、网络设备。战略问题在于这是周期性的——类似过去的半导体超级周期——还是结构性的，代表着需求的永久性提升。有些一次性波动因素：EnterpriseSG指出船舶结构存在低基数效应，药品也可能波动。但即使剔除这些，电子和非电子品类的底层趋势都异常广泛。非石油转口48.1%的增长进一步证实新加坡正在捕获贸易流量，而不仅仅是生产商品。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Malaysia's third-quarter GDP came in at 5.5 per cent year-on-year, beating the advance estimate of 5.3 per cent. Growth for the first nine months reached 5.6 per cent. Bank Negara Governor Abdul Rasheed Ghaffour says the economy is "poised to enter 2027 from a position of strength."

Analyst: The growth composition confirms Malaysia's emergence as ASEAN's most balanced high-growth economy. Manufacturing expanded 7.7 per cent, driven by electrical and electronics products surging 16.5 per cent on global AI demand. Construction rose 5.9 per cent, with non-residential buildings — predominantly data centres — accelerating 16.7 per cent. Services grew 5.8 per cent, with information and communications at 8.7 per cent driven by data centre activity. Malaysia is capturing value across the entire AI infrastructure chain — from semiconductor packaging and testing to the physical facilities to the digital services they power. Exports surged 25.2 per cent, accelerating from 17 per cent in Q2, suggesting the RM350.4 billion in MIDA-approved investments are now translating into real output.

Lead presenter: Any weaknesses in the numbers?

Analyst: Mining and quarrying fell 6.1 per cent due to scheduled maintenance, with crude oil and condensate production down 12.4 per cent. BNM attributes this to planned shutdowns rather than structural decline, but it bears watching. Agriculture recovered to 1.4 per cent growth from a 3.6 per cent contraction. On the demand side, machinery and equipment investment at 12.7 per cent indicates companies are building capacity, not merely fulfilling orders. The inflation picture is enviable by ASEAN standards — 1.9 per cent headline versus the Philippines' 7.8 per cent — which gives Malaysia monetary policy room that most regional peers lack. The moderation from Q2's 6.0 per cent reflects base effects from cash transfers in Q3 2025 rather than a loss of momentum. BNM expects above 5 per cent growth for the full year.

主播：马来西亚第三季度GDP同比增长5.5%，超过了5.3%的预估。前九个月增长达到5.6%。国家银行行长阿卜杜尔·拉希德·加富尔表示经济"有望从强势地位迈入2027年"。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Vietnam has crossed a historic threshold. Total trade in the first ten months of 2026 surpassed US$1 trillion for the first time ever — up 32.3 per cent year-on-year. And that is not the only record: registered FDI hit US$54.85 billion, surging 74 per cent — the highest for this period since 2009.

Analyst: To appreciate the scale, Vietnam's total trade for all of 2024 was approximately US$732 billion. The country has added roughly US$280 billion in annual trade volume in under two years. Exports reached approximately US$499 billion, up 27.4 per cent. The FDI composition is revealing: new project registrations reached US$30.73 billion across 3,478 projects — 2.2 times the value of the same period in 2025. Singapore led newly registered FDI at US$9.52 billion, followed by South Korea at US$5.81 billion, with Luxembourg — likely reflecting holding-company structures rather than genuine Luxembourg-origin investment — at US$4.99 billion. Manufacturing received 51.6 per cent of total FDI. A World Bank report found that AI-related goods contributed more than 70 per cent of Vietnam's export growth.

Lead presenter: Vietnam has also received its first investment-grade sovereign rating.

Analyst: R&I upgraded Vietnam's foreign-currency sovereign rating from BB+ positive to BBB- stable — a significant milestone. This could lower borrowing costs and attract institutional investors mandated to hold only investment-grade sovereign debt. But there are constraints: consumer prices rose 4.57 per cent in the first ten months, slightly above the government's 4.5 per cent target. And the government's 10 per cent full-year GDP target would require approximately 12.5 per cent Q4 growth — extremely ambitious. The 70 per cent AI-related export concentration is both a strength and a vulnerability: it shows how deeply Vietnam is embedded in the global AI supply chain, but a correction in AI demand would hit disproportionately. Public debt declining from 41.3 per cent of GDP in 2020 to 30.5 per cent in 2025 provides fiscal headroom that many ASEAN peers lack.

中文主播稿

主播：越南跨越了历史性门槛。2026年前十个月贸易总额首次突破1万亿美元——同比增长32.3%。这还不是唯一的记录：注册FDI达548.5亿美元，飙升74%——2009年以来同期最高。

分析师：要理解这一规模，越南2024年全年贸易总额约为7,320亿美元。该国在不到两年内增加了约2,800亿美元的年贸易额。出口达到约4,990亿美元，增长27.4%。FDI构成颇具启示：新项目注册额达307.3亿美元，涉及3,478个项目——为2025年同期的2.2倍。新加坡以95.2亿美元领先新注册FDI，其后是韩国58.1亿美元，卢森堡——可能反映控股公司结构而非真正的卢森堡来源投资——49.9亿美元。制造业获得了总FDI的51.6%。世界银行报告发现AI相关商品贡献了越南出口增长的70%以上。

主播：越南还首次获得了投资级主权评级。

分析师：R&I将越南外币主权评级从BB+正面上调至BBB-稳定——这是一个重要的里程碑。这可能降低借贷成本，吸引被授权仅持有投资级主权债务的机构投资者。但存在约束：前十个月消费者价格上涨4.57%，略高于政府4.5%的目标。而政府10%的全年GDP目标需要约12.5%的第四季度增长——极为雄心勃勃。70%的AI相关出口集中既是优势也是脆弱性：它显示了越南多么深入地嵌入了全球AI供应链，但AI需求的回调将带来不成比例的打击。公共债务从2020年占GDP的41.3%降至2025年的30.5%，提供了许多东盟同行所缺乏的财政空间。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Thailand's data centre ambitions face a new geopolitical complication. The kingdom has 42 operational data centres with approximately THB 750 billion in cumulative investment, and 36 new projects approved last year worth US$23.1 billion. But US Bureau of Industry and Security export rules now require licences for advanced AI chip shipments to Thailand.

Analyst: The new rules target Nvidia H100, H200, and Blackwell processors — the hardware that powers the most advanced AI workloads. What makes this significant is the two-tier market it creates. Shipments to US-headquartered hyperscalers such as AWS and Microsoft are exempt, subject to monthly reporting. But independent chip-rental operators and Chinese-linked ventures face heightened scrutiny, with data centres using high-end US hardware barred from remotely renting large-scale compute to China-headquartered or embargoed entities without end-user verification and BIS approval. This bifurcation advantages the hyperscalers, who are already Thailand's largest data centre investors.

Lead presenter: How is the Thai government responding?

Analyst: With calibrated reassurance. Deputy Prime Minister Suphajee Suthumpun says the targeted chip categories represent just 0.7 per cent of Thailand's total semiconductor imports. Prime Minister Anutin says both US and Chinese tech firms have reaffirmed investment commitments. This dual-hedging strategy is pragmatic but becomes harder to sustain as US-China technology decoupling deepens. The more strategically significant move is the Federation of Thai Industries' proposed supply-chain mapping exercise covering power electronics, power modules, PCBs, control electronics, and cooling equipment. Thailand already hosts Delta Electronics, Schneider Electric, Fabrinet, Western Digital, and Seagate — deepening this domestic supply chain would reduce exposure to future trade restrictions. PwC and Oxford Economics project Thai data-centre capex rising from US$58.6 billion in 2027 to US$150 billion by 2050. Goldman Sachs estimates the buildout adds approximately 0.2 percentage points to GDP annually over 2026 to 2028.

中文主播稿

主播：泰国的数据中心雄心面临新的地缘政治复杂局面。泰国拥有42座运营数据中心，累计投资约7,500亿泰铢，去年又批准了36个新项目，价值231亿美元。但美国工业与安全局的出口规则现在要求对向泰国出口先进AI芯片实施许可证管理。

分析师：新规则针对Nvidia H100、H200和Blackwell处理器——驱动最先进AI工作负载的硬件。重要之处在于它创造了双层市场。对AWS和微软等美国总部超大规模运营商的出货豁免，需按月报告。但独立芯片租赁运营商和中资关联企业面临更严格审查，使用美国高端硬件的数据中心未经终端用户验证和BIS批准，不得向中国总部或受制裁实体远程出租大规模算力。这种分化有利于超大规模运营商——它们已是泰国最大的数据中心投资者。

主播：泰国政府如何应对？

分析师：以精心校准的方式安抚各方。副总理素帕吉表示目标芯片类别仅占泰国半导体进口总额的0.7%。总理阿努廷表示美国和中国科技企业均已重申投资承诺。这种双向对冲策略是务实的，但随着中美技术脱钩加深，维持起来将更加困难。战略上更重要的举措是泰国工业联合会提议的供应链测绘，覆盖电力电子、功率模块、印刷电路板、控制电子和冷却设备。泰国已有Delta电子、施耐德电气、Fabrinet、西部数据和希捷——深化国内供应链将降低对未来贸易限制的敞口。普华永道和牛津经济研究院预测泰国数据中心资本支出将从2027年的586亿美元升至2050年的1,500亿美元。高盛估计2026至2028年间该建设每年为GDP增加约0.2个百分点。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: And finally to the Philippines, where the central bank faces perhaps the most difficult policy challenge in ASEAN right now. Inflation has hit 7.8 per cent — a three-year high — while GDP growth has slumped to 2.1 per cent, the slowest since the pandemic. The word on every analyst's lips: stagflation.

Analyst: The BSP has hiked 125 basis points in 2026, including a 50-basis-point jumbo increase in October, bringing the policy rate to 5.50 per cent. But BPI chief economist Emilio Neri Jr. calculates the real policy rate is approximately negative 2.3 per cent — meaning that despite aggressive tightening, monetary policy remains effectively accommodative because inflation has outrun rate increases. That is a sobering finding. The Q3 GDP breakdown reveals broad-based weakness: household consumption grew just 2.4 per cent, its weakest non-pandemic expansion since 2010; investment contracted 0.2 per cent; agriculture fell 4.8 per cent. Only government consumption at 6.8 per cent provided meaningful support.

Lead presenter: So rates need to go higher, but higher rates risk deepening the slowdown?

Analyst: Exactly the dilemma. The BSP may need to push rates to 6.00 per cent or beyond before policy becomes genuinely restrictive. Bank of America forecasts 25 basis points in December and a further 25 in February 2027. But every rate hike adds pressure to an economy already producing the weakest household consumption in a decade and actual investment contraction. The contrast with ASEAN peers is stark: Vietnam is growing above 7 per cent, Malaysia at 5.5 per cent, Indonesia at 5.47 per cent. The Philippines has triple Malaysia's inflation rate and less than half its growth. Agriculture's 4.8 per cent contraction directly affects rural incomes and food prices, creating a feedback loop where supply-side weakness feeds demand-side inflation. The BSP meets for the final time this year on 17 December. Citigroup has already applied the stagflation label to the Philippine economy — the concern now is whether that label becomes a self-fulfilling drag on investment and capital flows.

中文主播稿

主播：最后转向菲律宾，央行正面临东盟当前最困难的政策挑战。通胀达到7.8%的三年高位，而GDP增长跌至2.1%，为疫情以来最慢。每位分析师嘴边的词汇是：滞胀。

分析师：BSP在2026年已加息125个基点，包括10月的50个基点超大幅加息，将政策利率提至5.50%。但BPI首席经济学家埃米利奥·内里计算，实际政策利率约为负2.3%——这意味着尽管大幅紧缩，货币政策实际上仍然是宽松的，因为通胀已经超越了加息幅度。这是一个发人深省的发现。第三季度GDP构成显示全面疲弱：家庭消费仅增长2.4%，为2010年以来最弱的非疫情期扩张；投资收缩0.2%；农业下降4.8%。只有政府消费以6.8%提供了有意义的支撑。

主播：所以利率需要更高，但更高的利率可能加深经济放缓？

分析师：正是这个困境。BSP可能需要将利率推高至6.00%甚至更高才能使政策真正具有限制性。美银预期12月加25个基点，2027年2月再加25个基点。但每一次加息都给已经产生十年来最弱家庭消费和实际投资收缩的经济增加了压力。与东盟同行的对比鲜明：越南增长超过7%，马来西亚5.5%，印尼5.47%。菲律宾的通胀率是马来西亚的三倍，增长不到其一半。农业4.8%的收缩直接影响农村收入和食品价格，形成供给侧疲弱助推需求侧通胀的反馈循环。BSP今年最后一次会议定于12月17日。花旗集团已经给菲律宾经济贴上了滞胀标签——现在的担忧是这个标签是否会成为对投资和资本流动自我实现的拖累。
