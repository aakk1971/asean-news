Asian stocks suffer sharpest selloff in months as AI bubble fears spread

Event: 6 November 2026. Multiple market sources.

Verified reporting

Asian equity markets saw their largest single-day losses in months on 6 November. South Korea's KOSPI fell 8.6% to 4,923, triggering a 20-minute trading halt. Japan's Nikkei 225 dropped about 6%. Taiwan's TAIEX fell more than 8%. The selloff followed a 5.52% decline on the Nasdaq Composite and a more than 12% drop in the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index overnight.

The Institute of International Finance reported emerging-market net portfolio outflows of US$33.8 billion in October — the largest monthly outflow since March. Equity outflows totalled US$31.7 billion, with emerging Asia accounting for US$28.7 billion. Taiwan alone saw US$31.8 billion in equity sales, the largest monthly figure in IIF records dating to 2000.

Our analysis

The selloff was triggered by a combination of AI valuation doubts, profit-taking after strong gains, and warnings from major bank CEOs about potential 10–20% equity declines. For ASEAN businesses, the immediate question is whether this capital flight extends to regional markets and currencies, or remains concentrated in the tech-heavy North Asian exchanges.

Unknowns & what comes next

Whether this is a correction or the start of a broader downturn depends on upcoming tech earnings, US Federal Reserve signals, and whether AI investment commitments hold. Watch Southeast Asian currency and bond-flow data in coming weeks.

AI泡沫忧虑蔓延，亚洲股市遭遇数月来最大跌幅

事件：2026年11月6日。来源：多家市场报道。

已核实事实

11月6日亚洲股市遭遇数月来最大单日跌幅。韩国KOSPI指数暴跌8.6%至4,923点，触发20分钟临时停牌。日经225指数跌约6%，台湾加权指数跌逾8%。此前纳斯达克综合指数跌5.52%，费城半导体指数跌逾12%。

国际金融协会(IIF)报告显示，10月新兴市场净投资组合流出338亿美元，为3月以来最大月度流出。股票流出317亿美元，其中亚洲新兴市场占287亿美元。台湾单月股票抛售达318亿美元，创IIF自2000年有记录以来最高。

原创分析：此轮抛售由AI估值疑虑、获利回吐及大行CEO关于股市可能下跌10-20%的警告共同触发。东盟企业需关注资本外流是否从北亚科技股蔓延至本区域市场和货币。

OCBC raises 2026 loan growth guidance after Q3 beat

Released 7 Nov 2026. Reuters via The Manila Times. Singapore.

Verified reporting

OCBC raised its 2026 loan growth guidance to low double digits, from high-single to low-double digits previously. The bank also guided for total income growth of at least mid-single digits, and lowered its credit-cost guidance to 15–20 basis points from 20–25 basis points. Q3 net profit of S$2.04 billion slightly beat the S$2.01 billion analyst consensus. Net interest income rose 4% on loan growth, though net interest margin fell 18 basis points. Banking wealth management income (excluding Great Eastern) rose 2% to a record S$1.12 billion.

Our analysis

OCBC's guidance upgrade signals confidence in lending demand across Asia, driven by the region's wealth boom. However, trading income fell 10% and insurance income fell 37%, showing how non-banking units can drag on group results. The lower credit-cost guidance suggests the bank sees stable asset quality ahead.

Unknowns & what comes next

The bank said it is monitoring the Middle East conflict, energy markets and conditions in Indonesia. Peers DBS and UOB reported weaker-than-expected profits, raising questions about whether OCBC's upgrade reflects bank-specific factors or broader lending momentum.

华侨银行第三季业绩超预期，上调2026年贷款增长指引

2026年11月7日公布。路透社经马尼拉时报报道。新加坡。

已核实事实

华侨银行将2026年贷款增长指引上调至低两位数，此前为高单位数至低两位数。同时预计总收入至少实现中单位数增长，信贷成本指引从20-25个基点下调至15-20个基点。第三季净利20.4亿新元，略超分析师预期的20.1亿新元。净利息收入受贷款增长推动增长4%，但净息差下降18个基点。银行财富管理收入（不含大东方）增2%至创纪录的11.2亿新元。

原创分析：上调指引反映亚洲贷款需求及财富增长的信心。但交易收入降10%、保险收入降37%，显示非银行业务对集团业绩的拖累。信贷成本下调显示资产质量预期稳定。同业星展及大华银行业绩逊于预期，华侨银行是否为个案值得观察。

Indonesia's Q3 GDP grows 5.47%, above forecast

Released 5 Nov 2026; reference period Q3 2026. Jakarta.

Verified reporting

Indonesia's GDP grew 5.47% year on year in the third quarter, accelerating from 5.29% in Q2 and beating the 5.18% Reuters poll forecast. Quarter-on-quarter growth was 1.59%. Exports grew 4.59%, household consumption rose 5.04%, and government spending increased 8.95%. The government said it expects full-year growth of 5.4–5.5%.

Our analysis

Indonesia's growth beat comes at a time when many Asian economies face headwinds from the tech selloff and capital outflows. The strong government spending component raises questions about sustainability, while robust household consumption suggests resilient domestic demand. Businesses should assess whether the growth is broad-based or concentrated in specific sectors.

Unknowns & what comes next

The composition of export growth by commodity and destination is not fully detailed. Watch whether the capital outflows hitting other Asian markets spill over to Indonesian assets and the rupiah.

Philippine exports hit record US$9.87 billion in September

Released 2 Nov 2026; reference period September 2026. Philippine Statistics Authority.

Verified reporting

Philippine merchandise exports reached US$9.87 billion in September 2026, up 35.6% from US$7.28 billion a year earlier — the highest monthly export value since the PSA began tracking trade in 1991. This marked the 21st consecutive month of year-on-year growth. January-to-September exports totalled US$73.95 billion, up 17.2%. Electronic products led at US$6.92 billion (70.1% of total), adding US$2.89 billion year on year. Imports rose 25.7% to US$15.03 billion, widening the trade deficit to US$5.15 billion.

Our analysis

The Philippines' export surge is driven overwhelmingly by electronics and semiconductors, reflecting the country's deepening role in global chip supply chains. The heavy concentration in one sector is both a strength and a vulnerability — especially given the current AI bubble fears that are shaking tech stocks across Asia.

Unknowns & what comes next

Whether this export pace is sustainable depends on global semiconductor demand and whether the AI investment cycle continues. The widening trade deficit also bears watching as it could put pressure on the peso.

菲律宾9月出口创纪录达98.7亿美元

2026年11月2日公布；统计期为9月。菲律宾统计局。

已核实事实

菲律宾9月商品出口达98.7亿美元，同比增35.6%，为统计局1991年有记录以来最高月度出口额。连续21个月同比增长。前九个月出口总计739.5亿美元，增17.2%。电子产品出口69.2亿美元，占出口总额70.1%。进口增25.7%至150.3亿美元，贸易逆差扩大至51.5亿美元。

原创分析：出口激增主要由电子和半导体驱动，体现菲律宾在全球芯片供应链中地位加深。高度集中于单一行业既是优势也是风险——尤其在AI泡沫忧虑冲击亚洲科技股之际。贸易逆差扩大可能对比索构成压力。

Singapore Airlines unveils S$2 billion cabin overhaul

Announced 6 Nov 2026. Singapore Airlines.

Verified reporting

Singapore Airlines unveiled new seat products across all four cabin classes — its largest-ever investment at S$2 billion. The seats will go into retrofitted Airbus A350 aircraft and new Boeing 777-9s. First Class gets 1-2-1 suites with 80-inch beds and retractable centre dividers for double beds. Business Class features 1-2-1 seats with 79-inch flat beds and privacy doors. Entry into service is expected H2 2027, with A350 retrofit completion targeted by end 2030.

Our analysis

The investment signals SIA's bet on premium travel demand in Asia. However, the timeline carries execution risk: the Boeing 777-9 is certified but not yet in service, and the A350 seat certification is still pending. Competitors have been upgrading aggressively; whether SIA's new product arrives fast enough to maintain its premium positioning is the key question.

Unknowns & what comes next

Boeing 777-9 delivery timeline remains uncertain. Watch for fare and yield impacts once the new cabins enter service.

新加坡航空投入20亿新元全面更新客舱

2026年11月6日公布。新加坡航空。

已核实事实

新加坡航空发布四舱全新座椅产品，投资额达20亿新元，为历来最大。新座椅将安装于改装的A350机队及新波音777-9。头等舱采用1-2-1布局套间，配80英寸床及可收回隔板实现双人床。商务舱配79英寸全平躺床及隐私门。预计2027年下半年投入服务，A350改装目标于2030年底完成。

原创分析：巨额投资反映新航看好亚洲高端旅行需求。但时间线存在执行风险：777-9已获认证但尚未服役，A350座椅认证仍在进行中。竞争对手也在积极升级，新产品能否及时上线以维持高端定位是关键。

Malaysia draws 32.3 million visitors in nine months, targets 43 million

Data: January–September 2026. Visit Malaysia 2026 campaign.

Verified reporting

Malaysia recorded 32.3 million international arrivals in the first nine months of 2026, up 2.4% from 31.5 million in the same period of 2025. The Visit Malaysia 2026 target is 43 million visitors. Tourism contributed RM323 billion in value added in 2025, or 15.9% of GDP, supporting about 3.7 million jobs. In Kuala Lumpur, JLL projected 1,384 new hotel rooms in 2026, about 60% luxury. The Waldorf Astoria Kuala Lumpur opened in October with 268 suites. Langkawi expects about 1,750 additional upscale rooms within two years, raising five-star inventory by roughly 85%.

Our analysis

At 32.3 million arrivals through September, Malaysia needs about 10.7 million visitors in the final quarter to hit its 43-million target — achievable given seasonal peaks but ambitious. The luxury hotel pipeline suggests operators are betting on higher-spending visitors rather than just volume. Langkawi's 85% jump in five-star rooms is a particularly bold bet that could reshape the island's tourism economics.

Unknowns & what comes next

Whether the luxury supply can be absorbed depends on airlift capacity and whether premium travellers shift from competitors like Thailand and Bali. Watch Q4 arrival figures and hotel occupancy rates.

马来西亚前九个月接待3,230万游客，目标4,300万

数据：2026年1至9月。马来西亚旅游年活动。

已核实事实

2026年前九个月马来西亚接待国际旅客3,230万人次，同比增2.4%。旅游年目标为4,300万。2025年旅游业增加值达3,230亿令吉，占GDP的15.9%，支撑约370万个就业岗位。吉隆坡预计2026年新增1,384间酒店客房，约60%为豪华级。华尔道夫吉隆坡酒店10月开业，设268间套房。兰卡威未来两年预计新增约1,750间高端客房，五星级库存增长约85%。

原创分析：前九个月3,230万人次意味着第四季需再吸引约1,070万游客才能达标——有季节性旺季支撑但仍具挑战。豪华酒店供给大增显示业者看好高消费客群。兰卡威五星级客房增85%是大胆押注，可能重塑该岛旅游经济格局。

Thailand's GULF Energy posts 28% profit jump on solar and digital expansion

Q3 2026 results reported 6 Nov 2026. Gulf Energy Development (GULF).

Verified reporting

Gulf Energy Development reported Q3 net profit attributable to shareholders of THB9.289 billion, up 28% year on year. Core profit rose 24% to THB9.045 billion. Revenue surged 75% to THB52.809 billion. The company maintained 2026 revenue and EBITDA growth guidance of 12–15%. Four solar and solar-plus-storage projects totalling 373 MW are expected to start in November and December. GULF now operates 14 domestic solar projects with 1,379 MW capacity, up from five projects totalling 532 MW a year earlier. Its digital business returned to profitability after its GSA01 data centre began full operation in June.

Our analysis

GULF's results highlight two major trends in Southeast Asian energy: the accelerating shift to solar power and the growing intersection of energy and data centres. The company's rapid solar expansion — from 532 MW to 1,379 MW in one year — reflects Thailand's aggressive renewable-energy push. The data-centre profitability milestone is notable given the broader AI infrastructure buildout across the region.

Unknowns & what comes next

Whether GULF's revenue growth rate is sustainable at 75% depends on one-off contributions versus recurring operations. Watch the commissioning of the 373 MW in new solar capacity and whether data-centre profitability is maintained.

泰国GULF能源净利增28%，太阳能与数据中心驱动增长

2026年第三季业绩，11月6日公布。Gulf Energy Development。

已核实事实

GULF第三季归母净利92.89亿泰铢，同比增28%。核心净利90.45亿泰铢，增24%。营收528.09亿泰铢，猛增75%。公司维持2026年营收和EBITDA增长12-15%的指引。11至12月将有4个太阳能及储能项目共373MW投运。目前运营14个国内太阳能项目，装机1,379MW，较一年前5个项目532MW大幅增长。数据中心业务在GSA01于6月全面运营后恢复盈利。

原创分析：业绩凸显东南亚能源两大趋势——太阳能加速发展及能源与数据中心融合。一年内太阳能装机从532MW增至1,379MW，反映泰国激进的可再生能源政策。数据中心扭亏为盈值得关注，契合区域AI基础设施建设浪潮。
