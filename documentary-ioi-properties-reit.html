Lead presenter: Good evening and welcome to a special ASEAN Business Brief documentary. Tonight we take you inside one of Malaysia's most anticipated listings of 2026 — the IOI Properties Group REIT, set to debut on Bursa Malaysia's Main Market on 25 November. Tonight we trace that journey, examine every asset in this REIT, look at what analysts are saying about the parent company's share price, and weigh the case for and against investing — because the numbers tell a story of real tension. A 6.6 per cent yield against a 0.59 times oversubscription rate. Premium assets against extreme concentration risk. Let's begin.

The IOI Story: From Palm Oil to Property Empire
IOI故事：从棕榈油到地产帝国

THE IOI STORY · IOI的故事

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To understand this REIT, you need to understand the man who built the empire behind it. Tan Sri Lee Shin Cheng was born in 1939 and founded IOI on 31 October 1969 as Industrial Oxygen Incorporated — a small company selling industrial gases. The firm listed on the Kuala Lumpur Stock Exchange in 1980.

Analyst: The transformation came in 1983 when Lee entered the palm oil plantation business. What made him exceptional was efficiency — his plantations achieved yields of roughly six tonnes per hectare per year, against a Malaysian industry average that has been stuck at about four tonnes for decades. By the time of his death in June 2019, IOI had grown to manage over 200,000 hectares across Malaysia and Indonesia, with 15 mills and 98 estates. Plantations still generate about 63 per cent of IOI Group's profits. The company also became Asia's largest vegetable oil-based oleochemical producer with annual capacity above 890,000 tonnes.

Lead presenter: And the property business?

Analyst: IOI entered property development in 1984 and launched its first township — the 930-acre Bandar Puchong Jaya — in 1990. The model was classic Malaysian property development: buy land cheap, build townships, generate value. By 2013 the property arm had grown large enough to justify a demerger. IOI Properties Group Berhad listed on Bursa Malaysia on 15 January 2014 as a standalone company. Today, it is run by Lee Yeow Chor, the founder's son. The group's total assets stand at approximately 36 billion ringgit. And here is what few people outside the industry realise — most of that value is not in Malaysia. It's in Singapore.

中文主播稿

主播：要理解这个REIT，需要先了解建立其背后帝国的那个人。丹斯里李深静生于1939年，1969年10月31日创立IOI，起初只是一家名为"工业氧气公司"的小企业。公司于1980年在吉隆坡证券交易所上市。

分析师：转折发生在1983年，李深静进入棕榈油种植业。使他与众不同的是效率——他的种植园每公顷年产量约六吨，而马来西亚行业平均水平数十年来一直停滞在约四吨。到2019年6月去世时，IOI在马来西亚和印尼管理超过20万公顷土地，拥有15家榨油厂和98个庄园。种植业务仍贡献IOI集团约63%的利润。公司还成为亚洲最大的植物油基油脂化学品生产商，年产能超89万吨。

主播：那地产业务呢？

分析师：IOI于1984年进入房地产开发领域，1990年推出首个城镇项目——占地930英亩的蒲种再也。这是经典的马来西亚地产开发模式：低价买地、建设城镇、创造价值。到2013年，地产部门已大到足以分拆上市。IOI置业集团于2014年1月15日在马来西亚交易所独立上市。如今由创始人之子李耀祖掌管。集团总资产约360亿令吉。而业界外很少有人意识到的是——这些价值的大部分不在马来西亚，而在新加坡。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: You mentioned that most of the value is in Singapore. Walk us through that.

Analyst: This is one of the most remarkable stories in Asian real estate. IOI Properties first entered Singapore in 1996 by buying a commercial building in Bugis. Fast forward three decades and Singapore now holds approximately 64 per cent of IOI Properties' 36 billion ringgit in assets — roughly five billion Singapore dollars worth of prime real estate in the financial district.

Lead presenter: What are the key properties?

Analyst: The flagship is IOI Central Boulevard Towers — twin office towers in the heart of the financial district, directly across from Lau Pa Sat. The total investment was approximately four billion Singapore dollars including land. It has about 1.3 million square feet of leasable space, with tenants including Amazon and Morgan Stanley, commanding rents above 11 Singapore dollars per square foot per month. Fully leased, it could generate at least 180 million in annual rent. Then there is South Beach, a hotel-office-residential complex developed jointly with City Developments since 2011. Shenton House in Raffles Place, acquired for 538 million, is being redeveloped with a total investment of up to two billion into offices and serviced apartments. And Marina View Residences — built on a 1.5 billion dollar land site with another billion in construction — will include a 350-room W Hotel and 683 W Residences, completing in 2028.

Lead presenter: But crucially, none of these Singapore assets are in the REIT being listed?

Analyst: Correct. And that's an important point. The REIT being listed next week holds only the Malaysian assets. IOI Properties has previously indicated it may eventually list Singapore assets in a separate Singapore-listed REIT — but that is a future story, not today's. What is being sold to investors now is the Malaysian portfolio.

中文主播稿

主播：你提到大部分价值在新加坡。请详细说明。

分析师：这是亚洲房地产最引人注目的故事之一。IOI置业1996年首次进入新加坡，购买了武吉士一栋商业楼。三十年后的今天，新加坡资产约占IOI置业360亿令吉总资产的64%——约50亿新元的金融区黄金地产。

主播：主要物业有哪些？

分析师：旗舰项目是IOI中央大道双塔——位于金融区核心、老巴刹对面的双子办公楼。总投资约40亿新元。可出租面积约130万平方英尺，租户包括亚马逊和摩根士丹利，月租超过每平方英尺11新元。满租情况下年租金收入至少1.8亿新元。其次是南岸项目，自2011年起与城市发展有限公司合资开发的酒店-办公-住宅综合体。莱佛士坊的盛顿大厦以5.38亿新元收购，正在重建为办公楼和服务式公寓，总投资高达20亿。滨海湾景苑建于15亿新元的地块上，另投10亿建设，将包含350间客房的W酒店和683套W公寓，预计2028年竣工。

主播：但关键是，这些新加坡资产都不在此次上市的REIT中？

分析师：正确。这一点很重要。下周上市的REIT仅包含马来西亚资产。IOI置业此前表示，可能最终会将新加坡资产纳入一个在新加坡上市的独立REIT——但那是未来的事，不是今天的。现在出售给投资者的是马来西亚投资组合。

The REIT: Nine Assets, One Mall
REIT：九项资产，一个购物中心

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Let's get into the actual REIT. Nine assets valued at 7.66 billion ringgit. What's in the box?

Analyst: Let me break it down clearly. The centrepiece is IOI City Mall in Putrajaya, which accounts for 67.2 per cent of the entire portfolio value and roughly 77 per cent of revenue. It has 889 tenants and 97.9 per cent committed occupancy — an excellent number. Revenue has grown from 392 million ringgit in 2023 to 581 million in 2025, nearly doubling distributable income from 141 million to 270 million over the same period. Beyond the mall, there are two office properties — IOI City Towers and PFCC Towers — making up 13 per cent, and six hotels accounting for 19.8 per cent. The hotels are all Marriott-branded: Putrajaya Marriott, Le Méridien Putrajaya, Moxy Putrajaya, Four Points by Sheraton Puchong, W Kuala Lumpur, and Courtyard by Marriott Penang. All operate under master leases with sponsor subsidiaries.

Lead presenter: And the financial structure?

Analyst: The IPO price is 96 sen retail, 91 sen institutional. The net asset value per unit is 91 sen, so at the retail price investors pay a modest 5 per cent premium — a price-to-book of 1.05 times. Gearing is 34.1 per cent, which is 2.65 billion in borrowings, well below the regulatory 50 per cent cap. The projected first full-year distribution per unit is 6.33 sen, translating to a 6.6 per cent yield on the retail price. The REIT plans to pay at least 90 per cent of distributable income quarterly. One critical point: the entire 2.1 billion ringgit raised in this IPO goes to the sponsor — IOI Properties Group. The REIT itself receives zero fresh capital.

中文主播稿

主播：让我们深入了解REIT本身。九项资产，估值76.6亿令吉。具体包含什么？

分析师：让我清晰地分解。核心是布城的IOI城市购物中心，占整个资产组合价值的67.2%，约占收入的77%。拥有889个租户，承诺出租率97.9%——非常出色的数据。收入从2023年的3.924亿令吉增长到2025年的5.81亿，同期可分配收入几乎翻倍，从1.41亿增至2.70亿。购物中心之外，还有两栋办公楼——IOI城市大厦和PFCC大厦——占比13%，以及六家酒店占比19.8%。酒店全部为万豪品牌：布城万豪、布城艾美、布城Moxy、蒲种福朋喜来登、吉隆坡W酒店和槟城万怡。全部以总租赁方式与发起人子公司运营。

主播：财务结构如何？

分析师：零售IPO价格96仙，机构价格91仙。每单位净资产值为91仙，因此零售价仅有5%的温和溢价——市净率1.05倍。杠杆率34.1%，即26.5亿借款，远低于监管上限50%。预计首个完整年度每单位派息6.33仙，以零售价计收益率6.6%。REIT计划每季度分配至少90%的可分配收入。一个关键要点：此次IPO募集的全部21亿令吉归发起人——IOI置业集团所有。REIT本身未获得任何新资本。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Let's look at what the market thinks. First the parent company, IOI Properties Group.

Analyst: As of early October, the parent's shares closed at 3.30 ringgit. The analyst consensus from 10 analysts polled by S&P Global is a Buy. The average price target is 4.41 ringgit — that's 33.6 per cent upside from the last close. The range runs from 3.82 on the low end to 5.20 on the high end. Of those ten analysts, six rate it Strong Buy, none just Buy, four Hold, and zero Sell. No analyst has a sell recommendation. The Strong Buy count actually rose from four in May to six by August as the REIT IPO timeline became clearer — analysts see the listing as a value-unlocking event for the parent company.

Lead presenter: And the REIT itself — what does the IPO demand tell us?

Analyst: This is where it gets interesting — and concerning. The public tranche attracted just 0.59 times oversubscription. To be blunt, that is very weak. It ranks 50th out of 52 Bursa Malaysia IPOs balloted in 2026, against a median oversubscription of 20 times. For statistical context, Bursa IPOs with less than 10 times oversubscription this year have averaged a negative 4 per cent first-day return, while the overall 2026 IPO average is positive 21.9 per cent. So history is not on the side of a strong listing-day pop. The market is telling us something — retail investors are either unconvinced by the yield relative to alternatives, concerned about concentration risk, or simply have better options.

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Let's lay out the positive case first. What are the strongest arguments for investing in this REIT?

Analyst: There are five that stand out. First, the yield. At 6.6 per cent projected for the first full year, it compares favourably against Malaysian government bond yields and many existing listed REITs. If income is your priority, this delivers quarterly distributions backed by nine freehold prime commercial assets. Second, IOI City Mall itself is genuinely excellent — 97.9 per cent occupancy across 889 tenants is among the best on Bursa. Revenue has nearly doubled in two years. This is not a struggling asset.

Lead presenter: And beyond the mall?

Analyst: Third, the gearing is conservative at 34.1 per cent, well below the 50 per cent cap, giving the REIT headroom for future acquisitions without immediately diluting existing unitholders. Fourth, the sponsor pipeline is visible — IOI Properties Group retains malls like IOI Mall Damansara and IOI Mall Puchong that could be injected into the REIT in future, creating organic growth beyond rental escalations. And fifth, the six Marriott-branded hotels operate under master leases providing guaranteed minimum rent, which reduces income volatility from the hospitality segment and offers upside when hotels perform well. For a yield-seeking investor with a multi-year horizon, this is a credible income play backed by real, performing assets.

中文主播稿

主播：先列出积极的一面。投资这个REIT最有力的论据是什么？

分析师：有五点突出。第一，收益率。首个完整年度预计6.6%，优于马来西亚国债收益率和许多现有上市REIT。如果收入是首要考量，这提供由九项永久地契优质商业资产支持的季度派息。第二，IOI城市购物中心本身确实优秀——889个租户97.9%的出租率在马来西亚交易所名列前茅。收入在两年内几乎翻倍。这不是一项陷入困境的资产。

主播：购物中心之外呢？

分析师：第三，34.1%的杠杆率保守，远低于50%上限，为REIT未来收购提供了空间，无需立即稀释现有持有人。第四，发起人的资产储备清晰可见——IOI置业集团仍持有IOI万达购物中心和IOI蒲种购物中心等资产，未来可注入REIT，创造超越租金递增的有机增长。第五，六家万豪品牌酒店以总租赁方式运营，提供保底最低租金，降低酒店业务收入波动，表现良好时还有上行分享。对于具有多年投资视野的收益型投资者，这是一个由真实、表现良好的资产支持的可信收入工具。

THE BEAR CASE · 看空理由
SCRIPT READY · VIDEO NOT RENDERED
稿件已就绪 · 视频未生成

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Now the other side. What should give investors pause?

Analyst: Six things, and I will not mince words. First and most critically — concentration risk. IOI City Mall is 67 per cent of the portfolio by value and 77 per cent of revenue. This is one of the most concentrated REIT portfolios on Bursa Malaysia. Any disruption to that single asset — a major tenant leaving, a competing development drawing footfall, or Putrajaya's commercial trajectory stalling — hits the entire REIT disproportionately.

Lead presenter: The oversubscription numbers also raised eyebrows?

Analyst: That is the second flag. At 0.59 times, it's among the weakest demand of any Bursa IPO this year. IPOs with that level of demand have historically averaged negative returns on listing day. Third, every single ringgit of the 2.1 billion raised goes to the sponsor — IOI Properties Group — not to the REIT. The REIT receives no fresh capital for growth or debt reduction. This is essentially the sponsor monetising its assets. Fourth, the six hotel master leases are with sponsor subsidiaries. The guaranteed rent sounds reassuring, but it ultimately depends on the sponsor's own financial health — and IOI Properties Group carries roughly 18 billion ringgit in debt, the highest of any listed Malaysian developer. That creates circular risk. Fifth, the IOI group has ESG history — Greenpeace reported on habitat destruction in 2008, 2015, and 2016, Finnwatch raised labour concerns in 2014, and the RSPO suspended IOI's palm oil sustainability certification in 2016. These relate to plantations, not property, but ESG-focused funds may avoid the name. And sixth, REITs are inherently interest-rate sensitive. Bank Negara has held the OPR at 2.75 per cent for a year, but any future tightening would increase borrowing costs and compress the yield premium investors demand.

中文主播稿

主播：现在看另一面。什么应该让投资者三思？

分析师：六个方面，我不会含糊其辞。首先也是最关键的——集中风险。IOI城市购物中心按价值计占投资组合的67%，按收入计占77%。这是马来西亚交易所上最集中的REIT投资组合之一。该单一资产的任何变故——主要租户离开、竞争项目分流客流、或布城商业发展轨迹停滞——都将对整个REIT造成不成比例的冲击。

主播：超额认购数据也引人关注？

分析师：这是第二个警示。0.59倍在今年马来西亚交易所IPO中属于最弱的需求之列。这种需求水平的IPO历史上首日平均回报为负。第三，IPO募集的全部21亿令吉归发起人——IOI置业集团——而非REIT。REIT未获得任何新资本用于增长或减债。这本质上是发起人将其资产变现。第四，六项酒店总租赁协议的对手方是发起人子公司。保底租金听起来令人安心，但最终取决于发起人自身的财务健康——而IOI置业集团约有180亿令吉债务，为马来西亚上市开发商中最高。这构成循环风险。第五，IOI集团有ESG历史——绿色和平组织在2008、2015和2016年报告了栖息地破坏，Finnwatch在2014年提出劳工问题，RSPO在2016年暂停了IOI的棕榈油可持续性认证。这些涉及种植业而非地产，但注重ESG的基金可能回避。第六，REIT本质上对利率敏感。国家银行已将OPR维持在2.75%一年，但未来任何收紧都将增加借贷成本并压缩收益率溢价。
