1 · Overview / 概览

IOIPG Malaysia REIT (stock code: 5359) is set to list on Bursa Malaysia's Main Market on 25 November 2026. At an IPO price of RM0.96 per unit (retail) and approximately 5.5 billion units, the listing values the REIT at roughly RM5.28 billion — making it Malaysia's largest REIT IPO of 2026.

The REIT is sponsored by IOI Properties Group Berhad (KLSE: IOIPG), itself the property arm of the IOI conglomerate founded by the late Tan Sri Lee Shin Cheng. The offering comprises nine freehold commercial assets valued at RM7.66 billion, headlined by the flagship IOI City Mall in Putrajaya.

The projected FY2027 distribution yield is 6.6% based on the RM0.96 retail price, with gearing of 34.1% — well below the regulatory 50% cap.

IOIPG马来西亚REIT（股票代码：5359）将于2026年11月25日在马来西亚交易所主板上市。以每单位RM0.96的零售IPO价格和约55亿个单位计算，上市估值约RM52.8亿——为马来西亚2026年最大的REIT上市。

该REIT由IOI置业集团（KLSE: IOIPG）发起，后者是已故丹斯里李深静创立的IOI集团旗下的地产部门。此次发行包括九项永久地契商业资产，估值RM76.6亿，以布城旗舰IOI城市购物中心为核心。

预计2027财年派息收益率为6.6%（基于RM0.96零售价），杠杆率为34.1%——远低于监管上限50%。

2 · The IOI Story: From Palm Oil to Property Empire
IOI故事：从棕榈油到地产帝国

1969 FOUNDED → 1980 LISTED → 1983 PALM OIL → 1984 PROPERTY → 2014 DEMERGER → 2026 REIT IPO

The founder: Tan Sri Lee Shin Cheng (1939–2019) / 创始人：丹斯里李深静

Known as "The Tree Whisperer," Lee Shin Cheng founded IOI in 1969 as Industrial Oxygen Incorporated — a small industrial gas company. Through shrewd acquisition of undervalued palm oil plantations in the 1980s, he transformed IOI into one of the world's most efficient palm oil producers, with yields of roughly 6 tonnes per hectare per year against a Malaysian industry average of 4 tonnes. By the time of his death in June 2019, the Lee family's IOI holdings — across plantations, oleochemicals, refineries, and property — were worth billions.

被誉为"树语者"的李深静于1969年创立IOI，最初是一家名为"工业氧气公司"的小型工业气体企业。通过在1980年代精明收购被低估的棕榈油种植园，他将IOI转变为全球最高效的棕榈油生产商之一，每公顷产量约6吨，远高于马来西亚行业平均的4吨。到2019年6月去世时，李氏家族的IOI资产——涵盖种植园、油脂化学品、炼油厂和房地产——已价值数十亿。

IOI Group today / 今天的IOI集团

IOI Corporation (MYX: 1961) remains one of Asia's largest palm oil companies, managing over 200,000 hectares across Malaysia and Indonesia with 15 mills and 98 estates. Plantations contribute approximately 63% of group profits. The group is also Asia's largest vegetable oil-based oleochemical producer (890,000+ tonnes/year capacity). Current CEO: Lee Yeow Chor, son of the founder.

IOI集团（MYX: 1961）仍是亚洲最大的棕榈油企业之一，在马来西亚和印尼管理超过20万公顷种植园，拥有15家榨油厂和98个庄园。种植业务贡献集团约63%的利润。集团同时也是亚洲最大的植物油基油脂化学品生产商（年产能逾89万吨）。现任首席执行官：创始人之子李耀祖。

IOI Properties: Demerger and growth / IOI置业：分拆与成长

IOI entered property development in 1984, launching its first township — the 930-acre Bandar Puchong Jaya — in 1990. The property division was demerged and listed as IOI Properties Group Berhad on Bursa Malaysia on 15 January 2014. Since then, the group has grown aggressively into Singapore, where it now holds approximately 64% of its RM36.1 billion in assets by market value.

IOI于1984年进入房地产开发领域，1990年推出其首个城镇项目——占地930英亩的蒲种再也。地产部门于2014年1月15日分拆并以IOI置业集团名义在马来西亚交易所上市。此后，集团积极拓展新加坡市场，目前按市值计算，新加坡资产约占其RM361亿资产的64%。

IOIPG REIT (5359) — the new listing / 新上市REIT

At the RM0.96 retail price, the REIT trades at a price-to-book ratio of 1.05× — a modest 5% premium to NAV (RM0.91). Comparable Malaysian REITs for reference include Sunway REIT, Pavilion REIT, Paradigm REIT, and IGB REIT.

The 0.59× oversubscription rate is notably weak — ranking 50th out of 52 Bursa IPOs balloted in 2026, against a median oversubscription of 20×. For context, 2026 Bursa IPOs with less than 10× oversubscription averaged a −4.0% first-day return, while the overall 2026 IPO average was +21.9%.

以RM0.96零售价计，该REIT的市净率为1.05倍——较净资产值（RM0.91）仅有5%的温和溢价。0.59倍的超额认购率明显偏弱——在2026年马来西亚交易所52宗IPO中排名第50，远低于20倍的中位数。作为参考，2026年超额认购低于10倍的IPO首日平均回报为−4.0%，而全年IPO平均为+21.9%。

5 · The Case For Investing
投资的理由

① Attractive yield / 具吸引力的收益率

The projected 6.6% distribution yield compares favourably against Malaysian government bond yields and many existing listed REITs. If the REIT delivers on its income projections, unitholders receive a quarterly dividend backed by nine freehold prime assets.

预计6.6%的派息收益率优于马来西亚国债收益率和许多现有上市REIT。如果REIT兑现其收入预测，持有人将获得由九项永久地契优质资产支持的季度股息。

② IOI City Mall dominance / IOI城市购物中心的主导地位

IOI City Mall is one of Malaysia's largest malls with 97.9% committed occupancy across 889 tenants. Its location in IOI Resort City, Putrajaya — a growing commercial hub between Kuala Lumpur and KLIA — underpins strong footfall. Revenue has grown robustly year on year.

IOI城市购物中心是马来西亚最大的购物中心之一，889个租户的承诺出租率达97.9%。其位于布城IOI度假城的位置——吉隆坡与KLIA之间日益繁荣的商业中心——支撑了强劲的客流量。

③ Conservative gearing / 保守的杠杆率

At 34.1%, the REIT's gearing sits well below the Securities Commission's 50% regulatory cap, providing headroom for future acquisitions without immediately needing to issue new units or take on excessive debt.

34.1%的杠杆率远低于证券委员会50%的监管上限，为未来收购提供了空间，无需立即发行新单位或承担过多债务。

④ Sponsor pipeline / 发起人的资产储备

IOI Properties Group retains malls such as IOI Mall Damansara and IOI Mall Puchong that could be injected into the REIT in future, creating a visible organic growth path beyond just rental escalations.

IOI置业集团仍持有IOI万达购物中心和IOI蒲种购物中心等资产，未来可注入REIT，为其创造超越租金递增的有机增长路径。

⑤ Marriott hotel master leases / 万豪酒店总租赁

The six hotels operate under internationally branded master leases providing guaranteed minimum rent, reducing income volatility from the hospitality segment and offering upside participation when hotels perform well.

六家酒店以国际品牌总租赁方式运营，提供保底最低租金，降低酒店业务收入波动，并在酒店表现良好时提供上行分享。

6 · The Case Against / Risks
风险与顾虑

① Extreme concentration risk / 极端集中风险

IOI City Mall accounts for 67.2% of the portfolio value and approximately 77% of revenue. Any disruption to the mall — a major tenant departure, competing mall development, or Putrajaya's commercial trajectory stalling — would disproportionately impact the entire REIT. This is one of the most concentrated REIT portfolios on Bursa Malaysia.

IOI城市购物中心占资产组合价值的67.2%和约77%的收入。购物中心的任何变故——主要租户流失、竞争对手开发、或布城商业发展放缓——都将对整个REIT产生不成比例的冲击。这是马来西亚交易所上最集中的REIT组合之一。

② Weak IPO demand / IPO需求疲弱

The 0.59× oversubscription rate is a red flag. It suggests retail investors are not convinced by the valuation or yield relative to alternatives. Historically, low-oversubscription IPOs on Bursa Malaysia average negative first-day returns. This carries a risk of post-listing price weakness.

0.59倍的超额认购率是一个警示信号。这表明散户投资者对估值或收益率相对于替代投资的吸引力并不认可。历史上，马来西亚交易所低超额认购率的IPO首日平均回报为负。这意味着上市后股价可能承压。

③ Proceeds go to the sponsor / 募资全归发起人

The entire RM2.1 billion raised in the IPO goes to IOI Properties Group — not to the REIT. The REIT itself does not receive fresh capital for growth or debt reduction. This structure primarily benefits the sponsor's balance sheet, essentially monetising its property assets through the listing.

IPO募集的全部RM21亿归IOI置业集团所有——而非REIT本身。REIT未获得任何新资本用于增长或减债。这一结构主要有利于发起人的资产负债表，实质上是通过上市将其物业资产变现。

④ Related-party dependency / 关联方依赖

The hotel master leases are with sponsor subsidiaries, meaning the guaranteed rent ultimately depends on the sponsor's own financial health. With IOI Properties Group carrying approximately RM18 billion in debt — the highest among listed Malaysian developers — this introduces circular risk.

酒店总租赁协议的对手方是发起人的子公司，这意味着保底租金最终取决于发起人自身的财务健康状况。IOI置业集团约RM180亿的债务——为马来西亚上市开发商中最高——引入了循环风险。

⑤ ESG and sustainability concerns / ESG和可持续性顾虑

The IOI group has faced ESG controversies: Greenpeace reports on orangutan habitat destruction (2008, 2015, 2016), Finnwatch allegations of plantation labour issues (2014), and an RSPO sustainability certificate suspension in April 2016 that led several major buyers to cancel contracts (reinstated August 2016). While these relate to the plantation arm rather than the REIT's property assets, they may weigh on ESG-conscious institutional investors.

IOI集团曾面临ESG争议：绿色和平组织关于红毛猩猩栖息地破坏的报告（2008、2015、2016年），Finnwatch关于种植园劳工问题的指控（2014年），以及2016年4月RSPO可持续性认证被暂停导致多家主要买家取消合同（2016年8月恢复）。虽然这些涉及种植园业务而非REIT的地产资产，但可能影响注重ESG的机构投资者。

⑥ Interest rate sensitivity / 利率敏感性

REITs are highly sensitive to interest rate movements. While Bank Negara Malaysia has held the OPR at 2.75% since September 2025, any future tightening would increase the REIT's borrowing costs and potentially make its yield less attractive relative to fixed-income alternatives.

REIT对利率变动高度敏感。尽管马来西亚国家银行自2025年9月以来一直将隔夜政策利率维持在2.75%，但未来任何收紧都将增加REIT的借贷成本，并可能使其收益率相对固定收益替代品的吸引力下降。

7 · Our Verdict / Bottom Line
我们的分析结论

Strengths / 优势

• 6.6% projected yield
• Premium freehold assets
• 97.9% mall occupancy
• Conservative 34.1% gearing
• Revenue nearly doubled in 2 years
• Visible sponsor pipeline

Weaknesses / 劣势

• 77% revenue from one mall
• 0.59× oversubscription
• IPO proceeds bypass REIT
• Sponsor carries RM18B debt
• Related-party hotel leases
• Historical ESG controversies

Analysis (not a recommendation): IOIPG REIT presents a genuine tension. The underlying asset — IOI City Mall — is a high-quality, strongly performing retail property with near-full occupancy. The 6.6% yield is attractive in Malaysia's current rate environment, and gearing is conservative. However, the extreme portfolio concentration, the fact that IPO proceeds entirely benefit the sponsor rather than the REIT, the weak retail demand signalled by the 0.59× oversubscription, and the sponsor's high debt levels all warrant caution.

For income-focused investors with a long-term horizon who are comfortable with the concentration risk, the yield and asset quality have merit. For short-term investors looking for listing-day gains, the weak oversubscription statistics suggest limited upside — historically, similar IPOs on Bursa have averaged negative first-day returns.

The key question is whether the market will reward the REIT's income credentials or punish its structural shortcomings. The answer will likely depend on broader market conditions and interest rate expectations at the time of listing.

分析（非投资建议）：IOIPG REIT呈现出真实的矛盾。底层资产——IOI城市购物中心——是一处高质量、表现强劲的零售物业，出租率接近满租。6.6%的收益率在马来西亚当前利率环境下具有吸引力，杠杆率也较保守。然而，极端的资产组合集中度、IPO募资完全归发起人而非REIT所有、0.59倍超额认购率反映的弱零售需求，以及发起人的高债务水平，都值得谨慎对待。

对于注重收入、持有长期视角且能接受集中风险的投资者而言，收益率和资产质量有其价值。对于寻求短期上市首日收益的投资者，弱超额认购数据暗示上行空间有限——历史上，马来西亚交易所类似IPO首日平均回报为负。

关键问题在于市场将奖励REIT的收入资质，还是惩罚其结构性缺陷。答案很可能取决于上市时的整体市场状况和利率预期。
