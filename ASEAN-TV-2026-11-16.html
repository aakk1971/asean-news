English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. Our top story tonight — a historic milestone for Southeast Asia. ASEAN economic ministers have signed the Digital Economy Framework Agreement, or DEFA, at the 49th ASEAN Summit in Manila. It is being described as the world's first legally binding regional digital economy pact.

Analyst: This is genuinely significant. DEFA covers cross-border data flows, end-to-end digital trade, electronic payments, cybersecurity cooperation, source code protection, and artificial intelligence governance — all within a single binding framework for a market of 670 million people. Philippine Trade Secretary Cristina Roque called it "a landmark achievement, not only for ASEAN, but for the global digital economy," and said the agreement could help double ASEAN's digital economy to US$2 trillion by 2030. Singapore's Deputy Prime Minister Gan Kim Yong, who joined the signing, said it "positions ASEAN at the forefront of the digital economy" and that Singapore will build on this momentum when it assumes the ASEAN chairmanship in 2027.

Lead presenter: What makes this different from previous ASEAN economic agreements?

Analyst: Two things stand out. First, it is legally binding — previous ASEAN digital cooperation has been voluntary or aspirational. Second, it tackles the thorniest issues in digital trade head-on: cross-border data flows, which pit business efficiency against national sovereignty over data, and AI governance, which even the European Union has struggled to codify. The agreement was also signed alongside progress toward an ASEAN–Canada free trade agreement, an upgraded FTA with South Korea, a formal enhancement of the ASEAN Power Grid memorandum, and early-stage work on what could become an ASEAN Green Economy Framework Agreement. The challenge now moves to implementation — ASEAN has a history of ambitious agreements with slow follow-through, and the real test will be whether member states align their domestic regulations to match DEFA's provisions within a credible timeframe.

中文主播稿

主播：各位晚上好。今晚头条——东南亚一个历史性里程碑。东盟经济部长在马尼拉第49届东盟峰会上签署了《数字经济框架协议》（DEFA）。该协议被称为全球首个具法律约束力的区域数字经济协定。

分析师：这确实意义重大。DEFA涵盖跨境数据流通、端到端数字贸易、电子支付、网络安全合作、源代码保护和人工智能治理——所有这些都纳入一个面向6.7亿人口市场的统一约束性框架。菲律宾贸易部长克里斯蒂娜·罗克称其为"不仅对东盟、而且对全球数字经济具有里程碑意义的成就"，并表示该协议有望帮助东盟数字经济到2030年翻番至2万亿美元。参加签字仪式的新加坡副总理颜金勇表示，协议"使东盟处于数字经济的前沿"，新加坡将在2027年接任东盟轮值主席国时延续这一势头。

主播：这与以往的东盟经济协议有何不同？

分析师：有两点突出。第一，它具有法律约束力——此前东盟的数字合作都是自愿性或倡议性的。第二，它直面数字贸易中最棘手的问题：跨境数据流通——这在商业效率与数据主权之间形成张力——以及AI治理——即使欧盟也难以将其法典化。该协议签署的同时，东盟在推进与加拿大自贸协定、升级与韩国的自贸协定、正式强化东盟电力网络谅解备忘录，以及可能成为东盟绿色经济框架协议的前期工作方面也取得了进展。挑战现在转向执行——东盟向来不乏雄心勃勃的协议但后续落实缓慢，真正的考验将是各成员国能否在合理时间框架内协调其国内法规以符合DEFA的条款。

Philippine Economy Slows to 2.1% in Q3 2026 — Weakest Growth in 16 Years as Investment Collapses

菲律宾经济第三季度增速降至2.1%——16年来最弱，投资大幅萎缩

PHILIPPINES · 菲律宾

English presenter script

Lead presenter: We turn now to the Philippines, where the economic picture is deteriorating sharply. GDP growth slowed to just 2.1 per cent in the third quarter of 2026 — the weakest quarterly performance in sixteen years and the fifth consecutive quarter of deceleration.

Analyst: The numbers are stark. Services, the economy's backbone, grew 3.7 per cent and contributed 2.5 percentage points to growth — essentially the only sector pulling its weight. Agriculture contracted 4.8 per cent, dragged down by weather disruptions and elevated input costs. Industry was essentially flat, registering a marginal decline of 0.003 per cent. But the real alarm is in investment. Public construction has fallen for a sixth straight quarter, as investigations into flood-control projects that began in late 2025 have paralysed government infrastructure spending. Capital formation collapsed — in the second quarter alone, investment fell 9.2 per cent.

Lead presenter: And the central bank has been raising rates in this environment?

Analyst: Yes — and that is the central tension. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas raised its policy rate by 25 basis points to 5 per cent in August, then by a further 50 basis points to 5.5 per cent in October. Headline inflation surged to 7.8 per cent in October, driven by energy and transport costs. Moody's Analytics has now slashed its full-year 2026 growth forecast to between 2.3 and 2.4 per cent, citing political uncertainty, persistent inflation, and battered investment sentiment. Economic Planning Secretary Arsenio Balisacan acknowledged that tighter financing conditions weighed on investment during the quarter. The Philippines now finds itself in a difficult policy bind: inflation is too high to cut rates, but the economy is too weak to absorb further tightening. This is the stagflation risk that markets have been pricing in, and it is now materialising in the GDP data.

中文主播稿

主播：接下来关注菲律宾，该国经济形势正在急剧恶化。第三季度GDP增速仅为2.1%——16年来最弱的季度表现，也是连续第五个季度减速。

分析师：数据触目惊心。服务业——经济的支柱——增长3.7%，贡献了2.5个百分点的GDP增长——实质上是唯一发挥作用的部门。农业收缩4.8%，受天气干扰和投入成本上升拖累。工业基本持平，仅录得0.003%的微幅下降。但真正令人警觉的是投资。公共建设已连续第六个季度下降，2025年底启动的防洪工程调查使政府基建支出陷入瘫痪。资本形成大幅萎缩——仅第二季度投资就下降了9.2%。

主播：在这种环境下央行还在加息？

分析师：是的——这正是核心矛盾所在。菲律宾央行8月加息25个基点至5%，10月又加息50个基点至5.5%。10月份整体通胀率飙升至7.8%，主要由能源和交通成本推动。穆迪分析已将2026年全年增长预测大幅下调至2.3%至2.4%，理由是政治不确定性、持续通胀和投资信心受损。经济规划部长阿塞尼奥·巴利萨坎承认，更紧的融资条件拖累了季度投资表现。菲律宾目前陷入困难的政策困境：通胀过高无法降息，但经济又太弱无法承受进一步收紧。这就是市场一直在定价的滞胀风险，而现在它正在GDP数据中成为现实。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Turning now to Indonesia, where the economic data is painting a very different picture from the country's battered stock market. Third-quarter GDP grew 5.47 per cent year on year — accelerating from 5.29 per cent in the second quarter and comfortably beating the market consensus forecast of 5.2 per cent.

Analyst: The acceleration was driven primarily by robust manufacturing activity. Indonesia's expanded mandatory biodiesel programme — which requires a higher blend of palm oil-derived fuel — has turbo-charged palm oil processing and related industrial output. Quarter on quarter, growth came in at 1.6 per cent, beating the 1.3 per cent forecast though decelerating from the 3.7 per cent sequential surge in the second quarter. Finance Minister Suahasil Nazara said growth could slightly exceed the government's 5.4 per cent budget target and reach 5.5 per cent for the full year.

Lead presenter: And yet the Jakarta Composite Index is still down roughly 45 per cent from its January peak?

Analyst: That is the extraordinary disconnect in Indonesia right now. The real economy is performing strongly — GDP above 5 per cent, manufacturing expanding, government revenue on track — but equity markets are pricing in the MSCI reclassification risk we have been covering extensively. Foreign investors have recorded roughly 98 trillion rupiah in net selling year to date. The IHSG at around 5,050 is trading at valuations last seen in 2017, despite corporate earnings that are broadly in line with or above expectations. This divergence cannot persist indefinitely. Either the market will re-rate if MSCI pulls back from reclassification, or the prolonged capital outflow will eventually begin to drag on the real economy through tighter financial conditions and reduced business confidence. The government's challenge is managing this disconnect long enough for its governance reforms to satisfy MSCI's demands without the market stress itself becoming the story.

中文主播稿

主播：现在转向印尼，这里的经济数据描绘出与该国遭受重创的股市截然不同的画面。第三季度GDP同比增长5.47%——从第二季度的5.29%加速，轻松超出5.2%的市场共识预期。

分析师：加速主要由强劲的制造业活动驱动。印尼扩大的强制性生物柴油计划——要求更高比例的棕榈油衍生燃料混合——大幅提振了棕榈油加工和相关工业产出。环比增长1.6%，超出1.3%的预期，但较第二季度3.7%的环比激增有所放缓。财政部长苏阿哈西尔·纳扎拉表示，增长可能略超政府5.4%的预算目标，全年有望达到5.5%。

主播：但雅加达综合指数仍较1月高点下跌约45%？

分析师：这正是印尼目前异常的割裂。实体经济表现强劲——GDP超过5%、制造业扩张、政府收入达标——但股票市场正在为我们持续报道的MSCI重新分类风险定价。外国投资者年初至今累计净卖出约98万亿印尼盾。IHSG在约5,050点的估值水平回到2017年，尽管企业盈利大致符合或超出预期。这种割裂不可能无限期持续。要么MSCI撤回重新分类后市场重新估值，要么持续的资本外流最终将通过更紧的金融条件和商业信心下降开始拖累实体经济。政府的挑战是在市场压力本身成为主题之前，管理好这种割裂，争取足够时间让治理改革满足MSCI的要求。

Vietnam's Trade Surpasses US$1 Trillion for First Time as FDI Surges 76% and Government Targets 10% Growth

越南贸易额首次突破1万亿美元，FDI激增76%，政府目标10%增长

ASEAN BUSINESS BRIEF
VIETNAM · 越南
SCRIPT READY · VIDEO NOT RENDERED
稿件已就绪 · 视频未生成

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Vietnam continues to generate eye-catching economic numbers. In the first ten months of 2026, the country's total merchandise trade surpassed one trillion US dollars for the first time in history — just nineteen years after trade was barely US$100 billion when Vietnam joined the WTO in 2007.

Analyst: The figures are remarkable. Total trade of US$1.01 trillion represents a 32.3 per cent year-on-year increase, with exports at US$499 billion and imports at approximately US$511 billion. But the real headline within the headlines is foreign direct investment. Registered FDI surged 76.4 per cent to US$50.36 billion in the first ten months, while disbursed FDI — the money actually flowing into the economy — rose 12.1 per cent to US$21.07 billion. The government is targeting 10 per cent GDP growth for the full year. Deputy Prime Minister Nguyen Van Thang acknowledged that this requires approximately 12.5 per cent growth in the fourth quarter — ambitious, but said it was "not beyond reach."

Lead presenter: The government has also introduced a package of stimulus measures?

Analyst: Yes. Effective 1 November, Vietnam's state electricity company EVN cut average retail electricity prices by 2 per cent. The special consumption tax on petrol has been reduced to zero through March 2027. And the 2 per cent VAT reduction on eligible goods and services continues through year-end. These are targeted measures to ease cost-of-living pressures while maintaining export competitiveness. The strategic risk remains concentration: Vietnam's US$140 billion surplus with the United States makes it a prominent target for trade rebalancing, and the Section 301 duties imposed in July are already testing that vulnerability. The 76 per cent surge in registered FDI suggests global manufacturers are still choosing Vietnam as a production base despite these risks — but the gap between registered and disbursed FDI indicates that converting commitments to actual factory floors takes time, and policy uncertainty can slow that conversion significantly.

中文主播稿

主播：越南继续创造引人注目的经济数据。2026年前十个月，该国商品贸易总额历史上首次突破1万亿美元——距2007年越南加入WTO时贸易额仅约1,000亿美元，仅过了19年。

分析师：数据令人瞩目。贸易总额1.01万亿美元，同比增长32.3%，出口4,990亿美元，进口约5,110亿美元。但数据中的真正焦点是外国直接投资。前十个月注册FDI激增76.4%至503.6亿美元，而实际到位FDI——真正流入经济的资金——增长12.1%至210.7亿美元。政府将全年GDP增长目标定为10%。副总理阮文胜承认这需要第四季度约12.5%的增长——雄心勃勃，但他说"并非遥不可及"。

主播：政府还推出了一揽子刺激措施？

分析师：是的。自11月1日起，越南国家电力公司EVN将平均零售电价下调2%。汽油特别消费税降至零，持续至2027年3月。对符合条件商品和服务的2%增值税减免持续至年底。这些是在维持出口竞争力的同时缓解民生成本压力的定向措施。战略风险仍是集中度问题：越南对美1,400亿美元顺差使其成为贸易再平衡的显著目标，7月份实施的301条款关税已在考验这一脆弱性。注册FDI激增76%表明全球制造商仍在选择越南作为生产基地，尽管存在这些风险——但注册与到位FDI之间的差距表明，将承诺转化为实际工厂需要时间，而政策不确定性可能显著减缓这种转化。

ASEAN BUSINESS BRIEF

English presenter script

Lead presenter: There are interesting developments out of Thailand. The Bank of Thailand says revised manufacturing data could lift the country's growth numbers significantly — potentially from 2.4 per cent to as high as 2.9 per cent.

Analyst: This requires some context. The Office of Industrial Economics has introduced a revised Manufacturing Production Index with broader coverage of electronics — specifically adding integrated circuits, other electronic components, notebooks, laptops, optical transceivers, and uninterruptible power supplies. Governor Vitai Ratanakorn was careful to explain that this reflects better measurement of existing activity, not a sudden jump in actual production. The revision could add between 0.1 and 0.5 percentage points to headline growth, but official third-quarter figures from the National Economic and Social Development Council have not yet been published to confirm the effect.

Lead presenter: And the export picture has also changed dramatically?

Analyst: Yes. The Bank of Thailand raised its 2026 export growth forecast from 14 per cent to 22 per cent — driven overwhelmingly by demand linked to artificial intelligence investment and data centre construction across the region. This is consistent with the broader ASEAN story of AI-driven demand reshaping trade patterns. However, Governor Vitai issued a pointed warning that the recovery remains "K-shaped" — strong exporters and large investors are thriving, while households and small businesses face pressure from rising oil and food prices. He said growth of about 2 per cent "may not generate enough income for many households and businesses." On the trade front, Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul said a proposed Thai-US trade agreement has reached the legal review stage, with Thailand expecting a US tariff rate of approximately 19 per cent, possibly lower. The resolution of trade uncertainty with the United States would be a significant positive catalyst for Thai business sentiment, though existing tariffs cannot be unwound quickly even with a deal in place.

中文主播稿

主播：泰国传来有趣的动态。泰国央行表示，修订后的制造业数据可能大幅提升该国增长数字——有望从2.4%提高到2.9%。

分析师：这需要一些背景说明。工业经济办公室推出了修订版制造业生产指数，扩大了电子产品的覆盖范围——具体增加了集成电路、其他电子元件、笔记本电脑、光收发器和不间断电源。行长维泰·拉塔纳哥仔细解释说，这反映的是对现有活动更好的衡量，而非实际产出的突然跳升。修订可能为GDP增长增加0.1至0.5个百分点，但国家经济和社会发展委员会的官方第三季度数据尚未公布以确认实际效果。

主播：出口前景也发生了戏剧性变化？

分析师：是的。泰国央行将2026年出口增长预测从14%上调至22%——主要由与人工智能投资和区域数据中心建设相关的需求驱动。这与AI驱动需求重塑贸易格局的更广泛东盟叙事一致。然而，维泰行长发出尖锐警告，称复苏仍呈"K型"——强劲的出口商和大型投资者蓬勃发展，而家庭和小企业则承受着油价和食品价格上涨的压力。他说约2%的增长"可能无法为许多家庭和企业创造足够的收入"。贸易方面，阿努廷·查恩维拉坤总理表示，拟议中的泰美贸易协定已进入法律审查阶段，泰国预期美方关税税率约为19%，可能更低。与美国贸易不确定性的化解将对泰国商业信心构成重大积极催化，不过即使达成协议，现有关税也无法迅速取消。

Malaysia's Economy Set for Third Consecutive Year Above 5% as World Bank Urges Structural Reforms

马来西亚经济有望连续第三年增长超5%，世界银行敦促结构性改革

ASEAN BUSINESS BRIEF
MALAYSIA · 马来西亚
SCRIPT READY · VIDEO NOT RENDERED

English presenter script

Lead presenter: And finally to Malaysia, where the economic story continues to be one of remarkable consistency. The country is on track for a third consecutive year of growth above 5 per cent — a performance unmatched by any major ASEAN economy except Vietnam.

Analyst: Third-quarter GDP came in at 5.3 per cent, beating market expectations and extending a run of strong quarters. Bank Negara Malaysia held the overnight policy rate at 2.75 per cent in November, maintaining its year-long hold, with the governor explicitly stating there is no need to tighten. Headline inflation is averaging just 1.8 per cent — the lowest in ASEAN — with core inflation at approximately 2 per cent. That combination of above-5 per cent growth and sub-2 per cent inflation is genuinely exceptional in the current global environment.

Lead presenter: But the World Bank has a note of caution?

Analyst: Indeed. At the launch of its October 2026 Malaysia Economic Monitor on 11 November, the World Bank's Zafer Mustafaoğlu said the near-term outlook remains uncertain and that Malaysia is exposed through its trade and financial channels as a highly open economy. The Bank specifically called for deepening structural reforms — describing them as "not optional but essential for sustainable growth and better jobs." The focus is on small and medium enterprises, which the report says must shift toward higher productivity, technology adoption, intellectual property creation, and innovation. SME financing, the World Bank argues, needs to move beyond basic working capital toward high-risk expansion capital for automation, digitalisation, AI, and green transitions. Malaysia is on the brink of high-income status — but the Bank's message is clear: getting there and staying there require different policy approaches, and the reforms needed for the latter must start now while the economy's strong performance provides a cushion for adjustment.

中文主播稿

主播：最后来看马来西亚，这里的经济故事继续展现出非凡的稳定性。该国有望连续第三年实现超5%的增长——在主要东盟经济体中除越南外无人能及。

分析师：第三季度GDP录得5.3%，超出市场预期，延续了一连串强劲季度。马来西亚国家银行11月维持隔夜政策利率2.75%不变，延续其长达一年的按兵不动，行长明确表示无需收紧。整体通胀率平均仅1.8%——东盟最低——核心通胀约2%。在当前全球环境下，超5%增长与低于2%通胀的组合确实非常出色。

主播：但世界银行有一些提醒？

分析师：确实如此。世界银行在11月11日发布2026年10月版《马来西亚经济监测》时，其东南亚区域负责人扎费尔·穆斯塔法奥卢表示，近期前景仍不确定，作为高度开放的经济体，马来西亚通过贸易和金融渠道面临风险。世行特别呼吁深化结构性改革——称其"不是可选项，而是实现可持续增长和更好就业的必要条件"。重点在中小企业，报告称它们必须转向更高生产率、技术采用、知识产权创造和创新。世行认为，中小企业融资需要从基本运营资金转向用于自动化、数字化、AI和绿色转型的高风险扩展资本。马来西亚处于高收入国家地位的门槛上——但世行的信息很明确：达到和维持这一地位需要不同的政策路径，后者所需的改革必须趁经济强劲表现为调整提供缓冲的当下就开始。
