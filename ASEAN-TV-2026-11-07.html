Singapore's largest bank DBS Group reported third-quarter net profit of two point eight one billion Singapore dollars, down five percent year on year. The decline was driven by lower market trading income amid unfavourable equity conditions.

However, the nine-month picture is strong: net profit reached a record eight point eight two billion, up two percent, on total income of seventeen point nine billion — also a record. Fee income and treasury customer sales grew by double digits, led by wealth management.

English presenter script

Indonesia's Energy Ministry has raised its twenty-twenty-six nickel downstream export target to forty-five billion US dollars, up twelve and a half percent from last year's forty billion. Since banning raw nickel ore exports in twenty-twenty, the country has forced investors to build smelters domestically, producing stainless steel, ferronickel, and increasingly battery-grade nickel for electric vehicles.

The government is now studying whether to extend a similar export ban to tin, which could affect global supply of the metal used in electronics soldering.

Our analysis: Indonesia's nickel downstreaming is arguably the most successful industrial policy in Southeast Asia this decade — a template that other resource-rich nations may follow.

中文主播稿
