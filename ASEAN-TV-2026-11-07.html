Good afternoon. This is ASEAN Business Brief for November seventh. We begin with the biggest story across the region.

Asian equity markets suffered their largest single-day losses in months yesterday. South Korea's KOSPI plunged eight point six percent, triggering a trading halt. Japan's Nikkei fell about six percent. Taiwan's index dropped more than eight percent. The selloff followed a five-and-a-half percent decline on the Nasdaq overnight.

Singapore Airlines has unveiled its largest ever cabin investment — two billion Singapore dollars — covering all four classes from First to Economy. The new First Class features private suites with eighty-inch beds that can convert into doubles. Business Class gets seventy-nine-inch flat beds with privacy doors.
