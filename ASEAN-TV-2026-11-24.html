Vietnam Trade Turnover Tops US$1 Trillion for First Time as Exports Surge 27.5% in Ten Months

越南贸易额首次突破1万亿美元，前十月出口飙升27.5%

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. Our top story tonight — a historic trade milestone. Vietnam's total merchandise trade turnover has surpassed US$1 trillion for the first time, reaching US$1,010.55 billion in the first ten months of 2026 — a 32.3 per cent year-on-year increase. Exports totalled US$499.24 billion, up 27.5 per cent, while imports reached US$511.31 billion, up 37.4 per cent.

Analyst: This is a transformative milestone that places Vietnam — a country with a GDP of approximately US$460 billion — in the same trade-volume league as economies several times its size. The trade-to-GDP ratio now exceeds 200 per cent, making Vietnam one of the most trade-dependent economies in the world alongside Singapore and Hong Kong. But the composition reveals both strength and vulnerability. Foreign-invested enterprises account for 81.2 per cent of exports at US$405.31 billion, growing 33 per cent, while domestic firms contribute just 18.8 per cent at US$93.93 billion, growing only 8.2 per cent. The trillion-dollar achievement is overwhelmingly driven by multinationals — Samsung, Foxconn, LG, Intel — using Vietnam as a production base. Domestic firms are being left behind.

Lead presenter: The bilateral surplus with the United States is enormous.

Analyst: US$140.10 billion — this is now larger than the US trade deficit with Japan and approaching the scale of the US deficit with the European Union. It makes Vietnam a potential target for US trade enforcement. The existing Section 301 duty of 12.5 per cent on Vietnamese-origin products is a direct consequence. Vietnam also runs a US$134.94 billion deficit with China, a US$48.63 billion deficit with South Korea, and a US$16.93 billion deficit with ASEAN partners — confirming its role as an intermediate processing hub that imports components from Northeast Asia and exports finished goods to Western markets. The cumulative ten-month trade deficit of US$12.07 billion reflects the import intensity of this model. The key question is whether the US$140 billion surplus will trigger additional American trade actions, and whether US Customs will increase enforcement of origin rules to identify Chinese goods being transshipped through Vietnam to circumvent tariffs.

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Singapore now — where October trade data has produced extraordinary numbers. Non-oil domestic exports surged 79.8 per cent year-on-year, accelerating from 68.3 per cent in September, while the merchandise trade surplus ballooned to SG$19.3 billion — more than double September's SG$9.03 billion and far exceeding the Trading Economics forecast of SG$7.90 billion.

Analyst: These are exceptional figures by any standard. The pharmaceutical sector drove the non-electronic outperformance with a 253.1 per cent surge, though this reflects the lumpy, batch-driven nature of pharmaceutical manufacturing in Singapore — large shipments of active pharmaceutical ingredients and finished drugs can produce dramatic single-month spikes that do not necessarily indicate a sustained acceleration. What is structurally significant is the electronics performance: disk media products surged 189.6 per cent to approximately SG$1.1 billion, personal computers rose 81 per cent to approximately SG$700 million, and integrated circuits increased 53.1 per cent — all reflecting genuine demand from the global AI infrastructure buildout.

Lead presenter: Is this sustainable?

Analyst: The breadth is encouraging. Cumulative NODX growth for the first ten months stands at 33.6 per cent, accelerating from 27.6 per cent through September, which suggests momentum is building rather than fading. The SG$19.3 billion surplus reflects Singapore's position as a net value-added exporter — it imports raw materials and components, adds high-value processing in pharmaceuticals, semiconductors, and precision engineering, and exports finished products at substantially higher prices. The 44.9 per cent import growth supports this reading — imports are rising because manufacturing output is rising. The key risk is whether the pharmaceutical surge is repeatable and whether AI-related electronics demand will sustain into 2027. But the expansion across electronics, pharmaceuticals, and specialised machinery means Singapore's export engine is operating on multiple cylinders, reducing the risk of a single-sector slowdown producing a sharp correction.

中文主播稿

主播：转向新加坡——10月贸易数据产生了非同寻常的数字。非石油国内出口同比飙升79.8%，较9月的68.3%进一步加速，商品贸易顺差膨胀至193亿新元——是9月90.3亿新元的两倍多，远超Trading Economics预测的79亿新元。

分析师：以任何标准衡量，这些都是卓越的数字。制药行业以253.1%的飙升驱动了非电子产品的优异表现，尽管这反映了新加坡制药制造业的批次驱动特性——大批量活性药物成分和成品药物的出货可能产生戏剧性的单月峰值，不一定表示持续加速。更具结构性意义的是电子产品表现：磁盘媒体产品飙升189.6%至约11亿新元，个人电脑增长81%至约7亿新元，集成电路增长53.1%——均反映了全球AI基础设施建设的真实需求。

主播：这种增长可持续吗？

分析师：广度令人鼓舞。前十个月NODX累计增长33.6%，从9月的27.6%加速，表明势头正在增强而非消退。193亿新元的顺差反映了新加坡作为净增值出口国的地位——进口原材料和零部件，在制药、半导体和精密工程领域进行高附加值加工，以大幅溢价出口成品。44.9%的进口增长支持这一解读——进口增长因为制造业产出增长。关键风险在于制药飙升是否可重复，以及AI相关电子需求能否延续至2027年。但跨电子、制药和专用机械的扩张意味着新加坡的出口引擎正在多个气缸上运转，降低了单一行业放缓导致急剧回调的风险。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To Indonesia now — where President Prabowo Subianto has returned from China with memoranda of understanding worth approximately US$71.8 billion. The agreements were exchanged in Shenzhen on 18 November on the sidelines of APEC. They cover smart city development, manufacturing and technology, infrastructure financing, and coal-to-ethanol technology.

Analyst: The headline figure demands careful disaggregation. The largest component — US$50 billion — is a commitment for ten "future smart city" sites across Indonesia, signed between a Danantara subsidiary and a consortium led by China's Shaanxi province. Infrastructure and smart city MoUs of this scale historically have a very low conversion rate from signed agreement to actual capital deployment. These are aspirational frameworks for projects that may take a decade or more to materialise, if they materialise at all. The more commercially concrete commitments are Hisense's US$10 billion for manufacturing and R&D, a US$10 billion infrastructure financing partnership between Danantara and China's State Development and Investment Corporation, and a US$1.8 billion coal-to-ethanol project with Pertamina and Beijing Petrochemical Engineering.

Lead presenter: What is the strategic context?

Analyst: The timing is significant. Indonesia is pursuing Chinese investment while its capital markets are under severe stress — the Jakarta Composite Index down 41.59 per cent year-to-date, MSCI reclassification risk looming, and 61 trillion rupiah in net foreign selling. The US$71.8 billion MoU total provides a counter-narrative: even as portfolio investors flee Indonesian equities, the government can point to massive bilateral commitments as evidence of long-term confidence. But MoUs and portfolio flows serve different functions — MoUs signal political alignment and future intent, while portfolio flows reflect current risk pricing. Meanwhile, the US-ASEAN Business Council has separately called on Indonesia to strengthen contractual predictability, citing concerns about production-sharing contract changes and shifting import licensing requirements. This highlights a two-track investment environment where the nature and reliability of foreign capital varies significantly by source country. Including the 35 additional deals on 20 November, the total China visit commitments reach approximately US$86.8 billion — but the question of conversion to actual deployed capital remains the critical unknown.

国家经济社会发展委员会上调2026年GDP预测至2.5%，但K型复苏加大科技出口与困境中小企业之间的分化

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Thailand's economic planning body, the NESDC, has raised its 2026 GDP growth forecast to 2.5 per cent from an August estimate of 2.2 per cent. But behind that headline upgrade is a widening K-shaped divide that is becoming ASEAN's most instructive case study in how GDP growth can obscure reality on the ground.

Analyst: The upgrade needs qualification. Bangkok Bank chief economist Kobsak Pootrakool points out that part of the increase reflects a routine five-year revision to the Manufacturing Production Index weights — the new weighting better captures smart electronics and AI-linked supply chains. He estimates this reweighting alone adds approximately 0.5 percentage points, effectively turning a 2.0 per cent estimate into 2.5 per cent. The Bank of Thailand's own forecast of 2.4 per cent, described as below the economy's potential, supports this interpretation. Seasonally adjusted quarter-on-quarter output was flat in Q2 and grew only 0.3 per cent in Q3 — the economy is barely expanding on a sequential basis.

Lead presenter: What does the K-shape look like in practice?

Analyst: The upper arm of the K is electronics and AI-related investment — data centres, semiconductor supply chains, smart electronics exports — connected to global demand that bypasses domestic constraints. The lower arm is domestically oriented sectors: SME lending is contracting, the lower end of the property market is sluggish, purchasing power is weak in provinces outside the main tourist destinations, and lower-income households have little disposable income. CIMB Thai Bank's research head Amonthep Chawla suggests Thailand's underlying potential may have fallen below 2 per cent unless productivity improves — meaning the economy is growing near its ceiling not because it's overheating but because the ceiling itself has lowered. The comparison with regional peers is unflattering: Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, and Vietnam are all growing between 5 and 6 per cent. Thailand at 2.5 per cent is an outlier that cannot be explained by external conditions alone. And the Bank of Thailand warns that tourism faces exposure to a China slowdown — if Chinese households cut spending, Thai tourism would feel the impact immediately.

中文主播稿

主播：泰国经济规划机构NESDC已将2026年GDP增长预测从8月的2.2%上调至2.5%。但在标题上调的背后，日益扩大的K型分化正在成为东盟最具启示性的案例研究——说明GDP增长如何掩盖实地情况。

分析师：上调需要加以说明。曼谷银行首席经济学家科布萨克·普特拉古指出，部分增加反映了对制造业生产指数权重的例行五年修订——新权重更好地捕捉了智能电子和AI相关供应链。他估计仅重新加权就增加了约0.5个百分点，实际上将2.0%的估计变成了2.5%。泰国银行自己2.4%的预测（被描述为低于经济潜力）支持这一解读。经季节性调整的环比产出在第二季度持平，第三季度仅增长0.3%——经济在环比基础上几乎没有扩张。

主播：K型分化在实践中是什么样的？

分析师：K的上臂是电子和AI相关投资——数据中心、半导体供应链、智能电子出口——连接着绕过国内约束的全球需求。下臂是面向国内的部门：中小企业贷款正在收缩，低端房地产市场低迷，主要旅游目的地以外省份的购买力薄弱，低收入家庭几乎没有可支配收入。CIMB泰国银行研究主管阿蒙泰·查瓦拉认为泰国的基础潜力可能已降至2%以下（除非生产率改善）——意味着经济正在接近其上限增长，不是因为过热而是因为上限本身降低了。与区域同行的比较令人尴尬：马来西亚、印尼、新加坡和越南的增长都在5%至6%之间。泰国2.5%是一个不能仅靠外部条件解释的异常值。泰国银行还警告旅游业面临中国放缓的风险——如果中国家庭削减支出，泰国旅游业将立即感受到冲击。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: To the Philippines now — where the stagflation picture is deepening. Headline inflation accelerated to 7.8 per cent in October, up from 7.2 per cent in September, while core inflation — stripping out food and energy — rose to 5.4 per cent from 4.8 per cent. This against a backdrop of Q3 GDP growth of just 2.1 per cent, the weakest quarterly result since the pandemic.

Analyst: The core inflation acceleration from 4.8 to 5.4 per cent is the most alarming figure because it confirms that price pressures have become generalised across the economy. When core inflation is itself accelerating, businesses are passing higher input costs through to consumers across a wide range of goods and services, and inflation expectations are becoming unmoored. BPI lead economist Emilio Neri put it most clearly: "Rate hikes don't grow rice, but pausing could harvest inflation." The BSP is fighting a supply-driven inflation shock with a demand-side tool. Rate hikes cannot increase rice production, fix typhoon-damaged supply chains, or reduce transport costs — but failing to hike risks allowing inflation to become self-reinforcing through wage-price spirals.

Lead presenter: How does this compare with the rest of ASEAN?

Analyst: The contrast is now extreme. Malaysia has 1.9 per cent inflation with 5.5 per cent growth. The Philippines has 7.8 per cent inflation with 2.1 per cent growth — four times the inflation at less than half the growth. Only the Philippines is in the stagflationary quadrant. The negative real policy rate of minus 2.3 per cent — the benchmark at 5.50 per cent minus inflation at 7.8 — explains why 125 basis points of hikes have failed to cool prices: in real terms, money is still cheap. Barclays forecasts inflation could breach 8.0 per cent in November and sees the possibility of another 50-basis-point hike at the BSP's December meeting. The World Bank has separately urged structural reforms in agricultural supply chains, stating that food price inflation is a supply-side problem requiring supply-side solutions. The PSEi has fallen 28 per cent year-to-date, trading at 9.5 times forward earnings — historically cheap, but cheapness alone does not attract capital when the macro trajectory is deteriorating.

中文主播稿

主播：转向菲律宾——滞胀形势正在加深。整体通胀在10月加速至7.8%，高于9月的7.2%，而核心通胀——剔除食品和能源——从4.8%上升至5.4%。背景是第三季度GDP仅增长2.1%，疫情以来最弱的季度表现。

分析师：核心通胀从4.8%加速至5.4%是最令人担忧的数字，因为它确认价格压力已在整个经济中全面化。当核心通胀本身也在加速时，企业正将更高的投入成本传导给各类商品和服务的消费者，通胀预期正在脱锚。BPI首席经济学家埃米利奥·内里说得最清楚："加息不能种出大米，但暂停可能收获通胀。"BSP正在用需求侧工具对抗供给驱动的通胀冲击。加息无法增加大米产量、修复台风破坏的供应链或降低运输成本——但不加息则有风险让通胀通过工资-价格螺旋变得自我强化。

主播：与东盟其他国家相比如何？

分析师：对比现在极为悬殊。马来西亚通胀1.9%伴随5.5%增长。菲律宾通胀7.8%伴随2.1%增长——四倍的通胀率、不到一半的增长。只有菲律宾处于滞胀象限。负2.3%的实际政策利率——基准5.50%减去通胀7.8%——解释了为何125个基点的加息未能冷却价格：实际上资金仍然便宜。巴克莱预测11月通胀可能突破8.0%，并认为BSP可能在12月会议上再次加息50个基点。世界银行另外敦促农业供应链结构性改革，指出食品价格通胀是需要供给侧解决方案的供给侧问题。PSEi年初至今下跌28%，前瞻市盈率9.5倍——历史性低廉，但当宏观轨迹恶化时，仅靠估值低廉并不能吸引资本。

Lead presenter: And finally to Malaysia — where the government is tackling the most consequential infrastructure policy question facing any ASEAN economy today. Data centres already account for 9.28 per cent of Malaysia's total electricity consumption and are heading toward 21 per cent as the 117 approved projects reach full operation. The government is planning a separate wholesale electricity market specifically for data centre operators, targeted for launch by 2029 or 2030.

Analyst: To put this in perspective, the entire residential sector in Malaysia consumes approximately 20 per cent of national electricity. Data centres are on track to match household consumption within two to three years — that is an extraordinary transformation for a sector that was negligible just three years ago. The separate wholesale market is the most significant structural reform. By creating a parallel procurement channel at market prices, Malaysia is segmenting its electricity market into two tiers: a subsidised tier for households and traditional industry, and a market-priced tier for data centres. This protects the fiscal cost of electricity subsidies while allowing data centre operators to pay the full cost of their power consumption.

Lead presenter: What other policy tools is the government deploying?

Analyst: Several important ones. First, they have stopped approving data centres that are not specifically designed for artificial intelligence workloads — effectively directing the pipeline toward higher-value computing rather than conventional cloud storage, since AI clusters generate higher revenue per megawatt. Second, they are addressing "phantom demand" — where approved projects reserve grid capacity but delay construction, blocking other users from accessing the grid. Third, states with insufficient water supply for cooling have been prohibited from hosting new facilities. And fourth, mandatory renewable energy quotas are being imposed on data centre operators. This is the most sophisticated regulatory framework for data centre power management in ASEAN. The challenge is execution — building generation and transmission capacity fast enough to serve both the existing 117 approved projects and the growing needs of other sectors, while transitioning an increasing share to renewable sources. The question of whether the AI-only approval policy might deter conventional cloud operators and redirect them to neighbouring countries with less restrictive policies is one worth watching.

中文主播稿

主播：最后转向马来西亚——政府正在应对任何东盟经济体面临的最重要的基础设施政策问题。数据中心已占马来西亚总电力消耗的9.28%，随着117个已批准项目全面运营，这一比例将向21%迈进。政府正计划专门为数据中心运营商设立独立的批发电力市场，目标在2029年或2030年启动。

分析师：从背景来看，马来西亚整个住宅部门消耗约20%的全国电力。数据中心有望在两到三年内追平家庭用电量——对于一个三年前还微不足道的行业来说，这是非凡的转变。独立批发市场是最重要的结构性改革。通过创建以市场价格运作的平行采购渠道，马来西亚实际上将其电力市场分为两个层级：为家庭和传统工业提供的补贴层级，以及为数据中心提供的市场定价层级。这保护了电力补贴的财政成本，同时允许数据中心运营商支付其电力消耗的全部成本。

主播：政府还部署了哪些政策工具？

分析师：有几个重要措施。第一，已停止批准非专门用于人工智能工作负载的数据中心——实际上将管线引导向更高价值的计算而非传统云存储，因为AI集群每兆瓦产生更高收入。第二，正在解决"幽灵需求"——已批准项目预留电网容量但延迟建设，阻止其他用户接入电网。第三，水资源不足的州已被禁止承接新设施。第四，对数据中心运营商施加强制性可再生能源配额。这是东盟最成熟的数据中心电力管理监管框架。挑战在于执行——足够快地建设发电和输电能力以同时服务现有117个已批准项目和其他行业不断增长的需求，同时将越来越多的份额过渡到可再生能源。仅限AI的审批政策是否会阻止传统云运营商并将其重定向到政策限制较少的邻国，这个问题值得关注。
