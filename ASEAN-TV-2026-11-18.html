Malaysia's Economy Grows 5.5% in Q3 2026, Beating Estimates as Tech Exports Surge 66.9%

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Good evening. We begin in Malaysia, where Bank Negara has released the final third-quarter GDP figures — and they beat expectations. The economy grew 5.5 per cent year-on-year, above the advance estimate of 5.3 per cent, though moderating from 6.0 per cent in the second quarter. For the first nine months of 2026, growth averaged 5.6 per cent.

Analyst: The upward revision from 5.3 to 5.5 per cent is significant because it confirms that Malaysia's economic momentum remains robust despite the quarterly deceleration. The story within the data is technology exports: electrical and electronic goods shipments jumped 66.9 per cent year-on-year, reflecting Malaysia's deepening role in the global AI and semiconductor supply chain. Total exports grew 5.7 per cent. On the demand side, Bank Negara attributed the moderation from Q2's 6.0 per cent largely to base effects — higher cash transfers in Q3 2025 had boosted consumption that quarter, making the year-on-year comparison tougher. Quarter-on-quarter, growth was 1.5 per cent, down from 2.5 per cent previously.

Lead presenter: What is the outlook from here?

Analyst: Governor Datuk Sri Abdul Rasheed Ghaffour struck a confident tone, saying the economy is poised to enter 2027 "from a position of strength." The government's full-year forecast is for growth near the upper end of the 4.8 to 5.3 per cent range — meaning Malaysia is on track to exceed 5 per cent for a third consecutive year. Headline inflation is projected at 1.5 to 2.5 per cent for 2026, though it may rise toward late 2026 and early 2027 as higher input costs pass through. BNM held its benchmark rate unchanged for the eighth consecutive meeting, but HSBC now forecasts a rate hike in the first quarter of 2027 as growth and inflation dynamics evolve. The 2027 GDP forecast is 4.2 to 5.2 per cent, with exports driven by global demand for tech products and key commodities, plus travel and ICT services. Investment will be supported by multi-year private and public projects and the realisation of the massive approved investment pipeline we reported on yesterday.

English presenter script

Lead presenter: A surprise from Jakarta this afternoon. Bank Indonesia has raised its benchmark seven-day reverse repo rate by 25 basis points to 6.00 per cent — its highest since January 2025. Most economists had expected the central bank to hold steady at 5.75 per cent.

Analyst: This is the fourth rate hike since May, bringing cumulative tightening to 125 basis points from the 4.75 per cent floor. A survey of eleven analysts showed 54.55 per cent expected a hold, while only 36.36 per cent correctly forecast today's 25-basis-point move. Governor Destry Damayanti said the decision was aimed at stabilising the rupiah amid heightened global uncertainty and keeping inflation anchored within the 2.5 per cent plus or minus one percentage point target for 2026 and 2027. The deposit facility rate was raised to 5.25 per cent and the lending facility rate to 6.75 per cent.

Lead presenter: The rupiah has been under enormous pressure this year?

Analyst: Enormous is the right word. The rupiah hit a record low of 18,285 per dollar today, and has lost approximately 8.7 per cent in 2026 — the worst performance among emerging Asian currencies. The currency slide is driven by a perfect storm of factors: the strong US dollar, with the Fed signalling possible further rate hikes; MSCI's consultation on potentially reclassifying Indonesia from Emerging Market to Standalone status, which we covered extensively last week; and political uncertainty following the resignation of Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka. Bank Indonesia has already intervened heavily in both offshore and domestic markets and offered currency-hedging incentives, but the pressure has overwhelmed those tools. Capital Economics has warned that two more rate hikes may be needed. The good news is that the real economy remains resilient — Q3 GDP grew 5.47 per cent and October inflation was a manageable 3.23 per cent with core at 2.56 per cent — but the disconnect between Indonesia's strong economic fundamentals and its battered financial markets continues to widen. The Jakarta Composite Index fell another 1.07 per cent today to 5,247.88.

分析师：这是5月以来的第四次加息，累计收紧幅度从4.75%的低点达到125个基点。对11位分析师的调查显示，54.55%预期维持不变，仅36.36%正确预测了今天的25个基点加息。行长德斯特里·达马扬蒂表示，此举旨在在全球不确定性加剧的背景下稳定卢比，并将通胀锚定在2026年和2027年2.5%正负一个百分点的目标范围内。存款便利利率上调至5.25%，贷款便利利率上调至6.75%。

分析师："巨大"是恰当的形容。卢比今天跌至1美元兑18,285卢比的历史新低，2026年已贬值约8.7%——在亚洲新兴货币中表现最差。汇率下滑源于多重因素的完美风暴：美元走强且美联储暗示可能进一步加息；MSCI就可能将印尼从新兴市场重新分类为独立市场的磋商——我们上周已详细报道；以及副总统吉布兰辞职带来的政治不确定性。印尼央行已在离岸和在岸市场大力干预，并提供汇率对冲激励，但压力已超出这些工具的能力。凯投宏观警告可能还需要两次加息。好消息是实体经济保持韧性——第三季度GDP增长5.47%，10月通胀为可控的3.23%、核心通胀2.56%——但印尼强劲的经济基本面与遭受重创的金融市场之间的割裂继续扩大。雅加达综合指数今天再跌1.07%至5,247.88点。

US and Thailand Substantially Conclude Reciprocal Trade Framework as Bangkok Pledges to Eliminate Tariffs on American Goods

美泰基本完成互惠贸易框架谈判，曼谷承诺取消对美商品关税

THAILAND · TRADE / 泰国 · 贸易

English presenter script

Lead presenter: A major trade development from the ASEAN summit in Manila. The United States and Thailand have issued a joint statement declaring they have "substantially concluded negotiations for an Agreement on Reciprocal Trade." If implemented, it would reshape the US$110.8 billion bilateral trade relationship.

Analyst: The framework is extensive. Thailand has pledged to eliminate tariffs on approximately 99 per cent of American goods and remove substantial non-tariff barriers affecting automobiles, medical devices, pharmaceuticals, ethanol, and poultry. Thailand would also accept US federal-standard vehicles and recognise certain FDA certificates and marketing authorisations. On the agricultural side, Bangkok committed to approximately US$2.6 billion in annual purchases of American farm products — specifically feed corn, soybean meal, and dried distillers grains with solubles. The framework also references approximately US$5.4 billion in annual US energy purchases and 80 American aircraft valued at US$18.8 billion, though these are forthcoming commercial arrangements rather than completed deals.

Lead presenter: What does the US give in return, and when does this take effect?

Analyst: This is where we need to be precise. The agreement has not been signed or implemented. Both governments must complete internal procedures before it takes effect, and Prime Minister Anutin acknowledged that further work was needed before formal signing. The 2025 framework had established a 19 per cent US reciprocal tariff rate on Thai imports, though the actual current applicable rates may differ. To put the trade imbalance in perspective: US goods exports to Thailand were US$19.5 billion in 2025, while imports from Thailand were US$91.3 billion — a deficit of US$71.9 billion. For Thailand, this deal represents a strategic calculation: accepting significant concessions on agricultural imports and market access in exchange for securing continued access to the American market, which absorbed over US$91 billion in Thai goods. For Thai farmers, the corn and soybean meal imports will increase competitive pressure, but for Thai manufacturers — particularly in electronics, automotive parts, and food processing — maintaining preferential access to the US market is existential. The deal is not done, but the momentum is real.

中文主播稿

主播：马尼拉东盟峰会传来重大贸易进展。美国和泰国发表联合声明，宣布双方已"基本完成互惠贸易协定谈判"。如果落实，将重塑1,108亿美元的双边贸易关系。

分析师：框架内容广泛。泰国承诺取消对约99%美国商品的关税，并消除影响汽车、医疗器械、药品、乙醇和家禽的重大非关税壁垒。泰国还将接受美国联邦标准车辆，并认可某些FDA证书和上市许可。在农业方面，曼谷承诺每年购买约26亿美元的美国农产品——具体为饲料玉米、豆粕和干酒糟及可溶物。框架还涉及约54亿美元的年度美国能源采购和价值188亿美元的80架美国飞机，但这些是即将达成的商业安排，而非已完成的交易。

主播：美国给予什么回报？何时生效？

分析师：这里我们需要准确表述。协议尚未签署或实施。双方政府必须完成内部程序才能生效，阿努廷总理承认在正式签署前还需进一步工作。2025年的框架曾确立了对泰国进口商品19%的美国互惠关税税率，但实际当前适用税率可能有所不同。从贸易失衡角度看：2025年美国对泰出口195亿美元，从泰进口913亿美元——逆差719亿美元。对泰国而言，这笔交易代表着战略博弈：在农产品进口和市场准入方面做出重大让步，以换取继续进入美国市场的权利——美国市场吸收了超过910亿美元的泰国商品。对泰国农民来说，玉米和豆粕进口将增加竞争压力，但对泰国制造商——特别是电子、汽车零部件和食品加工行业——保持对美市场优惠准入事关生存。交易尚未完成，但势头确实存在。

Vietnam's Trade Turnover Surpasses US$1 Trillion for First Time as Exports and Imports Surge in 2026

越南贸易总额首次突破1万亿美元，2026年进出口强劲增长

ASEAN BUSINESS BRIEF
VIETNAM · TRADE / 越南 · 贸易
SCRIPT READY · VIDEO NOT RENDERED
稿件已就绪 · 视频未生成

English presenter script

Lead presenter: A historic milestone for Vietnam. Total merchandise trade in the first ten months of 2026 surpassed one trillion US dollars for the first time ever — reaching US$1,010.55 billion, up 32.3 per cent year-on-year.

Analyst: This is a remarkable achievement for an economy that had total trade of under US$700 billion as recently as 2022. Exports reached US$499.24 billion, up 27.5 per cent, while imports surged even faster at US$511.31 billion, up 37.4 per cent, leaving a cumulative trade deficit of US$12.07 billion. That import surge is actually a positive signal — it reflects massive inflows of machinery, equipment, and raw materials feeding the country's expanding manufacturing base. Foreign-invested enterprises accounted for 81.2 per cent of exports at US$405.31 billion, up 33 per cent, underscoring how deeply integrated multinational manufacturing is in the Vietnamese economy. Thirty-seven product categories exceeded one billion dollars each, and eight exceeded ten billion.

Lead presenter: The US trade surplus remains a political flashpoint?

Analyst: It does. Vietnam's surplus with the United States reached US$140.10 billion in the first ten months — by far the largest bilateral surplus in Vietnam's trade portfolio. The European Union surplus stood at US$40.84 billion and Japan at US$3.42 billion. On the other side, Vietnam ran a US$134.94 billion deficit with China, US$48.63 billion with South Korea, and US$16.93 billion with ASEAN — reflecting the classic pattern where Vietnam imports components from Northeast Asia and exports finished goods to Western markets. The Section 301 tariff of 12.5 per cent imposed by the United States in July 2026 is the most direct consequence of this imbalance, and it has not slowed trade growth. But US$140 billion in surplus makes Vietnam a prominent target for further trade rebalancing. The trillion-dollar milestone is a testament to Vietnam's extraordinary integration into global supply chains, but the speed of that integration also creates vulnerabilities — particularly the dependence on foreign-invested enterprises for over 80 per cent of exports and the concentration of surplus in a single destination that is actively seeking to narrow it.

中文主播稿

主播：越南迎来历史性里程碑。2026年前十个月商品贸易总额首次突破一万亿美元——达到10,105.5亿美元，同比增长32.3%。

分析师：对于一个2022年贸易总额还不到7,000亿美元的经济体来说，这是非凡的成就。出口达4,992.4亿美元，增长27.5%，而进口增速更快，达5,113.1亿美元，增长37.4%，累计贸易逆差120.7亿美元。进口激增实际上是积极信号——它反映了大量机械、设备和原材料流入，为该国不断扩张的制造业基地提供支持。外资企业占出口的81.2%，达4,053.1亿美元，增长33%，凸显跨国制造业在越南经济中的深度整合。37个产品类别超过10亿美元，8个超过100亿美元。

主播：对美贸易顺差仍是政治焦点？

分析师：确实如此。越南前十个月对美顺差达1,401亿美元——是越南贸易组合中最大的双边顺差。对欧盟顺差408.4亿美元，对日本34.2亿美元。另一方面，越南对中国逆差1,349.4亿美元，对韩国486.3亿美元，对东盟169.3亿美元——反映了典型模式：越南从东北亚进口零部件，向西方市场出口成品。美国2026年7月实施的12.5%的301条款关税是这一失衡最直接的后果，但它并未减缓贸易增长。然而，1,400亿美元的顺差使越南成为进一步贸易再平衡的显著目标。万亿美元里程碑证明了越南融入全球供应链的非凡程度，但这种整合的速度也带来脆弱性——特别是出口超过80%依赖外资企业，以及顺差集中在一个正积极寻求缩小顺差的目的地。

Singapore Assumes ASEAN Chairmanship for 2027 with 'Deeper Integration, Wider Partnerships' Agenda

新加坡以"深化整合、拓宽伙伴关系"议程接任2027年东盟主席国

ASEAN BUSINESS BRIEF
SINGAPORE · DIPLOMACY / 新加坡 · 外交
SCRIPT READY · VIDEO NOT RENDERED

English presenter script

Lead presenter: Following yesterday's handover ceremony in Manila, Singapore has officially assumed the ASEAN chairmanship for 2027 under the theme "Deeper Integration, Wider Partnerships." Prime Minister Lawrence Wong received the gavel from President Marcos Jr. and outlined an ambitious agenda.

Analyst: Wong identified three core priorities. First, deepening regional integration — he said the bloc must take further steps so businesses can set up and operate more smoothly across borders, pushing toward what he described as a single ASEAN market. Second, expanding international partnerships, which is especially timely given 2027 marks the 50th anniversary of ASEAN's relations with the United States, Canada, and the European Union, the 30th anniversary of ASEAN Plus Three, and ASEAN's own 60th anniversary. Third, harnessing emerging opportunities in artificial intelligence and clean energy — two areas where Singapore has particular credibility and where regional coordination could create genuine competitive advantages.

Lead presenter: How does this connect to the DEFA signed under the Philippines?

Analyst: The ASEAN Digital Economy Framework Agreement is now Singapore's to implement. As the country that co-championed DEFA and arguably has the most advanced digital economy infrastructure in the region, Singapore is well positioned to drive the implementation framework. Wong was candid about the external pressures, saying ASEAN "will continue to face external pressures that will test our cohesion and unity" — a clear reference to major-power rivalry, the fragmented global economic order, and conflicts beyond the region. Marcos, in handing over the gavel, said it was "proper that Singapore should now take its helm" as ASEAN enters its 60th year. Under the Philippines' chairmanship, concrete achievements included DEFA, the early entry into force of the upgraded ASEAN Trade in Goods Agreement, the ASEAN-China Free Trade Agreement 3.0, and progress on Timor-Leste's integration — expected to assume the chairmanship by 2029. Singapore's diplomatic weight, economic strength, and institutional credibility give it perhaps the strongest platform of any recent chair. The question is whether that platform can translate into meaningful progress on the hard issues — South China Sea tensions, Myanmar, and navigating the US-China divide — where ASEAN's consensus model has historically struggled.

中文主播稿

主播：在昨天马尼拉的交接仪式之后，新加坡已正式接任2027年东盟主席国，主题为"深化整合、拓宽伙伴关系"。黄循财总理从马科斯总统手中接过主席槌，并阐述了雄心勃勃的议程。

分析师：黄循财确定了三个核心优先事项。第一，深化区域整合——他表示，东盟必须采取进一步措施，使企业能够更顺畅地跨境设立和运营，推动他所描述的东盟单一市场。第二，拓展国际伙伴关系，这时机特别恰当，因为2027年恰逢东盟与美国、加拿大和欧盟建交50周年，东盟10+3的30周年，以及东盟自身成立60周年。第三，抓住人工智能和清洁能源领域的新兴机遇——新加坡在这两个领域具有特别的信誉，区域协调可以创造真正的竞争优势。

主播：这与菲律宾任期内签署的DEFA有何关联？

分析师：《东盟数字经济框架协议》现在由新加坡来推动落实。作为DEFA的共同倡导者且拥有区域内最先进数字经济基础设施的国家，新加坡处于推动实施框架的有利位置。黄循财坦言外部压力，称东盟"将继续面临考验凝聚力和团结的外部压力"——明确指向大国竞争、碎片化的全球经济秩序以及区域外的冲突。马科斯在交接主席槌时表示，东盟进入第60年，"由新加坡来掌舵是恰当的"。在菲律宾主席国任期内，具体成就包括DEFA、升级版《东盟货物贸易协定》的提前生效、《东盟-中国自贸协定3.0》以及东帝汶一体化的推进——预计2029年接任主席国。新加坡的外交分量、经济实力和制度公信力赋予它或许是近年来任何主席国最强的平台。问题是这个平台能否在棘手议题上转化为实质性进展——南海紧张局势、缅甸问题以及应对中美分歧——而东盟的共识模式在这些问题上历来举步维艰。

English presenter script

Lead presenter: And we close tonight with the Philippines, where the peso has breached 64 per dollar — a record low — as the stagflation story we have been tracking continues to worsen. The currency's collapse mirrors the broader deterioration in economic indicators.

Analyst: The peso's slide past 64 per dollar is both a symptom and an accelerant of the Philippines' economic distress. It is a symptom because it reflects capital outflows driven by the same governance and growth concerns we have been documenting. It is an accelerant because a weaker peso feeds directly into inflation — particularly through imported fuel and food costs — which is already running at 7.8 per cent, nearly four times the central bank's target midpoint. The Philippine Stock Exchange Index has fallen to its lowest level since May 2020, the depths of the pandemic. BPI, one of the country's largest banks, has publicly urged the BSP to continue tightening, with the benchmark rate already at 5.5 per cent after the 50-basis-point hike in October.

Lead presenter: Is there any relief in sight?

Analyst: Not in the near term. Barclays has warned that inflation could reach 8 per cent in November, and projects another jumbo rate hike at the BSP's 17 December meeting. Citigroup expects two more quarter-point hikes to bring the rate to 6.0 per cent, with no easing until 2028. The fundamental problem remains the same: GDP growth has slowed to 2.1 per cent in Q3 — a 16-year low excluding the pandemic — while unemployment has risen to 5.4 per cent with 2.86 million Filipinos out of work. Infrastructure spending plunged 34.6 per cent from January to August, removing the one fiscal lever that could simultaneously address growth and productive capacity. The Philippines is now in the worst possible macroeconomic bind — tightening monetary policy into a slowing economy because inflation gives no room for relief. Among ASEAN economies we cover, this divergence is stark: today we reported Malaysia growing at 5.5 per cent and Vietnam crossing one trillion dollars in trade. The Philippines' challenge is not just economic — it is a question of when the institutional response catches up with the scale of the problem.
