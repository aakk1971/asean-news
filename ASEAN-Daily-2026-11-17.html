Singapore's Non-Oil Exports Surge 79.8% in October as AI Boom Propels Trade Surplus to Record S$19.3 Billion

17 November 2026. Enterprise Singapore / Investing.com / Xinhua.

Verified reporting

Singapore's non-oil domestic exports (NODX) surged 79.8 per cent year-on-year in October 2026, accelerating from 68.3 per cent in September and smashing the Reuters median forecast of 37.7 per cent, according to data released by Enterprise Singapore on 17 November. It marked the seventh consecutive month of NODX growth above 20 per cent and the strongest year-on-year increase since 1988.

Electronics NODX grew 73.9 per cent, with disk media products jumping 189.6 per cent to approximately S$1.1 billion, personal computers rising 81 per cent to about S$700 million, and integrated circuits climbing 53.1 per cent. Non-electronics NODX surged 81.9 per cent, driven by a 253.1 per cent increase in pharmaceuticals and ships and boats, partly reflecting a low base from the prior year. Shipments to the United States soared 214.6 per cent, with China and Taiwan also expanding.

The trade surplus ballooned to S$19.3 billion (approximately US$15 billion), more than double the S$9.03 billion recorded in September and far exceeding the anticipated S$7.9 billion. Total merchandise trade grew 50.7 per cent, with exports up 55.8 per cent and imports rising 44.9 per cent. Non-oil re-exports expanded 48.1 per cent. For the first ten months of 2026, NODX grew 33.6 per cent, up from an average 27.6 per cent through the first nine months.

Our analysis

Singapore's October trade data is extraordinary by any historical measure. The 79.8 per cent NODX surge — the strongest since 1988 — and a trade surplus that more than doubled to S$19.3 billion demonstrate that Singapore sits at the absolute epicentre of the global AI infrastructure buildout. The electronics component tells the story: disk media products nearly tripled, personal computers rose 81 per cent, and integrated circuits climbed 53 per cent, all reflecting insatiable demand for AI training and inference hardware. The 214.6 per cent surge in shipments to the United States underscores American tech companies' voracious appetite for AI components routed through Singapore's semiconductor ecosystem. With NODX growing 33.6 per cent year-to-date versus the government's raised full-year forecast of 14 to 16 per cent, Singapore will shatter its own projections. The pharmaceutical surge adds diversification, though the AI-driven electronics boom is the dominant force. The risk flagged by MAS last month — that AI-driven growth will ease in late 2027 — now carries even more weight, because the higher the peak, the more noticeable the eventual normalisation will be.

What we don't know yet

The full breakdown of October trade by destination and product category beyond the top-line figures. Whether the pharmaceutical and ships surge is a one-off base effect or a sustainable trend. How much of the electronics NODX represents genuine final demand versus inventory building ahead of potential tariff changes.

新加坡10月非石油出口飙升79.8%，AI热潮推动贸易顺差创纪录达193亿新元

2026年11月17日。新加坡企业发展局 / Investing.com / 新华社。

经核实的报道

根据新加坡企业发展局11月17日发布的数据，新加坡10月非石油国内出口（NODX）同比飙升79.8%，高于9月的68.3%，远超路透社37.7%的中值预测。这是NODX连续第七个月增长超过20%，也是自1988年以来最强劲的同比增幅。

电子产品NODX增长73.9%，其中磁盘媒体产品飙升189.6%至约11亿新元，个人电脑增长81%至约7亿新元，集成电路攀升53.1%。非电子产品NODX激增81.9%，主要受药品及船舶253.1%的增长推动，部分反映了去年同期的低基数效应。对美国出口飙升214.6%，对中国和台湾的出口也有所扩大。

贸易顺差膨胀至193亿新元（约150亿美元），是9月90.3亿新元的两倍多，远超预期的79亿新元。商品贸易总额增长50.7%，出口增长55.8%，进口增长44.9%。非石油转口贸易增长48.1%。2026年前十个月，NODX增长33.6%，高于前九个月平均27.6%的增速。

原创分析

新加坡10月贸易数据以任何历史标准衡量都是非凡的。NODX飙升79.8%——1988年以来最强——以及贸易顺差翻倍至193亿新元，表明新加坡处于全球AI基础设施建设的绝对中心。电子产品数据讲述了这个故事：磁盘媒体产品几乎增长两倍，个人电脑增长81%，集成电路攀升53%，全部反映了对AI训练和推理硬件的旺盛需求。对美国出口飙升214.6%，凸显了美国科技公司通过新加坡半导体生态系统对AI组件的巨大胃口。NODX年初至今增长33.6%，而政府上调后的全年预测为14%至16%，新加坡将远超自己的预测。药品激增增加了多元化，但AI驱动的电子产品繁荣是主导力量。MAS上月警告的风险——AI驱动增长将在2027年下半年放缓——现在更具分量，因为峰值越高，最终的正常化就越明显。

未知事项

10月贸易按目的地和产品类别的完整细分尚未公布。药品和船舶的激增是一次性基数效应还是可持续趋势尚不清楚。电子产品NODX中有多少代表真正的最终需求而非潜在关税变化前的库存囤积。

Malaysia's Investment Approvals Jump 20.7% to RM350.4 Billion in Nine Months as US Becomes Top Foreign Investor

17 November 2026. MIDA / The Star / New Straits Times.

Verified reporting

Malaysia's approved investments surged 20.7 per cent to RM350.4 billion (approximately US$78 billion) in the first nine months of 2026, up from RM290.3 billion in the same period of 2025, according to data released by the Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA) on 17 November. The total equals 81.3 per cent of the revised RM431.1 billion approved for all of 2025. A total of 4,387 projects were approved, expected to create 152,090 jobs once fully implemented.

Foreign investment accounted for RM212.3 billion or 60.6 per cent of the total, jumping 40.7 per cent year-on-year. The United States displaced China as the top foreign investor with RM60.0 billion — a fivefold increase from RM11.3 billion in the same period of 2025 — followed by China at RM38.6 billion, Singapore at RM38.5 billion, Japan at RM24.4 billion, and Australia at RM12.6 billion. These five countries accounted for 82 per cent of all foreign investment.

By sector, services led with RM219.1 billion (62.5 per cent), followed by manufacturing at RM106.3 billion and the primary sector at RM25.0 billion — the latter surging 420 per cent. Data centre and cloud computing investments reached RM123.5 billion, up 44.5 per cent and accounting for 35.2 per cent of all approved investments. Johor attracted the most investment at RM99.7 billion, followed by Selangor at RM87.9 billion, Penang at RM41.1 billion, and Kuala Lumpur at RM37.3 billion.

Our analysis

The fivefold increase in US investment to RM60 billion — making America Malaysia's largest foreign investor for the first time — is a structural shift that reflects the broader reorganisation of global technology supply chains. American tech companies are building AI infrastructure, semiconductor packaging facilities, and data centres across Malaysia at a pace that has transformed the investment landscape in under two years. The US overtaking China is particularly significant given that Chinese investment also grew, reaching RM38.6 billion — this is not a substitution story but an additive one, with Malaysia attracting more capital from both superpowers simultaneously. The data centre and cloud segment accounting for 35.2 per cent of all approved investments — RM123.5 billion, up 44.5 per cent — confirms that the AI infrastructure boom is the single most powerful force driving Malaysian investment. Johor's RM99.7 billion haul, boosted by its proximity to Singapore and its data centre corridor, makes it the top investment destination. The pipeline remains strong: MIDA reported RM41.2 billion in projects under review and RM60.5 billion in high-potential investments under discussion. The challenge is execution — of manufacturing projects approved since 2021, 87.4 per cent have reached production or construction, a creditable conversion rate.

What we don't know yet

The specific breakdown of US investment by sub-sector — how much is data centres versus semiconductor packaging versus other manufacturing. Whether the primary sector's 420 per cent surge is driven by a few mega-projects or broad-based growth. The realisation rate of data centre investments given growing concerns about energy and water supply capacity in key corridors.

马来西亚九个月投资审批飙升20.7%至3504亿令吉，美国成为最大外资来源国

2026年11月17日。马来西亚投资发展局 / 星报 / 新海峡时报。

经核实的报道

根据马来西亚投资发展局（MIDA）11月17日发布的数据，马来西亚2026年前九个月审批投资额飙升20.7%至3,504亿令吉（约780亿美元），高于2025年同期的2,903亿令吉。该总额相当于2025年全年修订后4,311亿令吉审批额的81.3%。共审批4,387个项目，预计全面实施后将创造152,090个就业机会。

外国投资占2,123亿令吉或总额的60.6%，同比增长40.7%。美国以600亿令吉取代中国成为最大外资来源国——较2025年同期的113亿令吉增长五倍——其后依次为中国386亿令吉、新加坡385亿令吉、日本244亿令吉和澳大利亚126亿令吉。这五个国家占所有外国投资的82%。

按行业划分，服务业以2,191亿令吉（62.5%）领先，其次是制造业1,063亿令吉和初级产业250亿令吉——后者飙升420%。数据中心和云计算投资达1,235亿令吉，增长44.5%，占所有审批投资的35.2%。柔佛州吸引最多投资997亿令吉，其次是雪兰莪879亿令吉、槟城411亿令吉和吉隆坡373亿令吉。

原创分析

美国投资五倍增长至600亿令吉——使美国首次成为马来西亚最大外资来源国——是一个结构性转变，反映了全球科技供应链更广泛的重组。美国科技公司正在马来西亚以前所未有的速度建设AI基础设施、半导体封装设施和数据中心，在不到两年的时间里改变了投资格局。美国超越中国尤为重要，因为中国投资也在增长，达到386亿令吉——这不是替代的故事，而是叠加的故事，马来西亚同时从两个超级大国吸引了更多资本。数据中心和云计算占所有审批投资的35.2%——1,235亿令吉，增长44.5%——证实AI基础设施热潮是推动马来西亚投资的最强大力量。柔佛州997亿令吉的投资额得益于毗邻新加坡及其数据中心走廊，使其成为首选投资目的地。投资管道仍然强劲：MIDA报告有412亿令吉项目正在审核中，605亿令吉高潜力投资正在洽谈。挑战在于执行——自2021年以来获批的制造业项目中，87.4%已达到生产或建设阶段，转化率值得肯定。

未知事项

美国投资按子行业的具体细分——数据中心、半导体封装与其他制造业各占多少。初级产业420%的飙升是由少数大型项目还是广泛增长推动的。鉴于对关键走廊能源和水资源供应能力日益增长的担忧，数据中心投资的实际到位率如何。

Thailand's Q3 GDP Reaches 2.9% as NESDC Raises Full-Year Forecast to 2.5%, Exports Surge 23.6% on AI Demand

16–17 November 2026. NESDC / Nation Thailand / The Star.

Verified reporting

Thailand's economy grew 2.9 per cent year-on-year in the third quarter of 2026, up from a revised 2.4 per cent in Q2, according to official data released by the National Economic and Social Development Council (NESDC) on 16 November. Quarter-on-quarter seasonally adjusted growth was 0.3 per cent. Growth for the first nine months averaged 2.8 per cent. The result slightly missed the 3.0 per cent Reuters poll median but confirmed the Bank of Thailand's earlier signal that revised manufacturing data would lift the headline figure.

Merchandise exports surged 23.6 per cent — the strongest growth in 21 quarters — worth US$106.4 billion, led by computer components, communications equipment, and integrated circuits linked to AI and data centre demand. Total investment grew 11.8 per cent, its fastest pace in 55 quarters, with private investment rising 16.2 per cent driven by a 20.5 per cent increase in machinery and equipment spending. Manufacturing output grew 3.7 per cent, the fastest in 16 quarters. Private consumption rose 2.5 per cent.

The NESDC raised its full-year 2026 GDP forecast to 2.5 per cent, the top of its previous 2.0–2.5 per cent range, and projected 2027 growth at 2.0 to 3.0 per cent. Export growth was revised sharply higher to 20.4 per cent for 2026 from 15.1 per cent. Foreign tourist arrivals fell 5.0 per cent to 7.06 million in Q3 but tourism receipts rose 11.7 per cent. The central bank held its key rate at 1.0 per cent, with the next review on 23 December. Public debt stood at 13.4 trillion baht, or 68.6 per cent of GDP.

Our analysis

The official Q3 data confirms the Bank of Thailand's earlier hint that revised manufacturing statistics would lift GDP growth to 2.9 per cent. What is more significant than the headline is the composition: investment growing at its fastest pace in 55 quarters (nearly 14 years) and exports at their strongest in 21 quarters signals a genuine, albeit concentrated, acceleration. Private investment surging 16.2 per cent with machinery spending up 20.5 per cent indicates companies are building capacity — not merely selling into one-off demand — which bodes well for sustained output. The AI and data centre connection is now explicit in Thai trade data, with computer components, communications equipment, and integrated circuits driving the export boom. However, NESDC chief Danucha Pichayanan acknowledged that property, services, and tourism sectors weighed on growth, and Q4 is expected to slow on a high base effect and weaker private investment. Thailand's K-shaped recovery persists: unemployment is a healthy 1.02 per cent and inflation is manageable at 2.4 per cent headline, but high household debt — the governor's repeated concern — continues to constrain domestic consumption and leaves Thailand trailing ASEAN peers that have fully recovered from the pandemic.

What we don't know yet

Whether the Q4 slowdown will be modest or sharp given the high base from Q4 2025. How much of the 16.2 per cent private investment surge is concentrated in a few AI-linked sectors versus broadly distributed. Whether the tourism shortfall — arrivals at 31 million projected for 2026 versus nearly 40 million in 2019 — reflects structural changes in travel patterns or temporary factors that will reverse.

泰国第三季度GDP达2.9%，NESDC上调全年预测至2.5%，出口因AI需求飙升23.6%

2026年11月16–17日。国家经济和社会发展委员会 / 国家报 / 星报。

经核实的报道

根据国家经济和社会发展委员会（NESDC）11月16日发布的官方数据，泰国第三季度经济同比增长2.9%，高于修订后第二季度的2.4%。经季节性调整后环比增长0.3%。前九个月平均增长2.8%。结果略低于路透社民调中值3.0%，但证实了泰国央行此前关于修订后制造业数据将提升整体数字的信号。

商品出口飙升23.6%——21个季度以来最强增长——价值1,063.8亿美元，由与AI和数据中心需求相关的电脑零部件、通讯设备和集成电路引领。总投资增长11.8%，为55个季度以来最快速度，其中私人投资增长16.2%，受机械设备支出增长20.5%推动。制造业产出增长3.7%，为16个季度以来最快。私人消费增长2.5%。

NESDC将2026年全年GDP预测上调至2.5%，为此前2.0–2.5%区间的上限，并预测2027年增长2.0%至3.0%。出口增长预测从15.1%大幅上调至20.4%。第三季度外国游客人数下降5.0%至706万人，但旅游收入增长11.7%。央行维持基准利率1.0%不变，下次审议定于12月23日。公共债务为13.4万亿泰铢，占GDP的68.6%。

原创分析

官方第三季度数据证实了泰国央行此前暗示修订后的制造业统计数据将把GDP增长推高至2.9%。比标题数字更重要的是构成：投资以55个季度（近14年）以来最快速度增长，出口创21个季度最强增长，标志着真正的、尽管集中的加速。私人投资激增16.2%且机械支出增长20.5%表明企业正在建设产能——而非仅仅满足一次性需求——这对持续产出是好兆头。AI和数据中心的关联现已明确体现在泰国贸易数据中，电脑零部件、通讯设备和集成电路驱动出口繁荣。然而，NESDC主任达努差承认房地产、服务和旅游部门拖累了增长，预计第四季度将因高基数效应和私人投资走弱而放缓。泰国的K型复苏持续存在：失业率为健康的1.02%，通胀在2.4%的整体水平上可控，但高家庭债务——央行行长反复强调的担忧——继续制约国内消费，使泰国落后于已完全从疫情中恢复的东盟同行。

未知事项

鉴于2025年第四季度的高基数，第四季度放缓将是温和的还是急剧的。16.2%的私人投资激增是集中在少数AI相关行业还是广泛分布。旅游缺口——2026年预计到访3,100万人次而2019年近4,000万——反映的是旅行模式的结构性变化还是将逆转的临时因素。

PHILIPPINES · ECONOMY / 菲律宾 · 经济

Citigroup Declares Philippines in Stagflation as Growth Slows to 2.1% and Inflation Hits 7.8%

9–17 November 2026. Citigroup / Manila Bulletin / Tribune / PhilStar.

Verified reporting

Citigroup's Asia economics chief Johanna Chua said the Philippines has entered stagflation — or what she termed "slowflation" — characterised by the combination of GDP growth of just 2.1 per cent in Q3 2026 and headline inflation of 7.8 per cent in October, far above the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) target of 2 to 4 per cent. Citi had forecast 3.8 per cent growth, calling the actual figure a "substantial miss."

It was the fifth consecutive quarter of slowing growth, with household consumption decelerating to 2.4 per cent from 2.9 per cent. Public construction fell 15.1 per cent, durable equipment investment dropped 7.8 per cent, and infrastructure and capital outlays plunged 34.6 per cent from January to August. The unemployment rate rose to 5.4 per cent in September, up from 3.8 per cent a year earlier, with 2.86 million Filipinos out of work.

Citi expects two more quarter-point BSP rate hikes to bring the benchmark to 6.0 per cent, with easing not resuming until 2028. Barclays warned inflation could reach 8 per cent in November and also projected a jumbo rate hike. S&P Global separately warned of stagflation risks, reinforcing the Citi assessment. Economic Planning Secretary Arsenio Balisacan disagreed with the stagflation label, saying it was "too early to judge" and that he would wait until year-end data. Citi CEO and Philippines banking head Paul Favila said geopolitical tensions and oil prices were limiting the government's room to respond.

Our analysis

When Citigroup — one of the Philippines' largest foreign banking operations — publicly labels the economy as being in stagflation, it represents a significant shift in the institutional narrative around Filipino economic performance. The data supports the assessment: five consecutive quarters of deceleration, GDP growth at a 16-year low excluding the pandemic, inflation at nearly four times the target midpoint, and rising unemployment that has taken the jobless rate from 3.8 to 5.4 per cent in a single year. The 34.6 per cent collapse in infrastructure spending from January to August is particularly damaging because it removes the one lever that could simultaneously boost growth and productive capacity. Secretary Balisacan's reluctance to accept the stagflation label is understandable politically but increasingly difficult to defend statistically. The outlook is grim: Citi projects two more rate hikes to 6.0 per cent with no easing until 2028, which would extend the Philippines' monetary tightening cycle well beyond regional peers. The Philippines is now an outlier in ASEAN — the only major economy simultaneously raising rates, experiencing rising unemployment, and decelerating toward 2 per cent growth while its neighbours ride an AI-driven export boom that the Philippines' domestic-demand-oriented economy cannot easily capture.

What we don't know yet

Whether inflation will reach 8 per cent in November as Barclays warns. Whether the BSP will deliver a standard 25-basis-point hike or a larger move at its 17 December meeting. When public infrastructure spending will resume as the flood-control investigations continue. Whether the government will formally revise its growth target below the 6–8 per cent range that is now clearly unreachable.

花旗集团宣布菲律宾进入滞胀，增长降至2.1%，通胀达7.8%

2026年11月9–17日。花旗集团 / 马尼拉公报 / 论坛报 / 菲律宾星报。

经核实的报道

花旗集团亚洲经济首席分析师蔡芳华表示，菲律宾已进入滞胀——或她所称的"慢胀"——其特征是第三季度GDP增长仅2.1%与10月通胀率7.8%的结合，远高于菲律宾央行（BSP）2%至4%的目标区间。花旗此前预测增长3.8%，称实际数据为"重大偏差"。

原创分析

当花旗集团——菲律宾最大的外资银行业务之一——公开将经济定性为滞胀时，这代表着围绕菲律宾经济表现的机构叙事发生了重大转变。数据支持这一评估：连续五个季度减速，GDP增长创16年新低（不含疫情），通胀几乎是目标中值的四倍，失业率在一年内从3.8%升至5.4%。1月至8月基础设施支出暴跌34.6%尤为致命，因为这消除了能够同时推动增长和产能的唯一杠杆。巴利萨坎部长不愿接受滞胀标签在政治上可以理解，但在统计上越来越难以辩护。前景黯淡：花旗预测再加息两次至6.0%且到2028年才会宽松，这将使菲律宾的货币紧缩周期远远超过区域同行。菲律宾目前在东盟中是异类——唯一一个同时加息、失业率上升、增长向2%减速的主要经济体，而其邻国正乘着菲律宾以内需为导向的经济难以参与的AI驱动出口热潮。

Vietnam's Foreign Investment Hits 17-Year High of US$54.85 Billion as Registered Capital Surges 74%

November 2026. VietnamNet / Foreign Investment Agency / Vietnam Briefing.

Verified reporting

Vietnam attracted US$54.85 billion in registered foreign direct investment in the first ten months of 2026, surging 74 per cent year-on-year — the highest level recorded since 2009, according to data from the Foreign Investment Agency under the Ministry of Finance. Disbursed FDI reached an estimated US$24.08 billion, up 13.1 per cent and the highest for the same ten-month period in five years.

New project registrations numbered 3,478 with US$30.73 billion in capital — project count up 4.7 per cent and capital more than doubling. Additional capital for existing projects totalled US$16.13 billion across 1,079 projects, up 33.2 per cent. Manufacturing and processing led newly registered capital at US$14.61 billion (47.5 per cent), followed by transportation and warehousing at US$5.15 billion (16.7 per cent).

Singapore was the top investing country with US$9.52 billion (31 per cent), followed by South Korea at US$5.81 billion, Luxembourg at US$4.99 billion, Hong Kong at US$3.57 billion, mainland China at US$2.77 billion, and Japan at US$1.58 billion. Ho Chi Minh City attracted over US$10.3 billion with 1,761 new projects, while Thai Nguyen province ranked second with over US$5.8 billion. Disbursed FDI was dominated by manufacturing and processing at US$19.96 billion (82.9 per cent), followed by real estate at US$1.76 billion.

Our analysis

Vietnam's registered FDI surging 74 per cent to US$54.85 billion — a 17-year high — is a resounding vote of confidence from global manufacturers despite the headwinds from Section 301 tariffs imposed by the United States in July. The more than doubling of new project capital to US$30.73 billion is particularly significant because it represents forward-looking commitments that will shape Vietnam's industrial landscape for years. The source-country breakdown reveals an interesting diversification: Singapore leads at 31 per cent, South Korea and Luxembourg together account for 35 per cent, and China contributes 9 per cent. Luxembourg's US$4.99 billion — third overall — likely reflects European holding company structures routing investment into Vietnamese operations, suggesting the actual beneficial source is broader than the nominal figures indicate. Ho Chi Minh City's US$10.3 billion dominance shows the southern economic hub remains the primary magnet, but Thai Nguyen's strong second-place finish reflects the northern electronics manufacturing cluster anchored by Samsung's massive operations. The gap between registered (US$54.85 billion) and disbursed (US$24.08 billion) FDI underscores the persistent challenge of converting commitments to operating factories — a process that takes two to four years and can be disrupted by policy shifts, infrastructure bottlenecks, and global trade tensions.

What we don't know yet

The specific mega-projects driving the registration surge — the data does not name individual investments. Whether Section 301 tariffs have shifted any planned US-bound export capacity away from Vietnam or merely raised costs. The breakdown of FDI by technology level and whether Vietnam is succeeding in attracting the higher-value manufacturing it has explicitly targeted in its latest resolution on FDI quality.

MSCI Removes Eight Indonesian Stocks in November Rebalancing, Constituents Shrink to 35

12–17 November 2026. MSCI / Indonesia Business Post / Bisnis Indonesia.

Verified reporting

MSCI removed eight Indonesian stocks from its Global Standard Indexes in its November 2026 semi-annual rebalancing, announced on 12 November, with changes taking effect after the close on 30 November. Among the stocks removed were PT Barito Pacific Tbk (BRPT) and PT Bumi Resources Minerals Tbk (BRMS), with no Indonesian stocks added. The number of Indonesian constituents in the MSCI Global Standard Indexes shrank to 35, down from 43 prior to the rebalancing. The price cutoff date was 20 October 2026.

The removal follows MSCI's October launch of a public consultation on reclassifying Indonesia from Emerging Market to Standalone Market status — a move that triggered an 11 per cent single-day plunge in the Jakarta Composite Index (IHSG) on 16 October and has contributed to a cumulative decline of approximately 45 per cent from the January 2026 peak. Passive funds tracking MSCI indexes are expected to adjust holdings ahead of the 30 November effective date, creating additional selling pressure on the removed stocks. The reclassification consultation runs until 15 January 2027, with a decision expected in February 2027.

The rebalancing was part of a broader Asia-Pacific adjustment: China had 38 deletions, Japan 5, Taiwan 4, Australia 3, the Philippines 2, and Singapore and Hong Kong one each. The Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) said it was maintaining dialogue with MSCI and international investors throughout the review period.

Our analysis

The shrinkage of Indonesia's MSCI Global Standard Index constituents from 43 to 35 — a loss of nearly 19 per cent of its representation in a single rebalancing — is the most tangible financial market consequence yet of the governance concerns that prompted MSCI's reclassification consultation. Each removal forces passive funds tracking MSCI indexes to sell those stocks, creating mechanical selling pressure that compounds the already-severe foreign capital outflows. The lack of any Indonesian additions is particularly telling: in a normal market environment, a growing economy with 5.47 per cent GDP growth would typically see index additions, not exclusively deletions. This confirms that MSCI is effectively treating Indonesia as a market in regulatory limbo. The timeline now matters enormously: the consultation closes on 15 January 2027, with a decision in February. That gives Indonesia approximately two months to demonstrate sufficient governance reforms to persuade MSCI to maintain its Emerging Market status. The irony is that Indonesia's real economy has never been stronger — 5.47 per cent Q3 growth, robust manufacturing, solid fiscal position — but the equity market is pricing in a worst-case scenario because capital market governance has not kept pace with economic growth.

What we don't know yet

The complete list of eight removed Indonesian stocks beyond BRPT and BRMS. The estimated passive fund outflows that will result from the index changes by the 30 November effective date. Whether Indonesia's regulatory authorities will announce specific governance reforms before the 15 January consultation deadline. How the IHSG will react to the additional selling pressure from the rebalancing on top of the existing reclassification overhang.

49th ASEAN Summit Concludes in Manila, Philippines Hands Chairmanship to Singapore for 2027

17 November 2026. PNA / Manila Bulletin / Rappler / BERNAMA.

Verified reporting

The 49th ASEAN Summit and Related Summits concluded in Manila on 17 November, marking the end of the Philippines' 2026 chairmanship under the theme "Navigating Our Future, Together." President Ferdinand Marcos Jr., who chaired the summit, formally handed the ASEAN chairmanship to Singapore, which will lead the bloc in 2027. Leaders of the ten ASEAN member states and dialogue partners including the United States, China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, India, and New Zealand participated.

Leaders reviewed progress on the ASEAN Community Vision 2045. Marcos said in his closing remarks that changing course "is not the same as losing direction," emphasising that member states must be able to rely on ASEAN and ASEAN on them. Our analysis

The Philippines' handover to Singapore represents more than a routine rotation — it marks a transition from a chair country experiencing severe economic distress to one at the peak of its economic strength.
